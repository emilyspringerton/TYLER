**PRODUCTION NOTE, READ BEFORE THE SCRIPT:**

Friday is Venus's day (Beleth #13, 2.22 Hz, *catalytic love*; `README.md` §VI: "Every love triangle
scene should be the most emotionally uncomfortable scene in the chapter," Rule 8). The triangle
here is not the Tides one. It is **Tyler, Hana, and the older Hana**, two of whom have the same
hands and the same reflexes and one of whom has a stopped watch. It is the most uncomfortable scene
in the block and it is made of soup.

**Form.** Cam locks off the rig in a wide, with a **green** strip on the lens hood, and the others
go to the far end of the room and busy themselves at nothing. The Doc UI, left alone with three people at
a table, becomes a clinical observer of discomfort: it counts. This is the episode's only comic
device and it is also its structure, and both are doing the same work.

**The Producer** offers to lower Beleth. (He has been seated since VH26; he is a guest.) He asks. He
tries. Nothing changes. It is Friday; the day does the work. Why he offered is VH37's business.

**Guard-rails.** The older Hana sits in the eighth chair on the tape line (VH29), one foot in the
room. Rule 21: nothing about the transit stop or the root-node bed appears. The "tell" in `README.md`
§V (Tyler dries his hands "too carefully") is the episode's one hard fact.

**Not resolved:** what each Hana wants to say; the cuff; the tenth ingredient.

*The older Hana reaches across the corner of the table. She does not look at what her hand is doing.
It finds the collar of Tyler's jacket, where it has turned under at the back, and she turns it right,
once, with the flat of two fingers, and withdraws.*

*She has done it without thinking, the way you put a hand on a dog.*

**OLDER HANA**
*(French; SUBTITLED, a half-second late: "Sorry. Reflex.")*
