## COLD OPEN

```
INT. SERVER ROOM — ICELAND — NO YEAR GIVEN
```

*The PA sits in the third chair of the arc, the one that is his, with his clipboard on his knee, two thermoses in front of
him on the floor and the third visible past the tape at the threshold. He has a pen. He has been
told by no one to do this and he is doing it.*

**LOWER THIRD (DOC UI):**
```
TC  — / —  ·  11:14:40
```

**PA**
*(French; SUBTITLED: "Inventory. For the record.")*
Inventaire. Pour le dossier.

*Cam turns the lens on him. He does not look up.*

**PA (CONT'D)**
*(French; SUBTITLED: "Thermos one. Minestrone. Warm. Has been warm since the first day. His recipe,
nine of ten. He told us. Somebody added the tenth.")*
Thermos un. Minestrone. Chaud. Chaud depuis le premier jour. Sa recette, neuf sur dix. Il nous l'a
dit. Quelqu'un a ajouté le dixième.

*He writes. He reaches the second one.*

**PA (CONT'D)**
*(French; SUBTITLED: "Thermos two. Coffee. Black. His. He said so out loud, which makes it the only
thing in this room with a name on it.")*
Thermos deux. Café. Noir. À lui. Il l'a dit à voix haute, ce qui en fait la seule chose ici qui a
un nom dessus.

*The sound guy, from behind the boom, laughs once — a short breath. It is the first laugh since
the inspector's, and a smaller one. The PA does not look up, but his shoulders drop an inch.*

**PA (CONT'D)**
*(French; SUBTITLED: "Thermos three. Tea. I can't see it from here.")*
Thermos trois. Thé. Je ne le vois pas d'ici.

*He looks up at last, at the doorway.*

**PA (CONT'D)**
*(French; SUBTITLED: "I would like to see it.")*
J'aimerais le voir.

**CAM**
*(French; SUBTITLED: "Behind the tape.")*
Derrière le ruban.

**PA**
*(French; SUBTITLED: "I know. I wrote the tape.")*
Je sais. C'est moi qui ai écrit le ruban.

---

*He stands. He goes to the tape. He does not cross it. He crouches at the very edge, puts his chin
on his fist, and looks at the third thermos from eleven inches away like a man looking at a coin
in a fountain he is not allowed to wade into.*

**PA (CONT'D)**
*(French; SUBTITLED, to the thermos: "You're the one that's new.")*
C'est toi, le nouveau.

*Nothing. He nods anyway.*

**PA (CONT'D)**
*(French; SUBTITLED: "Welcome to the unit.")*
Bienvenue dans l'équipe.

*Nobody laughs this time. The sound guy lowers the boom a few inches, as one lowers a flag.*

---

*Cam reaches into her bag and takes out her own thermos — she has had one the whole time, steel,
scratched, hers. She sets it on the floor beside the other two. Four.*

*The sound guy, after a moment, takes a travel mug from his pocket and sets it down, too. Five.*

*The PA looks at the row. He does not write anything. After a long moment he sets down his pen on
the clipboard and places the clipboard next to the cups. Six.*

**LOWER THIRD (DOC UI):**
```
OBJECTS OF RECORD: THERMOS (3) + CREW-OWNED (3)
THERMOS (1): WARM
THERMOS (2): WARM
THERMOS (3): WARM
CREW-OWNED (3): AMBIENT
```

**PRODUCER (O.S., radio, light, a half-step off)**
*(French; SUBTITLED: "That's — nice. That's very nice. Don't let it get sentimental.")*
C'est — gentil. C'est très gentil. Ne laissez pas ça devenir sentimental.

**SOUND GUY**
*(French; SUBTITLED: "You're the only one in here not drinking.")*
T'es le seul ici à ne pas boire.

*The radio does not answer.*

*Cam looks at the six things on the floor. Three are warm for no reason. Three are the temperature
of a room. The camera finds the difference in the way the steam moves in the line of light, the
three that rise and the three that don't.*

*And then, very slowly, she puts her own hand around hers and holds it, as one holds a hand.*

**LOWER THIRD (DOC UI), final card:**
```
THE WARMTH IS NOT A PERFORMANCE
THE WARMTH IS NOT YOURS
```

*SMASH TO BLACK.*

*END COLD OPEN.*
