**PRODUCTION NOTE, READ BEFORE THE SCRIPT:**

Friday, the third and last (Beleth #13, 2.22 Hz). The wall's eleventh line is *lend him something*
(VH40). The unit has four days of foreknowledge and one rule. This episode is the rule being kept.

**Form.** The **Loan Register.** The PA reads it aloud, item by item; each lender says a color and a
cost (Red: "yes, and it costs," VH26); the Doc UI prints the register as it is read. It is the show's
quietest inventory. It is also the unit's answer to the older Hana's account (*"He had nothing to
owe us"*): an object for each person, a cost named for each object, and a reason to come back.

**Three payoffs, in order.**
1. **The PA's word.** In VH09 he wrote one word on the back of a page; in VH28 the unit learned he
   loves the sound guy; in VH33 he started measuring the soup. He says it now. It is the word
   `PA-001` has held for eleven days. *"Reste."*
2. **Cam's Entry 25 (VH).** The entry that appeared in her notebook after she crossed the line in VH19 (an
   entry "in a hand that is hers and is not hers"), read aloud, in English, to the person it concerns.
   **This is the first time in the block a withheld text is spoken.** She could keep it sealed. Sealed
   is a choice (VH04). She releases it. Releasing is a choice too.
3. **Tyler's one true thing:** *the porch light.*

**Not resolved:** the cost the Convener will name; the older Hana's; the Producer's question.

*Nobody speaks. It is Friday, Venus's day, and the amplitude is the natural one: the tubes are warm, the
room has the quality of a room at which something is about to be said that has been in the air a
long time. The PA is still standing. He has the clipboard. In its margin, in a column, beside the
soup temperature and the Gap, is a word, written on the back of a page on the ninth day and carried
since. He looks at it. He puts the clipboard down on the cloth.*

*He crosses the room. He crosses it slowly, as a man crosses a ledge. Tyler watches him come. Tyler does not
stand. He sits with his hands on his knees and the cup of coffee Hana poured him an hour ago, cold,
in front of him, and his face is open.*

*The PA stops in front of the seventh chair.*

**PA**
*(French; SUBTITLED: "Reste.")*

*One word. It is not a request. It is not an order. It is a man giving back, at last, the thing that was

**TYLER**
*(French; SUBTITLED, very gently: "I can't.")*

*(French; SUBTITLED: "I know. I wanted to have said it.")*

**TYLER**
*(French; SUBTITLED: "Say it again.")*

*(French; SUBTITLED, and his voice goes, and he gets it back: "Reste.")*

**TYLER**
*(French; SUBTITLED: "I owe you that.")*

*The PA smiles. It is the first time in the whole of the cold open that he has smiled. He sits down on the floor, where

exactly in the posture of a man who has set something down.*

*Cam has not spoken since "I write the log in pencil." She has the notebook in her hand: the cloth cover, the corner worn
soft. She opens it to a page, not a new one. Her thumb is on it. She looks at the lens, at the strips of tape
on the hood: black, green, red, yellow, white. Her thumb moves to white.*

*She takes it off again.*

**CAM**
*(English; the first English she has spoken since the second episode, and her voice is low, and level,
and quite unlike the voice of the woman who has been speaking French for twenty days; SUBTITLED: —
no subtitle. It isn't needed.)*
I'd like to read something.

*Tyler turns. She is not looking at him. She is looking at the page.*

**CAM (CONT'D)**
*(English)*
It's Entry 25. It appeared in my notebook after I crossed. The hand's not quite mine. It's mine, after
seventy-two minutes of something I can't remember.

**CAM (CONT'D)**
*(English, reading, in a flat clear voice, the way she reads her own notes)*
"He'll be back. The chair will be here. Keep it warm."

*She closes the notebook.*

*Nobody moves. The Doc UI, which has withheld a word every time one was sealed, holds the line it was
given with perfect fidelity and no marker.*

**CAM (CONT'D)**
*(English)*
The other one said, "the camera will be here." Back in the first year. I think it's the same sentence. I
think I changed the noun.

*In the eighth chair the older Hana has put her face in her hands. She is not crying. She is just holding
her own head.*

**TYLER**
*(English, after a silence, quietly)*
It's a very good noun.

On croit que la dette, c'est la laisse.

C'est la lampe du porche.
