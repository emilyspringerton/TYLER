### Build 0166 | Einhorn Media | VALHANNA, continued — MOON 1, DAY 4

---

**PRODUCTION NOTE, READ BEFORE THE SCRIPT:**

Wednesday is Mercury's day (Agares #2, 0.51 Hz: *language acquisition and transit time dilation*;
`README.md` §XXIII: "the Agares frequency inverts during retrograde periods… language-as-direction
becomes misdirection… signs lie… the schedule is always the confident prediction, and Agares
inverted punishes confidence"). It is not retrograde. The Doc UI's subtitle track behaves as though
it were.

**Form: the subtitles lie.** Every SUBTITLED gloss in this episode is wrong in a way the French is
not. The script prints the lying gloss, marked `[LYING]`, and the French, and where it matters the
true meaning, marked `[TRUE]`. The crew speaks French and never reads the track; they have no
reason to. Only one person in the room reads the monitor, because only one person needs it: the
Duck, who has been standing in a French-speaking room for three days with English and a good deal of
patience.

**What this episode is for.** (1) The Duck's French comes back on a Wednesday. It does not need a
reason, but it has one: it is Mercury's day and the French word for it is *mercredi.* (2) The
unit has two members it has treated, by long courtesy, as a unit within the unit — the PA and
the sound guy — and a lying subtitle makes it impossible to keep treating them that way. (3)
The Doc UI prints the show's title for the first time. It prints it on a Wednesday. The Cataloguer
will have a footnote; the Cataloguer's footnote is in VH35.

**Not resolved:** what the title is *for*; the tenth ingredient; the sheet; the Gap Meter's drift.

**LOWER THIRD (DOC UI):**

*The Auditor's pot is wider than the corridor sink. The PA and the sound guy are carrying it between
them, one at each handle, down a hallway that was not built for an object this size, toward a sink
that was not built for it either. Cam walks behind with the rig on her shoulder. Behind her, a
duck in a tuxedo, on tiptoe, reading the little monitor on the side of the camera.*

*The PA backs through a door, pot first. The sound guy follows, steering it.*

**PA**
*(French; SUBTITLED [LYING]: "I'm done with you.")*
Je te suivrais n'importe où.

*[TRUE: "I'd follow you anywhere."]*

**SOUND GUY**
*(French; SUBTITLED [LYING]: "Get out of my sight. Take the stairs with you.")*
Moi aussi. Mais pas dans cet escalier.

*[TRUE: "Me too. But not down that staircase."]*

*They set the pot down on the lip of the sink and stand with their hands on it, breathing, both of
them smiling a little at the pot. Behind them the Duck has stopped.*

**DUCK**
*(English, alarmed, quietly)*
Oh no.

*Cam glances back. The Duck is reading the monitor. Its beak is open.*

**DUCK (CONT'D)**
*(English)*
Are you two breaking up?

**PA**
*(French; SUBTITLED [LYING]: "That's the worst thing you've ever said.")*
Quoi ? Non !

*[TRUE: "What? No!"]*

**DUCK**
*(English, reading)*
"That's the worst thing you've ever said." It's right here.

*The unit stops. Cam lowers the rig and turns the monitor around. She reads it. She reads it again,
silently. Her French has always been better than her tolerance for the subtitles; she has never
once had cause to look.*

**CAM**
*(French; SUBTITLED [LYING]: "Nice work, everyone.")*
Le moniteur ment.

*[TRUE: "The monitor's lying."]*

---

*Tyler has come out into the corridor with a bowl in one hand and the ladle in the other, and he
stands in the doorway, listening. He is the only person in the building who has never once looked
at the track.*

**TYLER**
*(French; SUBTITLED [LYING]: "I love it when the plan works.")*
Mercure. Le chemin évident est celui qui se dilate.

*[TRUE: "Mercury. The obvious path is the one that stretches."]*

**SOUND GUY**
*(French; SUBTITLED [LYING]: "We should do this more often.")*
On est mercredi. Éteignez-le.

*[TRUE: "It's Wednesday. Turn it off."]*

*Cam presses a key on the rig. The monitor goes still.*

**LOWER THIRD (DOC UI):**
```
SUBTITLES: OFF (BY REQUEST)
```

*She leaves it off. For the rest of the shot the script glosses the French in the reader's
margin and no one else's.*

---

*The Duck is standing in the middle of the corridor with a look on its face of a thing that has
been handed a sentence it did not expect to be able to read.*

**DUCK**
*(English)*
"Éteignez-le."

*(a beat)*

**DUCK (CONT'D)**
*(French, slowly, with the delicate accent of a bird that has dreamed in the language for three
weeks of nights)*
Éteignez-le. Ça veut dire : éteignez-le.

*Nobody breathes. It is a plain sentence, and it is correct.*

**DUCK (CONT'D)**
*(French)*
Je comprends.

*[gloss: "I understand."]*

**CAM**
*(French)*
Tu parles français.

*[gloss: "You speak French."]*

**DUCK**
*(French, thinking about it)*
Depuis ce matin. C'est mercredi.

*[gloss: "Since this morning. It's Wednesday."]*

*It is a very Duck answer. It is also, the room feels, the whole of the explanation.*

**TYLER**
*(French)*
Agares.

*[gloss: "Agares."]*

*He says only that. He raises the ladle an inch in salute to the bird and goes back into the room.*

---

*The PA and the sound guy have not moved from the sink. The sound guy is looking at the pot. The PA
is looking at the sound guy. The Duck, still in the middle of the corridor, translates for
the unit's benefit what it has been reading for four minutes.*

**DUCK**
*(French)*
Le moniteur a dit que vous vous sépariez.

*[gloss: "The monitor said you were splitting up."]*

**SOUND GUY**
*(French)*
On ne se sépare pas.

*[gloss: "We're not splitting up."]*

**PA**
*(French, a little too quickly, and then, because the corridor is empty and the pot is between them
and there is a duck, all at once)*
Je t'aime, tu sais.

*[gloss: "I love you, you know."]*

*The sound guy takes his hands off the pot. He takes his headphones off his neck and hangs them on
the sink tap, as a man puts down something he doesn't want near what comes next.*

**SOUND GUY**
*(French)*
Je sais. Je l'entends depuis Reykjavik.

*[gloss: "I know. I've heard it since Reykjavik."]*

*Cam, behind the lens, in a very small voice, in a corridor in which a great deal has been said:*

**CAM**
*(French)*
On le sait tous.

*[gloss: "We all know."]*

**HANA (O.S., from the room)**
*(French)*
Depuis avant Reykjavik.

*[gloss: "Since before Reykjavik."]*

*The Duck, very softly, in English, to nobody:*

**DUCK**
*(English)*
Now that one I understood.

---

*They carry the pot out of the corridor an hour later, washed, and back into the room. The Duck
walks behind it, in French, narrating the scene for the monitor out of habit: "Ils portent une
marmite. Elle est propre. Ils sont contents." It does not know it is narrating. It does not need
the track.*

*The room. The tubes. The seven chairs, the thermoses on the fourth, the horse by the seventh. And
Cam's monitor, switched back on at last, because the Doc UI has something to say.*

**LOWER THIRD (DOC UI):**
```
SUBTITLES: ON (RESTORED)
TITLE CARD: DISPLAYED FOR THE FIRST TIME
```

*A card fills the viewfinder. It is not the series title. It is not the one from the pilot. It is
in the type the show uses for the things it does not explain.*

```
V A L H A N N A
```

*The Duck reads it aloud, slowly, in French, one syllable at a time, a bird at a menu.*

**DUCK**
*(French)*
Val. Han. Na.

*[gloss: "Val. Han. Na."]*

**SOUND GUY**
*(French)*
Val… la salle ?

*[gloss: "Val… the hall?"]*

**PA**
*(French)*
Il y a Hana dedans.

*[gloss: "There's Hana in it."]*

**HANA**
*(French, quiet)*
Ce n'est pas mon nom.

*[gloss: "It isn't my name."]*

**DUCK**
*(French)*
C'est une salle. Elle dit pour qui.

*[gloss: "It's a hall. It says who it's for."]*

**HANA**
*(French)*
Pour qui ?

*[gloss: "For whom?"]*

*The Duck looks at the card. It looks at her. It is not a long look. A duck does not do long looks.
It does very precise ones.*

**DUCK**
*(French)*
Pour celle qui choisit.

*[gloss: "For the one who chooses."]*

**HANA**
*(French, after a pause)*
Je n'ai choisi personne.

*[gloss: "I haven't chosen anyone."]*

**TYLER (from the seventh chair, mildly, the ladle across his knee)**
*(French)*
Tu as choisi de demander.

*[gloss: "You chose to ask."]*

*Hana does not answer. The card holds on the viewfinder for exactly as long as it takes her to
look at the floor, and then it is gone, and the track is lying again on the monitor, and no one
reads it.*

**LOWER THIRD (DOC UI), small, in the track, in a gloss no one reads:**
```
[LYING] "SEE YOU ON THURSDAY."
```

*SMASH TO BLACK.*
