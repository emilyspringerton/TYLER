## COLD OPEN

```
INT. SERVER ROOM — ICELAND — NO YEAR GIVEN
```

*The printer, which has been silent since the repo construct ran out in VH02, clatters. The three
crew members turn as one. A single sheet feeds out, tears at the perforation by itself, and drops
into the tray. The PA picks it up as one picks up a summons.*

**LOWER THIRD (DOC UI):**
```
TC  — / —  ·  11:50:00
```

*On the page, in 1986 dot-matrix, with a heading in the cold ornamental typeface the Shell
Parliament uses for money:*

**ON SCREEN (the invoice):**
```
HEIKEGANI CRUSTOCRAT FINANCIAL BUREAU
STATEMENT OF ACCRUAL — ACCOUNT CO-001 (JOINT LIABILITY)
PERIOD:  _________
RATE:    PER DIEM
DAYS:    1
AMOUNT:  ERROR
```

**PA**
*(French; SUBTITLED: "It says 'error.'")*
Ça dit « erreur ».

**SOUND GUY**
*(French; SUBTITLED: "It's a good error. It's honest.")*
C'est une bonne erreur. Elle est honnête.

*The sheet shudders in the PA's hand. The printer gives a second, shorter chatter and a second
sheet falls into the tray. The PA reads it aloud, as he reads everything.*

**ON SCREEN (the second page):**
```
NOTICE OF EXCEPTION
Accrual engine cannot calculate against a day with no date.
Engine will retry.
```

**PA**
*(French; SUBTITLED: "'Engine will retry.'")*
« Le moteur va réessayer. »

*The printer clatters. A third sheet. And a fourth. The PA begins to catch them, then stops, and
simply holds out his arms for the printer to load.*

---

*A montage, over the printer's rhythm. Each page is the Shell Parliament trying a different method:*

**ON SCREEN (cut, one second each):**
```
RETRY 1: DIVIDE BY DAY OF WEEK .................. NO WEEKDAY
RETRY 2: DIVIDE BY MONTH ......................... NO MONTH
RETRY 3: DIVIDE BY PRIOR MONTH (28 DAYS) ......... REMAINDER 1
RETRY 4: ASSIGN TO NEAREST DAY (DAY 364) ......... DECLINED BY DAY 364
RETRY 5: ASSIGN TO NEAREST DAY (DAY 1) ........... DECLINED BY DAY 1
RETRY 6: ASSIGN TO LIABLE PARTY .................. NO LIABLE PARTY ON RECORD
```

*The PA stops on the sixth page. He reads it twice. He does not read it aloud.*

**CAM**
*(French; SUBTITLED: "What does it say?")*
Qu'est-ce que ça dit ?

**PA**
*(French; SUBTITLED: "It says there's no one to bill. The account's joint. It needs two names. It
only has one that's — in the room.")*
Ça dit qu'il n'y a personne à facturer. Le compte est joint. Il faut deux noms. Il n'y en a qu'un
qui est — dans la pièce.

*Cam's face does not change. The red light on the camera is steady.*

**CAM**
*(French; SUBTITLED, level: "Which one?")*
Lequel ?

*The PA looks at her. Looks at the paper. Looks, last, at the tape on the floor and the doorway
past it and the single green thermos on the sill.*

**PA**
*(French; SUBTITLED: "It doesn't say.")*
Ça ne le dit pas.

*That is a lie, very gently told. We can tell because the PA, who has not lied once in five
episodes, folds the page in half and puts it in his back pocket, where it does not fit.*

---

*The printer gives a final, lonely clack and a seventh sheet falls. The sound guy takes it, because
the PA won't. He reads it at arm's length, as if it might be hot.*

**SOUND GUY**
*(French; SUBTITLED: "'Account suspended. Court date: none. Thank you for your patience.'")*
« Compte suspendu. Date d'audience : aucune. Merci de votre patience. »

*He lowers the page.*

**SOUND GUY (CONT'D)**
*(French; SUBTITLED: "They said thank you.")*
Ils ont dit merci.

**CAM**
*(French; SUBTITLED: "That's how you know they mean it.")*
C'est comme ça qu'on sait qu'ils le pensent.

**LOWER THIRD (DOC UI), final card:**
```
CO-001: ACCRUAL SUSPENDED (ENGINE RETRIED 6 TIMES)
AMOUNT OWED: NONE
THE PA HAS A PAGE IN HIS POCKET
```

*SMASH TO BLACK.*

*END COLD OPEN.*
