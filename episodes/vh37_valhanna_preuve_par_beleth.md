# TYLER — VALHANNA, EPISODE 37 — COLD OPEN
## "Preuve par Béléth" ("Proof by Beleth")
### Build 0175 | Einhorn Media | VALHANNA, continued — MOON 1, DAY 13

---

**PRODUCTION NOTE, READ BEFORE THE SCRIPT:**

Friday, Venus's day (Beleth #13, 2.22 Hz, *catalytic love*). VH30's Producer offered to lower Beleth
and could not. Today he says why. `README.md` §VI (Tides): *"The Producer (O.S.), via the documentary
equipment which doubles as a Field projection array"* has been running Beleth at amplitude to keep a
crew "emotionally compromised and therefore easier to recruit." That is the Syndicate's design. This
episode is that design, disclosed to the people it was run on, by the person who ran it.

**Form.** An experiment. The PA runs a protocol on a clipboard with a pencil. The question is
whether the unit's fondness for itself was real or assisted, and the answer is a number. It is
the show's most scientific episode and it ends on the least scientific thing it has ever shown.

**The disclosure memo** (MEMO #081-R3) is in questions only. It does not use the word. It cannot.
It uses "the apparatus" and "the project," and it ends on the question the Producer has been unable
to ask for thirteen days.

**What the episode is for.** Rule 7 (*"one moment where you realize the warmth is not a
performance"*) has been the block's refrain. This is where the refrain is tested: by taking away the
assistance and counting.

**Not resolved:** how the Producer kept the lie as long as he did; what he would have done without
the Gap; the tenth ingredient.
