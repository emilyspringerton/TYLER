# TYLER — VALHANNA, EPISODE 32 — COLD OPEN
## "Le Dixième" ("The Tenth")
### Build 0170 | Einhorn Media | VALHANNA, continued — MOON 1, DAY 8

---

**PRODUCTION NOTE, READ BEFORE THE SCRIPT:**

It is Sunday again, which means the Auditor, which means soup. This is the second week of the
first moon, and the unit brings him what it made: nine of ten, the very good nine, from a card
with a blank on the last line. The Auditor tastes it. Then he does what he does when a thing is
about to happen that is not his to see: he leaves the room for a while.

**Form.** The middle of this episode is **black.** Not a cut: a *consent.* The unit has had the
five-color tape since Monday (VH26) and has not yet used the first color. They use it here. The PA
wants to set Camera 2 on the pot and see. The unit decides not to see. Both lenses go black for
nine minutes and forty seconds; the script gives the Doc UI log and nothing else. **Nothing is
recorded — not picture, not sound.** The audience is placed where the Auditor is: outside the room,
trusting it.

This is the Auditor's own ethic, VH04: *"I copy what's suppressed. I don't copy what you've sealed.
To suppress is to steal. To seal is to choose."* Black is refusal; here the one refusing is the unit,
on behalf of a man who didn't ask.

**What the audience is not told:** who adds the tenth. What the audience is allowed to notice: a
napkin, a pair of hands, a fleck on a lens.

**Not resolved:** who added it; whether the unit knows; the Gap's meaning.

*Three raps on the air beside the door. The Auditor comes in with the pot; this is the second pot, the
Sunday pot, the one he makes every week; the first pot, the Thursday-rinsed one, sits in the corner
of the room under a cloth. He puts the Sunday pot down in the middle of the arc and looks around
the room with the delight of a man who has found everything where he left it.*

*The unit does not let him take the lid off. The PA steps forward. The PA is holding a bowl in both
hands the way one holds a bowl at the end of a rehearsal.*

**PA**
*(French; SUBTITLED: "We made it.")*
On l'a fait.

**THE AUDITOR**
*(French; SUBTITLED: "Made what?")*
Fait quoi ?

*Hana nods at the first pot under its cloth. The Auditor looks at it. He looks at the bowl. He takes it. He
tastes. The whole unit watches him taste, a thing it is by now able to do together without
breathing. He holds the spoon in his mouth a moment longer than a spoon needs.*

**THE AUDITOR**
*(French; SUBTITLED: "Nine.")*
Neuf.

*A pause, in which the sound guy's shoulders do something small.*

**THE AUDITOR (CONT'D)**
*(French; SUBTITLED, warm: "A very good nine.")*
Un très bon neuf.

*He eats the rest of the bowl, standing, in front of them, with the open pleasure of someone
who has not been cooked for in thirty-one years or for as long as nobody remembers. When it is
empty he sets it down on the arm of the sixth chair, where the Duck, politely, pretends it has
noticed nothing.*

---

*Later. The two pots on the floor, the lids off. The Sunday pot is the Auditor's: nine, as ever.
The first is the unit's: nine, also. The unit has eaten neither and both. Tyler sits in the
seventh chair and is not in the pot's direction at all. He is looking at his hands.*

*The Auditor looks at his watch, a wristwatch, an old one, and looks at the room as if consulting it.*

**THE AUDITOR**
*(French; SUBTITLED: "I have a meter to read.")*
J'ai un compteur à lire.

**HANA**
*(French; SUBTITLED: "Here?")*
Ici ?

**THE AUDITOR**
*(French; SUBTITLED: "Down the corridor. It takes nine minutes.")*
Au bout du couloir. Ça prend neuf minutes.

*He looks at the pot he brought, and at the first pot, and at no one.*

**THE AUDITOR (CONT'D)**
*(French; SUBTITLED: "It may take eleven.")*
Ça peut prendre onze.

*He goes out. The corridor door closes behind him, gently, a man shutting a door on a room he
means to return to. The unit is left in the arc, and the pots. Nobody moves.*

---

*The PA's hand has found Camera 2: the small handheld, the backup, which he has had at his belt since
the day Cam crossed. He takes it out. He thumbs the switch. A red light comes on.*

**PA**
*(French; SUBTITLED, quietly: "We could point it at the pot.")*
On pourrait la pointer sur la marmite.

**CAM**
*(French; SUBTITLED: "We'd see who.")*
On verrait qui.

*Hana, who has been watching the corridor door, doesn't turn.*

**HANA**
*(French; SUBTITLED: "If we see, we know.")*
Si on voit, on sait.

**PA**
*(French; SUBTITLED: "And?")*
Et alors ?

**HANA**
*(French; SUBTITLED: "He said: I don't copy what's sealed.")*
Il a dit : je ne copie pas ce qui est scellé.

*The PA looks at the camera in his hand. He looks at it for a while. Then he looks at Cam, and she
is already holding out a strip of black tape.*

**CAM**
*(French; SUBTITLED: "Black.")*
Noir.

*She tears it with her teeth. She presses one length across the lens of the PA's handheld, and another
across the lens of her own rig. The tape is cut square, and it is smoothed down with a thumb, and the
two cameras look at nothing.*

*The Auditor is in the room. He has come back through the corridor door and is standing at the Sunday
pot with the spoon already in his hand. He looks ordinary. He looks like a man who read a meter.*

*Behind him, the unit has arranged itself in its chairs in the postures of people who were doing
something else. Cam is looking at the ceiling with professional interest. The sound guy is
looking at a point two feet to the left of everything. The PA is cleaning a pair of glasses that he
does not wear. Hana is looking at the Auditor's back; she does not look away from it. The horse
is eating nothing from the floor with great concentration. The Duck, in the sixth chair, is
looking at the pot.*

*The Auditor dips the spoon. He tastes.*

*He does not make a sound. He lets the spoon rest on his lip. His eyes close.*

*It is the second time we have seen him quiet. It lasts longer than the first.*

**THE AUDITOR**
*(French; SUBTITLED, barely audible: "Ten.")*
Dix.

*He opens his eyes. He does not look at anyone. He puts the spoon down on the lid of the pot, with great
care, as one puts down something that may have been a gift, and he says, to the pot, to the
tubes, to the whole bright inattentive room:*

**THE AUDITOR (CONT'D)**
*(French; SUBTITLED: "Thank you.")*
Merci.

*Nobody says "you're welcome." They say nothing at all. Tyler, in the seventh chair, is humming very
quietly, an absolutely ordinary tune, the kind a man hums to the dishes.*

**TYLER**
*(French; SUBTITLED, mildly: "It's getting cold.")*
Ça refroidit.

**THE AUDITOR**
*(French; SUBTITLED: "Yes.")*
Oui.

*He smiles. He picks up the ladle. He fills the bowls.*

---

*The PA, at the end of the arc, collects the handheld. He pulls the black tape from its lens. It
peels away with a small dry sound. On the lens, just inside the rim, there is a fleck of something
red, perhaps a quarter-inch across.*

*Cam is watching. She reaches across and, with a motion so quick it could be called a touch, wipes
the fleck off the glass with the cuff of her sleeve.*

*The PA looks at her. She looks at him.*

**PA**
*(French; SUBTITLED, low: "I didn't see anything.")*
Je n'ai rien vu.

**CAM**
*(French; SUBTITLED, equally low: "Neither did I.")*
Moi non plus.

**SOUND GUY (not looking)**
*(French; SUBTITLED: "Neither did I.")*
Moi non plus.

**HANA**
*(French; SUBTITLED: "Neither did I.")*
Moi non plus.

**OLDER HANA (O.S., corridor)**
*(French; SUBTITLED: "Neither did I.")*
Moi non plus.

**DUCK**
*(English)*
I saw a pot.

*Tyler has taken the napkin off the arm of his chair. He is drying his hands. Front and back, between
the fingers, once, and again, a full three seconds longer than a hand requires.*

**LOWER THIRD (DOC UI):**
```
TELL (HANDS): PRESENT
```

*Cam looks at his hands. She looks at the notebook in her jacket. She does not take it out. She looks
back at the lens, and at the strip of black tape in her fingers, which she folds in half and puts in
her pocket.*

*The Auditor, with his back to them, singing something under his breath, serves the tenth bowl.*

*SMASH TO BLACK.*

*END COLD OPEN.*
