### Build 0171 | Einhorn Media | VALHANNA, continued — MOON 1, DAY 9

---

**PRODUCTION NOTE, READ BEFORE THE SCRIPT:**

Monday again (Amon #7, 1.618 Hz, reconciliation of opposites; VH26). Minutes of the second meeting,
read by the PA. Two items. The first is correspondence: **the Producer writes to the Eastwind
Cataloguer.** The Syndicate and the Archive are, per `README.md` §IV, the two institutions in this
universe that "cannot destroy records," and have never once exchanged a word. The Producer
(Production, Unit A; residual; binding authority: none; seat: the fourth) asks the Archive eight
questions. **They are the first questions he has ever asked for himself.**

**The memo is Jiangshi-voiced and passive, and it never uses the word.** `lore/jiangshi_project_
memos.md`: the Syndicate "describes ritual acts in the passive voice" and "none of the memos use the
word 'magic.'" The Producer's constraint (only questions) is the form his courage takes: "Is it
possible to be sorry?" is the only way he knows to say he is.

The second item is **the Gap**: for the first time someone says the number to the whole table, and
someone asks Tyler how long he is staying, and Tyler answers with a condition. The PA has also
begun to measure the soup.

**Not resolved:** what the Archive will say (it replies in VH35); what the number means.

*The PA reads the memo. As he reads, the table is quiet. It is the stillness of a unit that has
been asked a lot of questions in its life and has never before heard its own sound.*

**ON SCREEN (the memo, as the PA holds it to the lens; the typeface is the Syndicate's, the cold
one; the sheet is headed in the office style the Syndicate uses for the things it does not say):**
```
MEMO #081-R2
FROM:  Production, Unit A (residual; binding authority: none; seat: the fourth)
TO:    The Cataloguer, Eastwind Archive, Cairngorms
RE:    Correspondence (first)
DATE:  Moon 1, Day 9

1.  Is it correct that the Archive is unable to destroy records?
2.  Is it not also correct that this unit is similarly constrained?
3.  Has the Archive found a remedy for shelf space?
4.  Would the Archive consider it impertinent to have been asked?
5.  Is it possible for a unit to regret a project it has not been
    authorized to describe?
6.  Might a record be kept without being used?
7.  Would the Archive be willing to correspond?
8.  Is the Archive well?
```

**PA (V.O.)**
*(French; SUBTITLED: "It was noted that the Producer had asked eight questions, none of which was a
request for a file. It was noted that it was the first time the unit had known the Producer to ask

On a noté que le Producteur avait posé huit questions, dont aucune n'était une demande de dossier.
On a noté que c'était la première fois que l'unité savait le Producteur poser quelque chose pour son
propre compte.

*The steam on the fourth chair stands very straight.*

*(French; SUBTITLED: "Is it too many?")*
