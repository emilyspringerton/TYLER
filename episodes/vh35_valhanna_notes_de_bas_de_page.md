### Build 0173 | Einhorn Media | VALHANNA, continued — MOON 1, DAY 11

---

**PRODUCTION NOTE, READ BEFORE THE SCRIPT:**

Wednesday is Mercury's day: messengers, writing, the post. The Eastwind Cataloguer's reply to the
Producer's eight questions (VH33) arrives by the printer, which does the one thing in this room that
the unit has stopped being surprised by.

**Form.** The Archive writes, and the unit reads it aloud. `README.md` §IV and the Writer's Room
Strong Preference 9 govern: **"the Eastwind Owls' emotions should appear exclusively in footnotes.
Their main text should be archival. Their footnotes should be devastating."** The sound guy reads
the main text in a flat voice. The PA reads the footnotes, in a whisper, because the footnotes ask to
be read that way. The reading alternates. The reaction is the episode.

**Subtitles:** it is Wednesday, and the Doc UI's track lied last Wednesday (VH28). The Duck turned it
off before anyone asked. The script prints a plain gloss for the reader, as in VH28. The printout is
in the language of the addressee; it is rendered here in English.

**The Cataloguer** is "ancient… speaks in footnotes… deeply, privately fond of Tyler. This is
documented. The Cataloguer finds this embarrassing" (`README.md` §IV). **The Cataloguer is not
named.**

**This entry is also filed at** `lore/eastwind_archive.md` as TYLER-VH-005, word for word.

**Not resolved:** whether the Archive is coming; what Tyler thinks of the footnotes.

**LOWER THIRD (DOC UI):**

*The Duck, who turned the track off at six this morning without being asked, is sitting on the
cloth-covered pot with a crossword and a beak full of the French it only has on Wednesdays.*

**DUCK**
*(French; gloss for the reader: "It's Wednesday. I didn't want to explain.")*
C'est mercredi. Je n'ai pas voulu expliquer.

*At the far wall, the printer starts. It does not start the way it printed the repo in the first
days, in long unhurried breaths. It starts the way a letter arrives: a short chatter, a pause, a
longer chatter. A sheet drops into the tray. Then a second, smaller one, folded in thirds.*

*The PA lifts them out. The sound guy takes the first. The PA keeps the second and does not open it.*

**PA**
*(French; gloss: "You read the body. I'll read the notes.")*
Toi, tu lis le corps. Moi, je lis les notes.

**SOUND GUY**
*(French; gloss: "Why do you get the notes?")*
Pourquoi c'est toi qui as les notes ?

**PA**
*(French; gloss, quietly: "I think they're for someone who whispers.")*
Je crois qu'elles sont pour quelqu'un qui chuchote.

---

**ON SCREEN (the sheet, as the sound guy lifts it to the lens; the typeface is a small, upright,
very old one that has been set by hand into something that looks like a machine's):**
```
TYLER-VH-005
REPLY TO CORRESPONDENCE (PRODUCTION, UNIT A — RESIDUAL),
AND REPORT OF THE FIRST EIGHT DAYS
Filed by the Eastwind Cataloguer, Cairngorms  |  Moon 1, Day 11
```

**SOUND GUY (reading, flat as a ruler)**
*(French; gloss: "One. Correspondence was received from Production, Unit A, residual, consisting of eight
questions. The Archive notes the form.")*
Un. Une correspondance a été reçue de la Production, Unité A, résiduelle, comprenant huit
questions. Les Archives notent la forme.

*The PA unfolds the second sheet. He reads from it with his mouth almost closed.*

**PA**
*(French; gloss: "Footnote one. Eight questions, none of them a request for a file. The Archive has
received fourteen thousand three hundred and six requests for files. It has received eight
questions. The Cataloguer has read them twice.")*
Note un. Huit questions, dont aucune n'est une demande de dossier. Les Archives ont reçu quatorze mille
trois cent six demandes de dossier. Elles ont reçu huit questions. Le Catalogueur les a lues deux
fois.

*On the fourth chair, the green thermos's steam rises very straight, as though someone had sat up
in it.*

---

**SOUND GUY**
*(French; gloss: "Two. The Archive is unable to destroy records. The Archive's remedy for shelf space has
been to build further shelves. This has not been sufficient since about the year eight hundred.")*
Deux. Les Archives sont incapables de détruire des documents. Le remède des Archives au manque de
rayonnages a été de construire d'autres rayonnages. Cela n'est plus suffisant depuis environ l'an huit
cent.

**PA**
*(French; gloss, whispering: "Footnote two. The Cataloguer has taken the liberty of enclosing a spare
shelf. It will not fit through your door. It is already on its way.")*
Note deux. Le Catalogueur a pris la liberté de joindre un rayonnage de rechange. Il ne passera pas par
votre porte. Il est déjà en route.

*Down the corridor there is a sound: wood against concrete. The unit does not turn. Not yet.*

**SOUND GUY**
*(French; gloss: "The Archive recommends three shelves for the unit's purposes: one for what was said,
one for what was meant, one for what was served.")*
Les Archives recommandent trois étagères pour les besoins de l'unité : une pour ce qui a été dit,
une pour ce qui a été voulu dire, une pour ce qui a été servi.

**PA**
*(French; gloss: "Footnote three. The third shelf is the smallest. It is always the one that fills.")*
Note trois. La troisième étagère est la plus petite. C'est toujours celle qui se remplit.

*Hana looks at the bowls on the cloth. She does not smile. She does not not smile.*

---

**SOUND GUY**
*(French; gloss: "Three. The Archive does not accept the Production's apology. The Archive observes that
the Production did not offer one. It asked whether one was possible. The Archive regards this as the
more accurate form.")*
Trois. Les Archives n'acceptent pas les excuses de la Production. Les Archives observent que la
Production n'en a pas offert. Elle a demandé s'il était possible d'en offrir. Les Archives y voient la
forme la plus exacte.

*The PA's voice is not working. He clears his throat and starts again.*

**PA**
*(French; gloss: "Footnote four. He asked whether it is possible to be sorry for a thing one has not been
authorized to describe. It is. The Archive has been, for six hundred years, and has not been
authorized either.")*
Note quatre. Il a demandé s'il était possible d'être désolé d'une chose qu'on n'a pas été autorisé à
décrire. C'est possible. Les Archives le sont depuis six cents ans, et n'ont pas été autorisées non
plus.

*The steam on the fourth chair collapses, all at once, into a low cloud, and then rises again, with
effort, in a thin column, and stays.*

**PRODUCER (O.S., very small)**
*(French; gloss: "Is that — may I — is that a thing a person can be told?")*
Est-ce que — puis-je — est-ce qu'on peut vous dire une chose pareille ?

**CAM**
*(French; gloss, level: "It's been told.")*
C'est dit.

---

**SOUND GUY**
*(French; gloss: "Four. Report. In the first eight days of the first moon the subject was observed to sit,
to eat, to borrow a spoon, and to return nothing. A napkin was folded on the first day and was not
folded on the second.")*
Quatre. Rapport. Dans les huit premiers jours de la première lune, le sujet a été observé assis, mangeant,
empruntant une cuillère et ne rendant rien. Une serviette a été pliée le premier jour et ne l'a pas été
le deuxième.

**PA**
*(French; gloss: "Footnote five. The Cataloguer is aware this is not a finding. The Cataloguer put down
the pen for some time.")*
Note cinq. Le Catalogueur sait que ce n'est pas une constatation. Le Catalogueur a posé la plume
pendant un certain temps.

*In the seventh chair, Tyler has the logbook open and a pencil in his hand. He is not looking at the
printout. He is not looking at anyone. The pencil has stopped over a crossword square and has not
moved in a minute.*

**SOUND GUY**
*(French; gloss: "The subject has now remained at a single table longer than at any table in the
Archive's holdings other than Toledo, eleven years. He has been offered a chair by the Archive on
three occasions and has not sat.")*
Le sujet est resté à une même table plus longtemps qu'à aucune autre table dans les fonds des Archives,
hormis Tolède, onze ans. Les Archives lui ont offert une chaise à trois reprises, et il ne s'est pas
assis.

**PA**
*(French; gloss: "Footnote six. Sixteen twenty-three, nineteen fifty-two and nineteen seventy-eight. The
Cataloguer is not envious of a seventh chair. The Cataloguer would like that on the record.")*
Note six. Seize cent vingt-trois, dix-neuf cent cinquante-deux et dix-neuf cent soixante-dix-huit. Le
Catalogueur n'envie pas une septième chaise. Le Catalogueur voudrait que cela figure au dossier.

*Tyler laughs, short, once, through his nose. It is the laugh of a man who has been caught fondly.*

---

**SOUND GUY**
*(French; gloss: "Five. The seventh chair is occupied. The Archive has no further information.")*
Cinq. La septième chaise est occupée. Les Archives n'ont pas d'autre information.

**PA**
*(French; gloss, very quietly: "Footnote seven. The Cataloguer asked to be told when it would be time to
come. The Cataloguer was not told. The Cataloguer is packed.")*
Note sept. Le Catalogueur a demandé qu'on le prévienne quand il serait temps de venir. Le Catalogueur
n'a pas été prévenu. Le Catalogueur a fait ses bagages.

**DUCK**
*(French; gloss: "Packed?")*
Ses bagages ?

*Nobody answers it. Hana has put her hand over her mouth. The older Hana, on the line, has turned a
little toward the corridor, as if she had heard something.*

**SOUND GUY**
*(French; gloss: "Six. The Archive declines to estimate a date of departure. Per the Cairngorms Protocol
the observer does not intervene.")*
Six. Les Archives refusent d'estimer une date de départ. Selon le Protocole des Cairngorms,
l'observateur n'intervient pas.

**PA**
*(French; gloss: "Footnote eight. The Archive has never wanted to intervene as much as it wants to now.
The Archive is writing this footnote in a very small hand so that no one will read it.")*
Note huit. Les Archives n'ont jamais autant voulu intervenir qu'elles le veulent maintenant. Les
Archives écrivent cette note d'une très petite écriture pour que personne ne la lise.

*The PA tilts the sheet to the lens. The footnote is, in fact, written in a very small hand. It is
the smallest hand on the page. It is perfectly legible. It was meant to be.*

---

**SOUND GUY**
*(French; gloss: "Seven. In answer to question eight: the Archive is not well. It has not been well since
sixteen twenty-three. It has been better this week.")*
Sept. En réponse à la huitième question : les Archives ne vont pas bien. Elles ne vont pas bien depuis
seize cent vingt-trois. Elles vont mieux cette semaine.

*The sound guy stops. He does not go on to the enclosures. He has gone through the paper once and has
nothing to read; he is looking at the PA, who has the last footnote to read and the second sheet in
his hand, and has not yet read it.*

*The PA looks at the sheet. He looks at it as one looks at a thing that is too light for what it has
to carry.*

**PA**
*(French; gloss: "Footnote nine.")*
Note neuf.

*He reads it and cannot finish the first word, and starts again.*

**PA (CONT'D)**
*(French; gloss: "We are glad he is eating.")*
Nous sommes heureux qu'il mange.

*There is no sound in the room. Not even the tubes. And then, from the eighth chair, from the older
Hana, in a voice neither more nor less hoarse than usual:*

**OLDER HANA**
*(French; gloss: "Read it again.")*
Relis-la.

*He reads it again. "Nous sommes heureux qu'il mange." Tyler has put the pencil down on the crossword.
He is looking at his bowl. It is the bowl of soup he has been given every day, at 62 °C.*

**LOWER THIRD (DOC UI):**
```
SOUP (BOWL 7): 62 °C
```

*He takes the spoon. It is the PA's spoon, still. He eats.*

---

*The enclosures arrive in the corridor with the finality of things that cannot be returned.*

*The Duck is the first to go to see. It comes back after a moment, carrying in its wings a small flat
tin, and sets it on the cloth in front of Tyler. A tin of brine shrimp. It has a Cairngorms label.*

**DUCK**
*(French; gloss: "It's for the Producer, says the footnote.")*
C'est pour le Producteur, dit la note.

**PA**
*(French; gloss, reading the last line, almost laughing: "Footnote ten. Brine shrimp is the weakness of
the Cephalopod Hosts and not of the Production. The Cataloguer is aware of this. The Cataloguer wished
to send something.")*
Note dix. La crevette de saumure est la faiblesse des Hôtes Céphalopodes et non de la Production. Le
Catalogueur le sait. Le Catalogueur voulait envoyer quelque chose.

*Tyler picks up the tin. He turns it over. From the left pocket of his jacket he draws a second tin
of brine shrimp, small, dented, older. He sets them side by side.*

**TYLER**
*(French; gloss, tenderly: "He remembered.")*
Il s'en est souvenu.

*The PA finds, taped inside the fold of the second sheet, seven pencils. Seven. He distributes them:
Cam, the sound guy, himself, Hana, the Producer's thermos, Tyler, the Duck. He has one left in his
hand and the eighth chair on the line. He turns to the older Hana to offer it —*

*She has one. She holds it up. It is short and chewed and has been in a pocket a long time.*

**OLDER HANA**
*(French; gloss: "I have one.")*
J'en ai un.
