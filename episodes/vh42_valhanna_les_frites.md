**PRODUCTION NOTE, READ BEFORE THE SCRIPT:**

Wednesday, the third: Mercury, language, post. The post this time is a bag of fries. `HQ-CANON-
TYLER-105-EPOCH-EXTINCTION.md` §3: *Los Hermanos Pingüinos,* the Penguin Brothers, "a monastic order
operating behind the world's most innocuous storefront… excellent food, spotless floors, nobody
asks why the walk-in freezer has a server rack." Canon Rule 3: **"Los Hermanos Pingüinos never
confirm the storefront is a front. Customers who ask are given extra fries."** Every question in this
episode that asks whether they are monks is answered with fries.

**The ignition order** (S06b E0–E1, `manuscripts/tyler_book2.md`: *"Black is refusal. Green is
consent. Red is cost. Yellow is the part everyone forgets to plan for. White is what's left over once
you've paid for all four."*) is *"a Pingüino rite… the five vestment colors of the order's five
deacons"* (HQ-105 §3). *"In the cursed timeline the sequence is performed wrong, which the Brothers regard
as the gravest possible liturgical error: a precedence violation. Aunt Sally's jurisdiction."* S06b E1
also has a text on a flip phone: **SUIT: wrong order again.**

**Form.** The Brother speaks in S-expressions: nested lists, parentheses first, in Spanish (the
order is Spanish-named; the grammar is older). `LANGUAGE: S-EXPRESSION (LITURGICAL)`. This is a
Wednesday, so the Doc UI's glosses are not to be trusted; the Duck is the interpreter, and a good
one. The glosses printed are true.

**What the Brother does:** correct an order. Not explain it. *"The rite is not explained; it is
served."* He does it once, to a row of thermoses on a floor.

**Not resolved:** what the rite is for; who sent the Brother; what "Black" is texting.

*The five thermoses are on the floor in a row, where the unit has kept them since the first week: against
the dead rack, upright, side by side. The minestrone. The coffee. The green enamel tea. Hana's flask.
The Duck's white one. They are in the order in which they appeared, and no one has ever had a reason
to put them in another.*

*A knock at the corridor door. A proper one. The knock of a man who is paid to knock.*

*The PA opens it. There is a person on the sill: heavyset, dark-haired, in a black-and-white uniform
under a yellow apron that says nothing. A black-and-white paper cap. Gentle, tired, enormous eyes. In his
arms a paper bag, translucent with grease, and another under that, and another under that. He holds
them toward the room like a man bringing news.*

**BROTHER (YELLOW)**
*(S-expression, Spanish; DUCK, interpreting)*
`(entrega (papas-fritas extra) (a unidad-a))`

**DUCK**
*(English)*
"Delivery. Extra fries. To Unit A."

*(French; gloss: "We didn't order anything.")*
On n'a rien commandé.

**BROTHER (YELLOW)**
*(S-expression)*
`(siempre (alguien) (llega))`

**DUCK**
*(English)*
"Always somebody turns up."
