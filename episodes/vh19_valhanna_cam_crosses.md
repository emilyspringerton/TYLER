**PRODUCTION NOTE, READ BEFORE THE SCRIPT:**

For fifteen episodes, Cam has been the one holding the camera and the only person in the unit who
never needed to be told what she was. In VH09 she filmed herself as a subject. In VH16 she left the
camera locked off. VH19 is the crossing: the first person to step over the line of light, with the
record running.

**The camera protocol is the whole episode.** The PA runs a second camera from the near side. The
two feeds do not agree. Per `characters/camera_op.md`: *the Camera Op's awareness is calibrated to
the timeline.* Cam's is AWARE-PRESENT; this is the episode that tests it.

**Rule 21 remains: nothing resembling the transit stop appears.** What Cam sees on the other side
is not shown. Her feed goes to the same white as a camera pointed at a window.

**Not resolved:** what Cam sees; what she brings back; the Bird Correction (PENDING).

## COLD OPEN

```
INT. SERVER ROOM — ICELAND — NO YEAR GIVEN
```

*Midnight, or what the timecode calls midnight. The seconds roll over: 23:59:59 becomes 00:00:00.
The date field does not change from dashes. The UI hesitates for the length of one frame and then
displays a thing it has not displayed before.*

**LOWER THIRD (DOC UI):**
```
TC  — / —  ·  00:00:00
DAY DID NOT END
```

**PA**
*(French; SUBTITLED: "It's tomorrow. It's not tomorrow.")*
C'est demain. Ce n'est pas demain.

**HANA**
*(French; SUBTITLED: "It's still the day. Midnight didn't take it.")*
C'est toujours le jour. Minuit ne l'a pas pris.

*Cam is standing at the tape. She has been standing there for some time. She has taken her jacket
off. The rig is on her shoulder.*

**CAM**
*(French; SUBTITLED: "I'm going to go over.")*
Je vais traverser.

*No one is surprised. Hana says nothing. The PA, after a moment, goes to the second camera — the
small one, the backup, a handheld in his bag — turns it on, and frames her from behind.*

**LOWER THIRD (DOC UI):**
```
CAMERA 1: CAM (ON SHOULDER)
CAMERA 2: THE PA (HANDHELD)
```

**PA**
*(French; SUBTITLED: "I'll hold your back.")*
Je tiens ton dos.

**CAM**
*(French; SUBTITLED: "That's the whole job.")*
C'est tout le travail.

---

*She steps over the tape. The horse, beside her, steps with her. Cam puts a hand flat on the
horse's neck, not to lean on her, but because her hand wants somewhere to be. They walk toward the
line of light together, the horse's hooves loud on the concrete, Cam's boots loud on the concrete,
the camera held at her eye like a weapon she is learning to put down.*

**ON SCREEN (CAMERA 2, the PA's, from behind and to the left):**
*Cam and the horse at the threshold. The light is on their faces. Cam's shoulders are rising and
falling. She puts one foot over the sill. Then the other. For one frame, in the PA's picture, she
is exactly where she is. Then the frame holds her and the doorway, and she has not moved.*

**ON SCREEN (CAMERA 1, Cam's own):**
*A white field. The color of a lens pointed into a window. Nothing in it, no horizon, no edge.
The sound of her breath. The sound of the horse's. And in the white, a thin line of text, white
on white, readable only because the UI has darkened it:*

```
CAMERA 1: SUBJECT NOT IN FRAME
```

**LOWER THIRD (DOC UI), split screen, both feeds:**
```
CAM1: WHITE        CAM2: DOORWAY, 1 SUBJECT, STATIONARY
```

*On Camera 2, she is standing on the sill, in the light, looking at nothing, absolutely still, a
statue of herself. On Camera 1, the white goes on.*

**SOUND GUY**
*(French; SUBTITLED, a whisper: "Which one's real?")*
Laquelle est vraie ?

**HANA**
*(French; SUBTITLED: "Both. That's how you know she's there.")*
Les deux. C'est comme ça qu'on sait qu'elle y est.

---

*The PA keeps the shot. His hand is steady. We hear him breathing through his mouth, shallow and
regular. The white goes on. Eleven seconds. Twenty. At thirty, Camera 1 flickers. A single frame,
too fast to read; the PA's hand tightens on his own camera; the frame is gone.*

*Camera 2: Cam turns. In the doorway, in the light, she turns to look back at the room. Her face is
wet. She is smiling, and her mouth is shaped for a word.*

**CAM**
*(French; SUBTITLED, mouthing, no sound: "—")*

*The audio on Camera 2 drops to room tone, 7.83 beneath it. The UI doesn't say who dropped it.*

**LOWER THIRD (DOC UI), final card:**
```
CAM: ACROSS
RECORD: RUNNING
AUDIO: 1 WORD WITHHELD (BY WHOM: NOT LOGGED)
```

*SMASH TO BLACK.*

*END COLD OPEN.*
