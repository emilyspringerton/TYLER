## SCENE ONE — THE PROPOSAL

```
INT. THE GARAGE, CHROME EVERYWHERE, SAME ONE AS ALWAYS — CONTINUOUS
```

The Unicorn, mid-diagnostic-scan of its own reflection in a hubcap, apropos of absolutely nothing.

**UNICORN (TEXT CARD)**
We should have a band.

**TYLER**
You don't sleep. When would you practice.

**UNICORN (TEXT CARD)**
I didn't say a good band.

Nobody objects to the premise. This, itself, is treated as a green light.

---

## SCENE TWO — THE FIRST PITCH

**DUCK**
"Government Clearance." Capital letters. People will assume it's real and get nervous, which is
most of what a band name is supposed to do anyway.

**TYLER**
That's also just your ability.

**DUCK**
Everything's already the ability. That's the bit.

The Ghost, at the piano no one remembers bringing into the garage, plays one dismissive chord.

---

## SCENE THREE — THE MUSICIAN OBJECTS

```
SAME GARAGE. THE PIANO IS DOING A LOT OF LOAD-BEARING WORK IN THIS CUTSCENE.
```

**GHOST (TEXT CARD, still playing, doesn't look up)**
None of you have asked the one member of this gang who has ever, technically, been in a band.

**TYLER**
You've been in a band.

**GHOST (TEXT CARD)**
I've been *at* a piano continuously since before this sentence started. Whether that constitutes
a band is a metaphysics question, not a music one.

**GHOST (TEXT CARD, one beat later)**
I vote no on "Government Clearance." Too many syllables to scream.

---

## SCENE FOUR — THE FROG DECLINES TO PARTICIPATE, WHICH IS A FULL PITCH

The Frog, in its top hat, says nothing for eleven full seconds — timed, on screen, via a corner
counter the UI overlay adds specifically for this beat.

**FROG (TEXT CARD, once the counter hits eleven)**
My suggestion is a secret.

**TYLER**
That's not how band names work.

**FROG (TEXT CARD)**
It's exactly how mine works.

**DUCK**
That's not a name, that's just your whole ability again.

**FROG (TEXT CARD)**
Everything's already the ability. Weren't you just saying that.

Duck has no response to being quoted back at itself.

---

## SCENE FIVE — THE PIZZA MAKES A CASE FOR HONESTY

**PIZZA (TEXT CARD)**
"Uninvestigated." One word. Nobody's ever going to look into why, same as they never look into me.
Full transparency, zero follow-up. That's the whole genre right there.

**TYLER**
That's uncomfortably good.

**PIZZA (TEXT CARD)**
I am on fire and nobody has confirmed I mind. I have range.

The Tree, static in the corner as always, says one line in French. It is not subtitled this time.
Nobody in the garage reacts, which the UI overlay treats as confirmation that the joke landed for
everyone except the audience.

---

## SCENE SIX — THE VOTE THAT ISN'T A VOTE

```
SAME GARAGE. THE ARGUMENT HAS BEEN GOING LONG ENOUGH THAT DOC WHEEL SHOWS UP UNPROMPTED.
```

Doc Wheel enters mid-conversation, the way it enters everything, already halfway to fixing
something nobody flagged as broken.

**DOC WHEEL (TEXT CARD)**
Whatever you land on, put it on the van. I'm not driving anything unlabeled twice.

**TYLER**
There's a van.

**DOC WHEEL (TEXT CARD)**
There's always a van.

The Retrieval Cart arrives, unbidden, carrying a single unlabeled band T-shirt. No one asks where
from. It is, per house rule, already the answer to a question nobody finished asking.

---

## SCENE SEVEN — THE ACTUAL DECISION

The Ghost stops playing. The garage notices the silence before it notices the sentence.

**GHOST (TEXT CARD)**
Here's my pitch, properly, since apparently no one else's is landing. "Mid-Piano." No hyphen
debate — it's one word, functionally. It's what I am. It's the one thing in this room that's true
whether or not anyone's watching, which, given the premise of everything we do, feels like the
actual point.

A long pause. Not the Frog's kind — an actual one, everyone present at the same time.

**UNICORN (TEXT CARD)**
...Damn.

**DUCK**
I hate that it's better than "Government Clearance."

**FROG (TEXT CARD)**
I'm keeping my suggestion secret regardless. Just so we're clear that I could have.

**PIZZA (TEXT CARD)**
Uninvestigated is still available as an EP title. I'm not giving up the bit, I'm shelving it.

## TAG — MID-PIANO

```

"MID-PIANO" — CONFIRMED, PER THE GANG, WHETHER OR NOT ANYONE ASKED
```

The Ghost resumes playing. Everyone else drifts back to whatever they were doing before the

**TYLER (V.O., last line, doesn't complete a sentence about himself, per standing rule)**
Nobody voted. It just got quieter once it was true. That's usually how the real ones land.
