### Build 0181 | Einhorn Media | VALHANNA, continued — MOON 1, DAY 19

---

**PRODUCTION NOTE, READ BEFORE THE SCRIPT:**

Thursday, the third. Jupiter, Stolas #36, 7.83 Hz: deep time sight, the hum, the day Tyler's instrument
reads highest. On the first Thursday the room saw eleven seconds of branches without his leave
(VH29, "I didn't do that on purpose"). On this one he asks to do the opposite.

**The Hybrid Arc** (`README.md` §XXIII): the interference-pattern state can be *navigated*: "Navigate
toward antinodes (voluntary absence — this comes later)." An *antinode* is where the pattern has zero
amplitude. "At a node, Tyler appears to be there. At an antinode, he appears to be gone. Both are the
same pattern." The canon says the hybrid "cannot maintain the interference state deliberately for more
than eleven seconds." **He proposes twelve.**

**Form.** Not branches this time. The Doc UI counts again, one to twelve, and each second is a
thing in the room: not what he sees; what *they* see. It is the first time the unit has watched him
leave on purpose. It is, he says, practice.

**The Gap Meter** does something it has not done: the moment he goes, it slams to its stop. *20.87.* The
full gap. It shows the unit what Saturday night will look like on the instrument. Nobody says
this. Everybody understands it.

**Rule 21** is untouched: the place he goes is not described, shown or named. "Nowhere. Politely."
**Rule 20:** he cannot explain the state; he explains the practice.

**Not resolved:** where; what it costs; the third Thursday's "next Thursday."

*The sound guy opens the back of the Gap Meter. It is the third time. They have the routine of

across; the lunchbox's needle sweeping and settling. He nods.*

**THE AUDITOR (CONT'D)**

*(French; SUBTITLED: "Next Thursday I'll —")*
Jeudi prochain je —

*He stops. He looks at the Gap Meter, which reads 17.89. He does not finish. He picks up the lid
of his lunchbox and closes it with the care of a man who has said a thing he knows is not true and
does not know which way.*

*Tyler has been sitting in the seventh chair since dawn, with the crossword, a pencil, the horse's head

**HANA**

**TYLER**
*(French; SUBTITLED, mild: "Leaving.")*
Partir.

Thank you.

He closes his eyes. He begins to hum, very low, unhurried, an ordinary tune: and then it is the
tune and the other tune, 7/8 and 11/8 at once, in the ratio that does not come out even.*
