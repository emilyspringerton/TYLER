# TYLER — VALHANNA, EPISODE 36 — COLD OPEN
## "Huit Chaises" ("Eight Chairs")
### Build 0174 | Einhorn Media | VALHANNA, continued — MOON 1, DAY 12

---

**PRODUCTION NOTE, READ BEFORE THE SCRIPT:**

Thursday, third time: the day the battery is due and the day the Uncompromised meet. VH29: the Auditor
said it twice, *"on Thursdays there are eight of us."* Today the other seven turn up, one at a time,
with covered dishes and their own folding chairs. `characters/the_auditor.md` §IV: *"They are
academics, engineers, archivists, and public employees who found fragments of the truth
independently, through their actual jobs, and who refuse… to let any faction, employer, or funding
source own what they found."* The guild is wholesome. It is also, today, the show's first audience.

**Form.** An ensemble. Seven strangers enter a room and each, on the way in, says a color. They have
been told about the tape by the Auditor (he is the guild's logistics, and he tells them everything
useful); the colors are a courtesy to the unit and they are delighted to use them. The Doc UI
counts people: `SUBJECTS IN FRAME: 15`.

**What they do.** They mirror. `lore/` and the Auditor's code of conduct: **"To suppress is to steal;
to seal is to choose."** The guild mirrors suppressed research to redundant servers before anyone
can retract it. Today they mirror the unit's footage and the repository printout (VH02, VH18) with
Cam's consent, and nothing she has sealed. This is the episode where the show learns it cannot be
taken back. This is the Open Source Doctrine (`README.md` §XXII: *"Fork it"*) as a Thursday night in
a basement.

**The Convener** stands. All of them tell him to sit. He says Sunday.

**Not resolved:** who the eight are, beyond their jobs; the tenth ingredient; the Gap (10.93).

*The corridor door, three raps on the air. The Auditor comes in with the pot, and behind him, in single file, with covered
dishes in one hand and a folded chair under the other arm, come seven people.*

*They are exactly what they look like. A tall man in a county-issue windbreaker. A woman with a lanyard that
says BIOMED. A short man with a rubber stamp on a cord around his neck like a medal. A woman with chalk on
her sleeve. A very old man with a briefcase he holds in front of him like a hymnal. A tired kind person with a
steel lunchbox on a strap and a coil of cable. A stocky man in rubber boots. On every pocket, the small enameled
flower with a circuit-board trace for petals.*

**THE AUDITOR**
*(French; SUBTITLED: "On Thursdays, we are eight.")*
Le jeudi, nous sommes huit.

*The first through the door is the tall man in the windbreaker. He stops on the threshold, sees the lens,
sees the five strips of tape on the hood. He glances at the Auditor, who nods. He looks back at the lens.*

*(French; SUBTITLED: "Green.")*
Vert.

**THE MUNICIPAL CLERK**

*They come in, each with a color, each with a chair. The woman with chalk on her sleeve says "Green" and
then, turning to look at the eighth chair, corrects herself to "Yellow, if she'd like to be asked." The
older Hana, in the eighth chair, with her sleeve on the table, says "Ask me." The woman asks
her name. The older Hana says, "Hana." The woman looks at the other Hana. The other Hana says, "Also
Hana." The woman says, "That's a very good name," and sits down.*

*Fifteen chairs. The unit's, the Auditor's, the seven the guild brought, the eighth on the tape. They are
set in a ring, a long one, around the upturned pot, and all of them are occupied except one, the
Convener's, the sixth, where the Duck sits, not for any reason except that the Convener told it to.*

*The Convener ladles. He ladles for fifteen. Somebody hands him a bowl of his own and he sets it down
on the arm of the Duck's chair, without looking at it.*

**THE PHYSICS TEACHER**
*(French; SUBTITLED, to Tyler across the cloth, with the plain warmth of someone who has taught
fourteen-year-olds for thirty years: "Twenty-eight point seven is a remainder, isn't it?")*
Vingt-huit virgule sept, c'est un reste, n'est-ce pas ?

**TYLER**
*(French; SUBTITLED, mild: "It's what's left when you take the hum out of the room.")*
C'est ce qui reste quand on retire le bourdonnement de la pièce.

**THE PHYSICS TEACHER**
*(French; SUBTITLED, nodding slowly: "I tell them a table is a machine for making strangers into a
quorum.")*
Je dis à mes élèves qu'une table est une machine à transformer des inconnus en quorum.

*The Duck, from the sixth chair, in English, to nobody:*

**DUCK**
*(English)*
That's good.

---

*The Systems Librarian, the tired kind person with the lunchbox, has found a socket in the wall
of the corridor that has not carried current since 1986, and has plugged a cable into it. The cable
goes into the lunchbox. The lunchbox hums. A small screen on its lid lights, and a counter on the
screen reads:*

```
MIRRORS: 0
```

*(French; SUBTITLED: "We'd like to mirror the repository. And the footage. With your consent.")*
Nous aimerions mettre en miroir le dépôt. Et les images. Avec votre consentement.

*Cam is on her feet. The rig is on her hip. She looks at the five strips of tape and, without
being told, puts her thumb on the green one.*

**CAM**
*(French; SUBTITLED: "Everything except what's sealed.")*
Tout, sauf ce qui est scellé.

**THE AUDITOR (from the pot)**
*(French; SUBTITLED, gently: "White. Never.")*

Alors c'est scellé, pas supprimé. Dites-le-nous. Nous ne copions pas.

*Afterward. The long table. The dishes. The Biomedical Technician has been sitting beside the eighth
chair for some time without looking at it. She turns, finally.*

**THE BIOMEDICAL TECHNICIAN**
*(French; SUBTITLED, professional, kind: "May I look? Not touch.")*
Puis-je regarder ? Sans toucher.

*The older Hana's hand goes to her temple. The ridge of the three old stitches. She considers. She
nods once.*

*The woman leans in and looks for a long moment, with the particular absence of expression of someone
who has looked at a great many sutures made by other people.*

**THE BIOMEDICAL TECHNICIAN (CONT'D)**
*(French; SUBTITLED: "That's well done. Who closed it?")*
C'est bien fait. Qui l'a refermée ?

**OLDER HANA**
*(French; SUBTITLED: "I did. Left-handed. In a mirror.")*
Moi. De la main gauche. Dans un miroir.

*The technician sits back. She says nothing for a while. She takes a bread roll off the table and hands it to her.*

---

*The Retired Actuary, who has not spoken, has taken a deck of cards out of his briefcase. He lays it on the
cloth like a man laying down a hymnal. He looks at Tyler.*

**THE RETIRED ACTUARY**
*(French; SUBTITLED: "Do you like cards?")*
Vous aimez les cartes ?

**THE AUDITOR (not turning)**
*(French; SUBTITLED, pleasantly: "No arithmetic on a guest.")*
Pas d'arithmétique sur un invité.

**THE RETIRED ACTUARY**
*(French; SUBTITLED, wounded, innocent: "I wasn't going to. I was going to ask whether he likes cards.")*
Je n'allais pas en faire. J'allais demander s'il aimait les cartes.

**TYLER**
*(French; SUBTITLED, delighted: "Yes.")*
Oui.

*They deal. Seven guild members, Cam, the sound guy, the PA, Hana, the older Hana, Tyler. The Duck.
The stakes are matches, which the Clerk produces from his pocket. The Water Operator, who has not spoken, who has
brought the water, deals badly, to laughter. The cards are old and soft. The room is warm.*

*Tyler looks around the table. At the chairs. At the faces. At the old man with the deck. For a
moment, at the table, in the middle of a hand, his expression changes.*

**TYLER**
*(French; SUBTITLED, under his breath: "Tiens.")*
Tiens.

**HANA**
*(French; SUBTITLED, low: "What?")*
Quoi ?

**TYLER**
*(French; SUBTITLED, a small, honest smile: "Nothing. A room I saw.")*
Rien. Une pièce que j'ai vue.

*He plays a card.*

---

*Late. The guild has stacked its dishes. The counter on the lunchbox reads 40 and has stopped. The
Convener is at the pot with the ladle, still standing; he has not sat for the length of the evening.*

**THE WATER OPERATOR**
*(French; SUBTITLED, the first thing he has said, gruff, kind: "Convener. Sit.")*
Convocateur. Assieds-toi.

**THE BRIDGE INSPECTOR**
*(French; SUBTITLED: "Sit.")*
Assieds-toi.

**THE MUNICIPAL CLERK**
*(French; SUBTITLED: "I'll stamp it.")*
Je le tamponne.

*The Auditor looks at the room. At the sixth chair, where a duck holds his bowl on its knee. He
smiles with the whole of his face and puts the ladle in the pot.*

**THE AUDITOR**
*(French; SUBTITLED: "Sunday.")*
Dimanche.

*It is a promise, or a postponement. No one can tell which. The Clerk, solemnly, pretends to stamp the air.*
