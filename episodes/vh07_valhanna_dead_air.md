**PRODUCTION NOTE, READ BEFORE THE SCRIPT:**

The Producer has spoken on the radio in every VALHANNA episode so far. In VH04 a stranger spoke to
his radio as to a person ("You have a chair too") and the line went *quiet*, not cut. In VH05 he
admitted he was "reading it off the wall." In VH06 he declined to drink. VH07 is the episode where the sound guy,
who has been recording the Producer's every transmission as a matter of professional habit, plays
the tapes back. The Producer's voice has a cadence. The cadence is not his.

**Writer's Room Rule 2 holds:** the Producer, a Jiangshi-adjacent voice, says "production," "the
unit," "the project," and never the word. The episode puts a question where the word would go.

**Not resolved:** what the Producer is; Hana; Entry 23; the Bird Correction (still PENDING).

## COLD OPEN

```
INT. SERVER ROOM — ICELAND — NO YEAR GIVEN
```

*The sound guy has a laptop open on an equipment case, headphones around his neck, and a
spectrogram on the screen. He has been looking at it for a while. The others know better than to
ask.*

**LOWER THIRD (DOC UI):**
```
TC  — / —  ·  11:32:03
```

**SOUND GUY**
*(French; SUBTITLED: "I want to play you something.")*
Je veux vous faire écouter quelque chose.

*He presses a key. Through the laptop's small speaker, in the room, the Producer's voice from VH02:*

**PRODUCER (RECORDED, VH02)**
*(SUBTITLED: "Keep rolling. We're only here to document the room.")*

*Cut to the spectrogram: a stack of bright bands, speech. He presses another key. The Producer
again, VH03:*

**PRODUCER (RECORDED, VH03)**
*(SUBTITLED: "There's no rest in a production. Keep rolling.")*

*Another. VH04: not a sentence at all — the quiet that came up from the radio when the inspector spoke
to it. The sound guy lets it play. It is eleven seconds of the held tone of VH02, and nothing on
top of it. Another. VH05. "Good, the tape. Very clean."*

*He lays the waveforms on top of one another. They do not match, they are different
sentences. But in the quiet between the words — in the breaths the Producer takes, and in the quiet where he took none — all of them show
the same thing: a bar, thin, steady, low, below the speech, at exactly the same pitch.*

**SOUND GUY**
*(French; SUBTITLED: "He doesn't breathe in between. The room does.")*
Il ne respire pas entre les mots. C'est la pièce qui respire.

*Cam lowers the rig an inch.*

**CAM**
*(French; SUBTITLED: "What's the pitch?")*
Quelle est la hauteur ?

*The sound guy turns the screen toward her. The bar is labeled by the software, small:*

**ON SCREEN:**
```
7.83 Hz
```

**SOUND GUY**
*(French; SUBTITLED: "Not 28.7. That's the other one. That's — the horse's.")*
Pas 28,7. C'est l'autre. C'est — celui du cheval.

*He hears himself. The PA looks up from his clipboard, then at Cam. Nobody has mentioned a horse
in this series. The sound guy takes off his headphones as if they had told him.*

---

*The PA, who has not spoken, goes to the radio. A black field unit with a push-to-talk and a
telescoping antenna, sitting on an equipment case by the door. He presses the key.*

**PA**
*(French; SUBTITLED: "Producer. Can you say something?")*
Producteur. Vous pouvez dire quelque chose ?

*Nothing.*

**PA (CONT'D)**
*(French; SUBTITLED: "Anything. Say 'copy.'")*
N'importe quoi. Dites « reçu ».

*The speaker gives them room tone. It is the room tone that has been under every one of his
transmissions — the held breath, the bar below the scale. For the first time it is all that comes
through. No voice on top of it.*

**LOWER THIRD (DOC UI):**
```
CHANNEL 1: OPEN
SPEECH: NONE
CARRIER: PRESENT
```

*The PA lowers the key and stays very still.*

**PA (CONT'D)**
*(French; SUBTITLED, not into the radio: "He was never talking. We were hearing him because we
needed somebody to be.")*
Il n'a jamais parlé. On l'entendait parce qu'il fallait que quelqu'un le fasse.

*Cam says nothing. The sound guy puts a hand flat on the equipment case, as one steadies a
bucking animal.*

---

*After eleven seconds of carrier, the radio speaks. It is not the Producer's voice. It is not
anyone's. It is a woman's voice reading a number, flat, the way a station announces time:*

**RADIO (unattributed, French; SUBTITLED: "Day three hundred sixty-five. Zero hours.")**
Jour trois cent soixante-cinq. Zéro heure.

*That's all. The carrier resumes.*

*The PA looks at the day number written, in his own hand, on the back of his clipboard — the one he
turned over in VH03 so nobody could see it. He does not turn it over now, either.*

**LOWER THIRD (DOC UI), final card:**
```
THE PRODUCER IS OFF AIR
THE DAY IS NOT
```

*SMASH TO BLACK.*

*END COLD OPEN.*
