## SCENE ONE — THE QUESTION NOBODY WANTED

```
INT. THE GARAGE, CHROME EVERYWHERE, SAME ONE AS ALWAYS — CONTINUOUS
```

Doc Wheel is holding two identical van keys and cannot remember which one starts the van and
which one starts the thing they agreed never to start again.

**DOC WHEEL (TEXT CARD)**
One of these is the van. I need someone who knows things to tell me which.

**TYLER**
Try both.

**DOC WHEEL (TEXT CARD)**
I tried both in a dream once. I'm not doing it awake.

## SCENE TWO — THE INSTINCT, WRONG ON ARRIVAL

Duck, immediately, without polling anyone:

**DUCK**
Ask the Tree. Its whole thing is "keeper of the universe's greatest secret." A van key is
practically beneath it.

The Tree turns, interested, and delivers what is clearly a complete and confident answer, at
length, in French. Nobody in the garage reacts. The UI overlay does not generate a subtitle. It
has never generated one for the Tree. This is not a glitch. This has always been true.

**GHOST (TEXT CARD, still at the piano)**
And that's why we don't ask the Tree.

**TYLER**
That wasn't a no. That might have been the entire answer.

**GHOST (TEXT CARD)**
Doesn't matter what it was. Matters what we can do with it, which is nothing.

## SCENE THREE — THE HOUSE RULE, STATED FOR THE FIRST TIME ON CAMERA

**PIZZA (TEXT CARD)**
There's a rule. Has been for a while. "Ask the Frog, not the Tree." Nobody ever filmed the reason,
we just all started doing it.

**TYLER**
Say the reason now, then. On camera.

**PIZZA (TEXT CARD)**
The Tree's secret is bigger. The Frog's secret is usable. Different things.

## SCENE FOUR — THE FROG, CONSULTED PROPERLY

The Frog, top hat as ever, is asked directly this time — not baited into volunteering, per house
rule, apparently a whole separate piece of etiquette nobody explains either.

**TYLER**
Which key.

**FROG (TEXT CARD)**
I know the secret to time travel. A van key is not a mystery, it's an inconvenience.

**FROG (TEXT CARD, one beat later, holding up one finger)**
That one. It's warmer. Keys that get used remember it.

Doc Wheel checks. It is, in fact, the van key.

**DOC WHEEL (TEXT CARD)**
...Huh.

## SCENE FIVE — THE UNICORN ASKS THE OBVIOUS FOLLOW-UP

**UNICORN (TEXT CARD)**
Wait. If the Tree's secret is bigger, why does asking the Frog actually work and asking the Tree
doesn't?

**DUCK**
Because a secret you can't get out of someone isn't information. It's just a shape they're making.

**FROG (TEXT CARD)**
I've been saying variations of that for as long as I've had the hat.

**GHOST (TEXT CARD)**
The Tree's ability is *called* "Grand Secret." Mine's just called "Mid-Piano," and you can at
least use mine to figure out where I am at any given moment. Some of us named our own limitations
honestly.

The Tree, unbothered, says something else in French. Still no subtitle. It does not seem to be
trying to be understood this time, which the UI overlay treats as its own small confirmation.

## SCENE SIX — THE RETRIEVAL CART, ARRIVING ON SCHEDULE

The Retrieval Cart arrives, unbidden, carrying a small hand-lettered sign: **"ASK THE FROG."**
No one asks who made the sign or when. It is, per house rule, already the answer to a question
nobody finished asking.

**DOC WHEEL (TEXT CARD)**
Put it up by the van keys. Permanently, this time.

**TYLER**
"This time" implies a first time.

**DOC WHEEL (TEXT CARD)**
There's always a first time. I don't remember most of them.

## LORE RECEIPT — CUSTODY LOG, UNNUMBERED ENTRY

**Filed by:** Eastwind Owl Field Archive, cross-filed with RED GARDEN's own asset log
**Classification:** GANG HOUSE RULE, ORIGIN DRAMATIZED. No `docs/BACKSTORY.md` claim touched —
this entry sits after roster formation and after "The Band Name," invents nothing about origin,
custody, or recruitment order.
**Object of record:** The house rule "ask the Frog, not the Tree," dramatized here for the first
time on camera, drawn directly from both characters' real, already-shipped RED GARDEN kits
(`packages/simulation/arena_ai_bridge.c`) rather than invented fresh:

| Hero | Q | W | R |
|---|---|---|---|
| Frog | LOOP BACK (rewind own position + HP) | BORROWED TIME (refunds an ally's next cast) | THE SECRET (vanish, untargetable a while) |
| Tree | VINE LASH (root + damage nearest foe) | UNTRANSLATED (passive, no effect here) | GRAND SECRET (self-root, armor + heal) |

The joke was already sitting in the ability table before this episode existed: Tree's own W is
mechanically named **"UNTRANSLATED"** — a passive that does nothing when checked — the same
property this episode dramatizes as "speaks in French, never subtitled, delivers nothing anyone
can act on." Tree's R, **"GRAND SECRET,"** is bigger-sounding than Frog's R, **"THE SECRET,"** by
name alone, exactly matching Duck's wrong instinct in Scene Two that the bigger-sounding claim
should be the more useful one. Frog's actual kit (a self-rewind, a refund for someone else, a
vanish) is functionally interactive in a way Tree's kit (root, hit, heal, stay quiet) structurally
isn't — the house rule was already true in the game files. This episode is the first time anyone
said it out loud.

anyway, on the theory that a gang's unstated rules are exactly the kind of footage that makes it
