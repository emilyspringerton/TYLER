## COLD OPEN

```
INT. SERVER ROOM — ICELAND — NO YEAR GIVEN
```

*The room as VH04 left it. Seven folding chairs: six in an arc, one apart, facing the open door.
Four bowls, three empty, one full and steaming in front of the fifth chair. The pot is gone; the
inspector took it. He left the chairs. Cam has the rig on her shoulder and the line of light
from the doorway lies across the floor eleven feet from her boots.*

*The PA has taken a roll of gaffer tape from his belt and, without being told, put down a strip across
the floor, parallel to the doorway, a foot short of the light and straight in front of the seventh
chair. It is the most professional thing anyone has done in four episodes.*

**LOWER THIRD (DOC UI):**
```
TC  — / —  ·  10:58:12
```

**PA**
*(French; SUBTITLED: "Nobody goes past the tape.")*
Personne ne passe le ruban.

**SOUND GUY**
*(French; SUBTITLED: "Who made that rule?")*
C'est qui qui a fait cette règle ?

**PA**
*(French; SUBTITLED: "I did. Just now. It's the only rule we've got.")*
Moi. À l'instant. C'est la seule qu'on ait.

*Nobody argues. The sound guy tests the tape with the toe of his boot, the way a person tests ice.*

---

*The steam comes. Not from the bowls: from the full one, the hot one at the empty fifth chair, and
from the two thermoses by the dead rack. It rises, as steam does, and then, as it did in VH04, it
leans. It crosses the arc in one long ribbon toward the seventh chair, over the seventh chair,
toward the door.*

*It reaches the PA's tape. It stops at it. Exactly at it. Steam that has read the rule.*

*Cam's finger finds the zoom ring and does not turn it.*

**CAM**
*(French; SUBTITLED, barely: "It's waiting for us to cross.")*
Elle attend qu'on traverse.

**SOUND GUY**
*(French; SUBTITLED: "Or we're waiting for it to.")*
Ou c'est nous qui attendons qu'elle traverse.

*They look at each other. It is a long, honest look: the look of two people who have just discovered
that the standoff has no other party. Each side is waiting for the other to be the first to make
the day into a day.*

**PRODUCER (O.S., radio, light)**
*(French; SUBTITLED: "Good, the tape. Very clean. Don't cross it.")*
Bien, le ruban. Très propre. Ne le franchissez pas.

**CAM**
*(French; SUBTITLED, not turning: "You never gave us a rule before.")*
Vous ne nous avez jamais donné de règle avant.

*Silence on the radio — a quarter-second longer than any Producer silence on record, and not
the held quiet of VH04. This one has someone in it.*

**PRODUCER (O.S.)**
*(French; SUBTITLED: "It's not mine. I'm reading it off the wall.")*
Ce n'est pas la mienne. Je la lis sur le mur.

*The PA looks at the wall. The wall is bare cinderblock. He looks at it for a long moment and then,
very quietly, writes nothing.*

---

*The steam withdraws. Not fast. It draws back from the tape along the air the way water leaves a
shore, back across the arc, back to the bowls, and the room is still. The line of light is just
light again.*

*Nobody moves for eleven seconds. We count them on the timecode, whose seconds still run.*

*Then Cam lowers the lens a few degrees and finds what the steam left. On the sill of the open
door, at the exact center of the threshold, stands a thermos.*

*A third thermos. Green enamel, dented, a strip of masking tape on the side with no writing on it.
Not the inspector's: his coffee is on the floor by the rack, where he left it.*

**LOWER THIRD (DOC UI):**
```
OBJECTS OF RECORD: THERMOS (3)
```

**PA**
*(French; SUBTITLED: "That's on the other side of the tape.")*
C'est de l'autre côté du ruban.

**SOUND GUY**
*(French; SUBTITLED: "That's not a rule, that's a fact.")*
Ça, ce n'est pas une règle, c'est un fait.

*Cam keeps the lens on it. Steam rises from the mouth of the third thermos, thin and steady, in the
light from somewhere else. It smells — the sound guy's face tells us before anyone speaks — of
something warm and herbal. Not minestrone. Not coffee.*

**SOUND GUY**
*(French; SUBTITLED: "Tea.")*
Du thé.

*The steam from the tea does not lean toward the seventh chair. It goes straight up, as if it had
no one in particular to tell.*

*SMASH TO BLACK.*

*END COLD OPEN.*
