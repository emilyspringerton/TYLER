**PRODUCTION NOTE, READ BEFORE THE SCRIPT:**

This is the episode the block has been approaching. The figure at the doorway is not Tyler, and not the inspector (the long shadow of VH03 was his pot;
VH04 says so). It is the older/alternate Hana of Season 6 (Take 2), "with blood on her sleeve," whose
warning — "DON'T LET HIM START—" — has been cut off since S06b E1 and which Hana (the present one)
invoked in VH01 to talk Tyler out of reading the printout.

**What this script does with her:** the figure puts a hand on the doorframe. We see the sleeve. We
do not see a face (Hana's absence from the crew is kept for one more episode). She **finishes the
sentence** — and the finish is not what anyone assumed.

**Canon firewall:** the warning was *"don't let him start,"* and Tyler, in VH01, stopped. This
script inverts it. If he stopped the read by choice, the new warning is: **don't let him stop.**
The Bird Correction stays PENDING; the reason it is pending is the part that changes.

**Not resolved:** what "start" and "stop" refer to; where this Hana came from; the Bird Correction.

*The horse stands in the middle of the floor, facing the door. The crew arranged around her
without discussing it: Cam on her left, the PA at her head, the sound guy behind with the boom
low. Behind them the arc of six folding chairs, empty; in front of the horse, the seventh, set apart,
facing the door. They have been standing like this for a long time. The thermoses have been moved. The
tape has a hoofprint in it.*

**LOWER THIRD (DOC UI):**
```
TC  — / —  ·  14:46:30
```

*The light from the doorway dims. Not out. It dims like a lamp when someone steps in front of it.
The horse's ears go forward. Her nostrils flare. She does not move.*

*A hand comes around the doorframe.*

*It is a woman's right hand, spread flat against the wood, fingers white at the knuckle. The sleeve
of a field jacket, canvas, olive, pushed to the elbow. The cuff is stiff. From cuff to forearm,
the cloth is dark and dried: a long brown stain that has set into the weave.*

**LOWER THIRD (DOC UI):**
```
OBJECTS OF RECORD: SLEEVE (1) — BLOOD, DRIED — OWNER: UNLOGGED
```

*Nobody breathes. The PA's pen has fallen out of his hand and he has not noticed.*

**FIGURE (O.S., hoarse, French; SUBTITLED: "Don't film my face.")**
Ne filmez pas mon visage.

*Cam does not lower the camera. She lowers the frame: the lens drops from the doorframe to the
hand. Only the hand.*

**CAM**
*(French; SUBTITLED, very quietly: "Hana.")*
Hana.

*The hand tightens on the wood. There is a very long breath from the other side of the door.*

**FIGURE (O.S.)**
*(French; SUBTITLED: "Not yet. Not her. I'm the one who said it. Keep her bowl hot.")*
Pas encore. Pas elle. C'est moi qui l'ai dit. Gardez son bol chaud.

---

*The sound guy has his headphones on; he takes one cup off. His meter shows the bar below the scale
and, over it, for the first time, a second line, thin, rising.*

**ON SCREEN:**
```
7.83 Hz
28.7 Hz
```

*They are almost on top of each other. They are never quite the same. There is a space between
them that the display does not label.*

**FIGURE (O.S.)**
*(French; SUBTITLED: "I said 'don't let him start.' Remember that? Somebody cut it off.")*
J'ai dit « ne le laissez pas commencer ». Vous vous souvenez ? Quelqu'un l'a coupé.

**PA**
*(French; SUBTITLED, faintly: "Start what?")*
Commencer quoi ?

*The same question as S06b E2. The room's silence is the same silence. The figure lets it sit, the
way someone does who has watched it sit before.*

**FIGURE (O.S.)**
*(French; SUBTITLED: "I was wrong about the verb.")*
Je me suis trompée de verbe.

*The hand slides a few inches down the doorframe. The horse's head lowers, as if the hand had
touched her.*

**FIGURE (O.S., CONT'D)**
*(French; SUBTITLED: "He started already. I saw it. Everyone starts. It isn't the starting. It's
when he* stops. *When he puts it back in the printer. When he* chooses *not to read the end.")*
Il a déjà commencé. Je l'ai vu. Tout le monde commence. Ce n'est pas le début. C'est quand il
*s'arrête.* Quand il le remet dans l'imprimante. Quand il *choisit* de ne pas lire la fin.

*The camera does not move. In her viewfinder the hand, the sleeve, the stain.*

**FIGURE (O.S., CONT'D)**
*(French; SUBTITLED: "Don't let him stop.")*
Ne le laissez pas s'arrêter.

---

*The hand withdraws. Not quickly. The fingers slide off the wood one at a time, the way a person
lets go of a rail. The sleeve is gone. The light returns. The line on the floor is clean.*

*Nobody speaks. The horse lifts her head, turns it, and looks at Cam. It is, in the shape of a
horse's face, the look of someone saying: *well?**
