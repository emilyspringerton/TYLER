In VH07 the sound guy said "the horse's" about the 7.83 Hz tone and could not say where the word
came from. VH10 sends the horse. **Canon:** Tyler kept an Icelandic horse in Toledo, 1040–1051 CE,
having traveled from Iceland via Portugal (`EPISODES.md`, Love Triangle #2, "Tyler / the Icelandic
horse / Dawud al-Muthanna's scholarly gaze").

The horse is not Tyler. Tyler is not in this script. The horse is simply, unremarkably, in the room.

**Writer's Room Rule 6** is the whole episode: *one impossible detail, treated as unremarkable.*

## COLD OPEN

```
INT. SERVER ROOM — ICELAND — NO YEAR GIVEN
```

*Cam has her back to the door, filming the printer. The PA is crouched beside the tape, reading
nothing. The sound guy is wearing headphones, eyes closed, listening to the bar below the scale.*

**LOWER THIRD (DOC UI):**
```
TC  — / —  ·  13:31:15
```

*Behind them, without a sound — the sound guy would have heard it — a hoof comes down on the
concrete.*

*Cam turns. The PA turns. The sound guy opens his eyes.*

*In the doorway, one step short of the PA's tape, stands an Icelandic horse. Small, dun, thick
winter coat, a long forelock that hides one eye. Her breath is a cloud in the warm room. She is
standing exactly where the third thermos stood. The thermos has moved. It is now beside her left
forefoot, upright, unspilled.*

**LOWER THIRD (DOC UI):**
```
OBJECTS OF RECORD: HORSE (1) — ICELANDIC — UNLABELED
```

*Nobody speaks for a while.*

**SOUND GUY**
*(French; SUBTITLED: "Oh. Hello.")*
Ah. Bonjour.

*The horse regards him with the one eye she has. He takes off his headphones, politely.*

**PA**
*(French; SUBTITLED: "She's behind the tape.")*
Elle est derrière le ruban.

**CAM**
*(French; SUBTITLED: "She's in front of the tape. We're behind it.")*
Elle est devant le ruban. C'est nous qui sommes derrière.

*The PA looks down at the tape and at where the horse is relative to the tape. Concedes the point.*

---

*The horse takes one step. Her hoof lands on the tape. Nothing happens. She does not look down. She
walks past it — the first living thing to cross the PA's only rule — and the frame holds on the
tape, on the print of the hoof in the silver gaffer.*

*She crosses the floor of the server room at the speed of someone who has been here before. She
passes the seventh chair, the one set apart facing the door, and two steps on stops and looks back
at it, the way you look back at a place laid for someone you know. She does not sit; she is a
horse. She goes on to the dead rack and stops there. She lowers her head. She nudges, with her nose, the first thermos — the
minestrone.*

**SOUND GUY**
*(French; SUBTITLED, softly: "She wants it.")*
Elle en veut.

**PA**
*(French; SUBTITLED: "It's for somebody.")*
C'est pour quelqu'un.

**SOUND GUY**
*(French; SUBTITLED: "It's for her. Look at her.")*
C'est pour elle. Regarde-la.

*The PA looks. The horse is looking at the PA, in the way a horse looks at a person holding a thing
she would like to be held to her. He unscrews the cap. Pours a little minestrone into the lid. Sets
the lid on the floor.*

*The horse drinks it. Delicately, with the lip. A horse drinking soup.*

**LOWER THIRD (DOC UI):**
```
7.83 Hz — PRESENT (SOURCE: HORSE)
```

*The sound guy checks his meter. He looks at the horse. He looks at the meter. He puts a hand over
his mouth, not out of surprise but to keep from laughing.*

**SOUND GUY**
*(French; SUBTITLED: "It's her. She's the hum. It was never in the walls.")*
C'est elle. C'est elle le bourdonnement. Ça n'a jamais été dans les murs.

---

*Cam lowers the camera slowly to the horse's eye line, a sort of courtesy. The horse, lid empty,
lifts her head and looks into the lens. For a moment the whole frame is her one visible eye, large
and dark, and the red record light reflected in it.*

*Then she turns her head and looks back at the open door. It is the first time anyone in the room
has looked toward the door as if toward someone. Her ears go forward.*

**CAM**
*(French; SUBTITLED: "She's waiting.")*
Elle attend.

**PA**
*(French; SUBTITLED: "For who?")*
Qui ?

*Cam doesn't answer. She isn't going to say the name, and the PA, who has a page in his pocket,
doesn't ask again.*

**LOWER THIRD (DOC UI), final card:**
```
THE HORSE CROSSED THE TAPE
NOTHING HAPPENED
THE HORSE IS WAITING FOR SOMEONE
```

*SMASH TO BLACK.*

*END COLD OPEN.*
