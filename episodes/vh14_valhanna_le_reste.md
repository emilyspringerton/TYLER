# TYLER — VALHANNA, EPISODE 14 — COLD OPEN
## "Le Reste" ("The Remainder")
### Build 0152 | Einhorn Media | VALHANNA, continued

---

**PRODUCTION NOTE, READ BEFORE THE SCRIPT:**

VH04 introduced the Auditor (the Convener of the Uncompromised; Senior Grid Compliance Inspector,
Regional Energy Authority; **name field blank, on principle**) and left him with one gloss on the
28.7 Hz tone: *"Not a noise. A remainder. It takes two bodies in the room."* He did not say a
remainder *of what.* VH14 is his second visit, and it is the shortest piece of science in the
series: he comes back to measure the remainder properly.

**He is the ally who hands you a gadget instead of a speech** (`characters/the_auditor.md` §I). The
gadget is the **Gap Meter**, built overnight from his own lunchbox rig, and it does not measure a
frequency. It measures the *interval* between two of them. The battery that runs it is the one he
lent Cam in VH04, labeled `JEUDI`, "not a spare," due Thursday. It is not Thursday. It is not any
day. He lets her keep it, and says why.

**Continuity carried from VH04 and VH05:** the PA's tape is on the floor (VH05) and the Auditor,
who has never needed anyone's permission to be polite, steps over it with an *excusez-moi.* He is
the second living thing to cross it after the horse of VH10. Seven chairs; the seventh facing the
door. The soup is his recipe, nine of ten.

**Not resolved:** the tenth ingredient; his name; whose seventh chair; Entry 23; the Bird
Correction (PENDING).

## COLD OPEN

```
INT. SERVER ROOM — ICELAND — NO YEAR GIVEN
```

*Afternoon, as far as the tubes are concerned. The crew sits in the arc of folding chairs; Hana in
the fifth, the bowl in her lap, empty now. The horse stands near the seventh chair. The PA's tape
has a hoofprint in it. The line of light lies across the floor.*

**LOWER THIRD (DOC UI):**
```
TC  — / —  ·  16:05:22
```

*Three raps on the open door. Not on the wood; on the air, with the flat of a knuckle, as a man
knocks on a doorframe that has no door. Hana looks up first.*

*The Auditor comes through sideways. No pot this time. Over one shoulder, the canvas satchel; in his
free hand, held above his head like a lantern, a flat brass instrument the size of a hardcover
book, coil of fine wire where the glass would be. The enameled pin on his shirt pocket: the
circuit-flower. He steps over the PA's tape without looking down.*

**THE AUDITOR**
*(French; SUBTITLED: "Excuse me. Don't get up. Routine.")*
Excusez-moi. Ne vous levez pas. Routine.

*Nobody was getting up. He beams at all of them, and at Hana a half-second longer than the rest.*

**THE AUDITOR (CONT'D)**
*(French; SUBTITLED: "Ah. The fifth chair is occupied. I'm glad.")*
Ah. La cinquième chaise est occupée. Tant mieux.

---

*He crosses to the sound guy. Of everyone in the room, he picks the one who actually listens, and
holds out the brass instrument on both palms, the way one hands over something that has just been
set.*

**THE AUDITOR (CONT'D)**
*(French; SUBTITLED: "Yesterday I said 'a remainder.' That was lazy. Here is the rest of the
sentence.")*
Hier, j'ai dit « un reste ». C'était paresseux. Voici la fin de la phrase.

*The sound guy takes it. It is warm. There is a battery well on the back, empty, and in it, as
Cam stands and crosses without being asked, a squat black cell with masking tape and one word
written on it in ballpoint. She slots it in. The dial swings off its pin.*

**LOWER THIRD (DOC UI):**
```
GADGET: GAP METER (28.7 ⟷ 7.83)
BATTERY: JEUDI (NOT DUE)
READING: 20.87 HZ
```

**THE AUDITOR (CONT'D)**
*(French; SUBTITLED: "It doesn't measure a frequency. Anybody can measure a frequency. It measures
how far apart two of them are standing.")*
Il ne mesure pas une fréquence. N'importe qui peut mesurer une fréquence. Il mesure l'écart entre
deux d'entre elles.

**SOUND GUY**
*(French; SUBTITLED: "Twenty point eight seven.")*
Vingt virgule quatre-vingt-sept.

**THE AUDITOR**
*(French; SUBTITLED: "Twenty-eight point seven is what's left when you take the hum out of the room.
The hum is yours." — to the horse — "Madame.")*
Vingt-huit virgule sept, c'est ce qui reste quand on retire le bourdonnement de la pièce. Le
bourdonnement est à vous. Madame.

*The horse's ear turns toward him. He sketches a small bow.*

**THE AUDITOR (CONT'D)**
*(French; SUBTITLED: "So the remainder is the room minus the one who hums. It takes two bodies. One
of them is not in it yet.")*
Donc le reste, c'est la pièce moins celui qui fredonne. Il faut deux corps. L'un n'y est pas encore.

*Nobody does the subtraction out loud. The PA's pen is not moving.*

---

*The Auditor turns to the seventh chair — set apart, squared to the floor tiles with the side of his
shoe in VH04 — and looks at it the way a man looks at a place he has laid for someone and is
beginning to be a little worried about. It is still facing the door.*

**THE AUDITOR (CONT'D)**
*(French; SUBTITLED, softer: "When that needle reaches zero, somebody has chosen. Not me. Not you.
Whoever the chair is for.")*
Quand l'aiguille atteindra zéro, quelqu'un aura choisi. Pas moi. Pas vous. Celui pour qui est la
chaise.

*He lifts a hand. He needs no more of the sentence.*

**CAM**
*(French; SUBTITLED, holding out the empty battery sleeve: "It's your battery. It's due Thursday.")*
C'est votre pile. Elle est due jeudi.

**THE AUDITOR**
*(French; SUBTITLED: "It is not Thursday. It is not anything. Keep it. A thing that is not due is a
thing that is yours.")*
Ce n'est pas jeudi. Ce n'est rien. Gardez-la. Ce qui n'est pas dû vous appartient.

*Cam closes her hand around the sleeve and does not say thank you. She nods. He nods back. It is an
entire contract.*

---

*He goes to the first thermos by the dead rack — the soup — unscrews the cap, and, as in VH04,
takes a thimbleful. He tastes. His face passes through three expressions in a second and ends on
one neither the PA nor the sound guy has seen on him: not worry, not delight. Recognition.*

**THE AUDITOR (CONT'D)**
*(French; SUBTITLED, mostly to himself: "It changed. Yesterday it was nine. Today there are ten and
I still didn't put it there.")*
Ça a changé. Hier, c'était neuf. Aujourd'hui il y en a dix et ce n'est toujours pas moi.

**PA**
*(French; SUBTITLED: "Who is it?")*
Qui est-ce ?

**THE AUDITOR**
*(French; SUBTITLED: "I'd hire them. They add it after I leave and they never taste first.")*
Je l'embaucherais. Ils l'ajoutent après mon départ et ils ne goûtent jamais d'abord.

*He screws the cap back on, pats it twice like a flank, and heads for the door. He steps over the
tape the same way. He stops with a hand on the frame.*

**THE AUDITOR (CONT'D)**
*(French; SUBTITLED: "Eat it. It's good. It was good before this room.")*
Mangez-la. Elle est bonne. Elle l'était avant cette pièce.

*He is gone. The line of light is just light.*

**LOWER THIRD (DOC UI), final card:**
```
THE REMAINDER IS THE ROOM MINUS THE ONE WHO HUMS
THE SOUP HAS TEN INGREDIENTS NOW
NOBODY SAW WHO ADDED IT
```

*SMASH TO BLACK.*

*END COLD OPEN.*
