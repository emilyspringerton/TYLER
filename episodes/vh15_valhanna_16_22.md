**PRODUCTION NOTE, READ BEFORE THE SCRIPT:**

For thirteen episodes the timecode has run in a room whose date is blank. VH15 is the minute at
which the timecode and the door agree. **16:22** is the canonical minute of the natal chart that
Tyler and Emily Springerton share (`EPISODES.md`, Series X notes: *"Feb 5, 1988, 4:22 PM EST, Foote
Hospital Jackson MI"*) and the root-node site `JACKSON-BIRTH-001` outputs a three-frequency
signal: **28.7 / 3.33 / 7.83 Hz.**

**What this script shows and does not show:** the door shows a window. It does not name a place.
The crew never says "Jackson" or "1988." The viewer who has read `EPISODES.md` will know; the crew
only knows what the room is doing. **Rule 21:** nothing resembling the transit stop appears.

**The Gap Meter** (VH14) reads 20.87 Hz when the episode begins. It reads something else at the
end, and the episode does not say what the change means.

**Not resolved:** the place; the person on the bed; Hana(s); the Bird Correction (PENDING).

*The doorway, on camera, is no longer a doorway onto light. It is a doorway onto a room.*

*We see it from the side, through the slant of the lens: a narrow room with a window, grey-gold,
snow on the sill. A rolling cart. A wall clock above a door, white face, black hands. The
hands read 4:21. They tick, in the sound guy's headphones, a half-second late. Every object has the
dull sheen of an old fluorescent hallway. There are cars in a lot beyond the glass whose
shapes the PA's eye identifies and the PA's mouth does not.*

*We do not see a bed. We do not see a person. We hear, very faintly, from somewhere near the
window, a sound that is not language. Someone at the beginning of breathing.*

*Nobody says where it is. The word is not said, because the PA, who has the habit of saying every

**HANA**
*(French; SUBTITLED, a whisper: "Don't film the bed.")*
Ne filme pas le lit.

**CAM**
*(French; SUBTITLED, a whisper: "I'm not.")*

*She is filming the clock. She holds on it. The hands tick toward the twenty-second minute.*

*At 4:22 on the clock in the other room, at 16:22:00 on the burned-in timecode — the first time in
the series that the two agree — the three tones lock into a chord. The sound guy takes off his
headphones. Not because it is loud. Because it is too much like a held note to wear.*

**LOWER THIRD (DOC UI):**
```
TC  — / —  ·  16:22:00
DOOR TIME  16:22:00
GAP METER: 20.87 → 4.50 → 25.37
```

*The Gap Meter's needle is trying to read three things at once and cannot. It sweeps, lands on
none, and settles back.*

*Behind them, on the floor, the horse lifts her head, then her whole body, stands. She walks to
the PA's tape and stands with her chest against it. She is looking at the window in the other room.*

*Hana puts a hand over her mouth.*

*For one minute the cold open holds this: a window, a clock, a chord, a horse, and four people who
do not say what they are looking at. At 16:23:00 the light is a line on the floor again. The
doorway is a doorway.*

**LOWER THIRD (DOC UI), final card:**
