# TYLER — VALHANNA, EPISODE 31 — COLD OPEN
## "Neuf sur Dix" ("Nine of Ten")
### Build 0169 | Einhorn Media | VALHANNA, continued — MOON 1, DAY 7

---

**PRODUCTION NOTE, READ BEFORE THE SCRIPT:**

Saturday is Saturn's day (Zagan #23, 4.44 Hz, *transmutation*; "lead-to-gold moments, literal or
metaphoric," `README.md` §XII). The week has been long and a little frightening. This episode is the
week's exhale, and it is a **cooking show.**

**Form.** *Les Recettes de l'Unité A.* The PA is the host; the sound guy is the co-host; Cam is
the camera and, as ever, not in the shot; the Duck is the judge; Tyler is the guest, who has been
forbidden to touch the pot; the horse is the studio audience; and **the Producer is the announcer,
who may only ask questions**, which is the funniest thing about him and has been the whole time. The
Doc UI prints ingredient chyrons.

**What the episode is for.** It runs the Auditor's recipe card (`lore/folding_chair_roster_
receipt.md`) the way a unit runs a thing it loves: with grave attention and no authority. It
tries, in a comic series of experiments, to *find* the tenth ingredient — and finds out that it
can't be found, only given. It also quietly resolves one small mystery from VH04: the card's
pencilled margin note, *"The seventh was set facing the door. Not my habit. Noted."* **Who set it.**

**Not resolved:** the tenth ingredient (which, by the end of this episode, the unit has understood
cannot be solved by them); the sheet in the tray; the Gap's meaning.

*Cam has made a small sign with gaffer tape on the back of a call sheet. It reads APPLAUSE. She holds it
at the edge of the frame. The horse stamps once. It counts.*

**PRODUCER (O.S., announcer's voice; the same voice, a half-step brighter)**
*(French; SUBTITLED: "Tonight on The Recipes of Unit A: could it be soup? Could one make it without the
inspector? Why not?")*
Ce soir dans Les Recettes de l'Unité A : serait-ce de la soupe ? Pourrait-on en faire sans l'inspecteur ?
Pourquoi pas ?

*The PA is behind a prep table made of two equipment cases and a door, in an apron he has made out
of the clipboard and a length of cable. He has a knife. He has never held a knife with intent.*

**PA**
*(French; SUBTITLED, to the lens, perfectly calm: "Good evening. I am a production assistant, and I have
never cooked.")*
Bonsoir. Je suis assistant de production, et je n'ai jamais cuisiné.

**SOUND GUY (co-host, headphones around his neck, a wooden spoon held like a microphone)**
*(French; SUBTITLED: "And I'm the co-host, which means I'm the one who tastes.")*
Et moi je suis le co-animateur, c'est-à-dire que c'est moi qui goûte.

*Applause. The horse.*

---

*Hana is at the end of the table with the recipe card in both hands: the laminated one, ten by fifteen
centimeters, the strip of masking tape down one edge with the one word on it. She reads it aloud in
the voice of a woman reading out a will.*

**HANA**
*(French; SUBTITLED: "Minestrone. The large pot. One.")*
Minestrone. La grande marmite. Un.

**LOWER THIRD (DOC UI), one chyron per ingredient, in the cooking show's borrowed type:**
```
1/10  OIGNON
```

*The PA cuts an onion. It goes badly. The sound guy hands him a tissue without being asked.*

**HANA**
*(French; SUBTITLED: "Two. Celery. Three. Carrot.")*
Deux. Céleri. Trois. Carotte.

```
2/10  CÉLERI
3/10  CAROTTE
```

*In the corner of the room, forbidden the pot by a sign taped to the floor that reads (in the PA's hand)
INTERDIT AU SIÈGE SEPT, Tyler is peeling carrots. It is not the pot. It is a pile of carrots on the
floor beside a bucket, a paring knife, a stool. He has been given a task so as not to be given none.*

*He is very good at it. The peel comes off in a single ribbon, turning, never breaking, like the
inside of a thought. He does not look at his hands. He has done this at a hundred tables.*

**LOWER THIRD (DOC UI):**
```
TELL (HANDS): ABSENT
```

*Cam, at the camera, holds that shot of his hands for slightly longer than the format requires.*

**DUCK (judge's chair, a high stool, the white thermos tucked under one wing)**
*(English)*
I want it noted that he's very good with a carrot.

**PA**
*(French; SUBTITLED: "It was noted.")*
C'est noté.

---

*Hana, reading on.*

**HANA**
*(French; SUBTITLED: "Four. White beans — soak, do not shortcut. Five. Tinned tomato. Six. Cabbage, the
dark kind.")*
Quatre. Haricots blancs : tremper, ne pas raccourcir. Cinq. Tomate en conserve. Six. Chou, le foncé.

```
4/10  HARICOTS BLANCS (TREMPER)
5/10  TOMATE
6/10  CHOU
```

*A montage in the grammar of the genre, with one difference: the music is the sound guy humming
along to the tubes. The beans have been soaking since yesterday in the bucket Tyler is not
allowed to touch. The tomatoes go in a way that sounds like surf. The cabbage goes in dark.*

**HANA**
*(French; SUBTITLED: "Seven. Small pasta, added late. Eight. Olive oil, more than you think. Nine. The
rind of the hard cheese.")*
Sept. Petites pâtes, à ajouter tard. Huit. Huile d'olive, plus que tu ne crois. Neuf. La croûte du
fromage dur.

```
7/10  PÂTES (TARD)
8/10  HUILE D'OLIVE
9/10  CROÛTE DE FROMAGE
```

*She stops. She turns the card over. The line after the nine is a numeral and a full stop, and
nothing.*

**HANA**
*(French; SUBTITLED: "Ten.")*
Dix.

```
10/10  ——
```

---

*The PA puts down the knife. The sound guy lowers the spoon. The whole studio, the stage-lit room, the
applause sign, the audience of one horse, holds still. It is the moment in the program where the
host says, to the audience: "and now, for the secret ingredient."*

**PA**
*(French; SUBTITLED, to the lens: "And now, for the secret ingredient.")*
Et maintenant, pour l'ingrédient secret.

*He has no idea what it is. Nobody has. They are going to find it.*

**PRODUCER (O.S.)**
*(French; SUBTITLED: "Could it be tea? Might I contribute?")*
Serait-ce du thé ? Puis-je contribuer ?

*A teaspoon of green tea, from the thermos on the fourth chair, goes into the pot. The sound guy
tastes it. His face moves through the whole of the day.*

**SOUND GUY**
*(French; SUBTITLED: "Green. No.")*
Vert. Non.

**DUCK**
*(English)*
It tastes like a bush.

*Next. A teaspoon of black coffee from the thermos by the rack. The sound guy tastes it.*

**SOUND GUY**
*(French; SUBTITLED: "Worse.")*
Pire.

*Next. Honey water from the flask in Hana's coat: she does this herself, and watches him taste.*

**SOUND GUY**
*(French; SUBTITLED: "Sweet. Wrong.")*
Sucré. Faux.

*Next. The PA shaves a little graphite from his pencil into the spoon. Everyone looks at him. He
looks at the spoon. He puts it down.*

**PA**
*(French; SUBTITLED, lofty: "I withdraw that.")*
Je retire ça.

*Next. A single hair from the horse's mane, which Cam, off-camera, is understood to have asked for
with her hands. The horse looks at the spoon. She looks at Cam. She does not stamp. She walks to the
far end of the room and stands with her back to the show.*

**DUCK**
*(English)*
She declines.

---

*The experiments go on. A drop of the Gap Meter's brass polish, withdrawn by the sound guy before it
reaches the pot. The folded call sheet from the first day, the DAY 365 row, blank — Hana's hand
closes on it; "Non," she says; the PA puts it back in his pocket. A grain of salt from the cellar
Tyler put in the center of Friday's table. The Duck, looking at the pot with great judicial
attention, declares:*

**DUCK**
*(English)*
I've seen soup go bad in this town. I know what it looks like. This isn't that.

**PA**
*(French; SUBTITLED: "Then what is it?")*
Alors c'est quoi ?

**DUCK**
*(English)*
It's good. It's flat.

*Hana looks at the card. She looks at the pot. Zagan is the transmuter, Saturn's spirit, lead into
gold; it is the only line in the table of the day. She says it dryly to the lens, as a joke about
the program's own premise:*

**HANA**
*(French; SUBTITLED: "Zagan. Four point four four. Transmutation.")*
Zagan. Quatre virgule quatre-vingt-quatre. Transmutation.

**DUCK**
*(English)*
I was promised alchemy.

**SOUND GUY**
*(French; SUBTITLED: "We made soup.")*
On a fait de la soupe.

**DUCK**
*(English)*
In this town that's the same thing.

---

*Applause. The horse, from the end of the room, without turning: one stamp.*

*Hana turns the card over once more. It is the back this time, the strip of masking tape down the
side, the tally marks, the word in ballpoint. There is a margin note in pencil along the bottom
edge. She reads it aloud, slowly, because she has not noticed it before.*

**HANA**
*(French; SUBTITLED: "'The seventh was set facing the door. Not my habit. Noted.'")*
« La septième était posée face à la porte. Pas dans mes habitudes. Noté. »

*She looks up. Not at the pot. At the corner, where a man is peeling a carrot.*

**HANA (CONT'D)**
*(French; SUBTITLED: "That was you.")*
C'était toi.

*The ribbon of peel stops, mid-turn.*

**TYLER**
*(French; SUBTITLED, thinking about it: "I may have asked.")*
J'ai peut-être demandé.

**HANA**
*(French; SUBTITLED: "Asked whom?")*
À qui ?

**TYLER**
*(French; SUBTITLED, mildly: "Later.")*
À plus tard.

**SOUND GUY**
*(French; SUBTITLED: "You set the chair from later?")*
Tu as posé la chaise depuis plus tard ?

**TYLER**
*(French; SUBTITLED: "Politely.")*
Poliment.

*He resumes peeling. It is the end of the explanation. In the corner of the shot, the Producer's steam
has stopped moving; the PA, who has understood something and does not wish to be seen having
understood, writes in the clipboard, once, and puts a box around it.*

---

**SOUND GUY**
*(French; SUBTITLED, at the pot, to himself, to the lens, to everyone: "You can't find the tenth. You
can only be given it.")*
On ne trouve pas le dixième. On nous le donne.

*Nobody answers. The pot is put away to be ready for tomorrow. The horse has returned to the
studio. The sound guy puts the wooden spoon back in the apron the PA made out of cable.*
