## COLD OPEN

```
INT. SERVER ROOM — ICELAND — NO YEAR GIVEN
```

*The camera is on a tripod for the first time. Cam has set it on the floor, wide, the doorway
visible at the edge of frame. She and the PA sit in two of the folding chairs, either side of the tripod. They
are in the shot. The record is running. She is a subject.*

**LOWER THIRD (DOC UI):**
```
TC  — / —  ·  12:07:51
SUBJECTS IN FRAME: 2
```

*Between them, on the seat of a third folding chair, lies the notebook. Small, cloth cover, the corner worn soft. Cam
has not touched it. The PA is looking at it the way you look at a letter you opened by mistake.*

**PA**
*(French; SUBTITLED: "You don't have to.")*
Tu n'es pas obligée.

**CAM**
*(French; SUBTITLED: "It isn't about having to. The inspector said sealing is choosing.")*
Ce n'est pas une question d'obligation. L'inspecteur a dit que sceller, c'est choisir.

**PA**
*(French; SUBTITLED: "I know what's on my page.")*
Je sais ce qu'il y a sur ma page.

**CAM**
*(French; SUBTITLED: "I know you do. You folded it into a pocket it doesn't fit in.")*
Je sais. Tu l'as plié dans une poche où ça ne rentre pas.

*A beat. He almost smiles. The sound guy, out of frame, drops a coil of cable and picks it up
again with elaborate care.*

---

*Cam picks up the notebook. Opens it, past all the other pages, to the one that was crossed out
once and rewritten. We do not see it. The camera behind her is on the tripod, wide. We see the
back of the book and Cam's face over it.*

**CAM (CONT'D)**
*(French; SUBTITLED: "I'm going to read it to you. Then you tell me if it's true.")*
Je vais te le lire. Après, tu me dis si c'est vrai.

*She reads. We see her lips move. And the sound goes away: not to silence. To room tone, held,
7.83 beneath it, the bar below the scale. The way the Producer's audio was lifted out in VH02.*

*She reads one line. We can tell from her breath: a single exhale, a few syllables' worth of mouth,
the shorter line of VH02. She closes the notebook.*

**LOWER THIRD (DOC UI):**
```
AUDIO: 1 LINE WITHHELD (BY WHOM: CAM)
```

*The UI has never said "by whom" and named the person in the frame. It does now.*

---

*The PA sits very still. He does not say whether it's true. He reaches into his back pocket, takes
out the folded invoice, flattens it on his knee. He doesn't unfold it all the way. He turns it so the
back side faces the camera: blank. And then he takes his pen, the cheap one from the clipboard,
and writes one word on the blank side.*

*The camera is on the tripod. It is wide. We see the pen move. We see the writing only as shape:
a single line, short, shorter than hers.*

*He turns it back around, folds it, puts it in the pocket where it doesn't fit.*

**PA**
*(French; SUBTITLED: "It's true.")*
C'est vrai.

**CAM**
*(French; SUBTITLED: "Both?")*
Les deux ?

**PA**
*(French; SUBTITLED: "Yes. Both.")*
Oui. Les deux.

*They sit. The sound guy, out of frame, has stopped winding the cable. He is simply holding it.*

---

*Cam stands, takes the camera off the tripod, and returns it to her shoulder. The frame goes back
to being hers. The PA looks at where the tripod stood and says nothing.*

**LOWER THIRD (DOC UI), final card:**
```
SEALED LOG // ENTRY 23 (VH) // [READ ALOUD — 1 LINE — NOT RELEASED]
CREW ENTRY // PA-001 // [WRITTEN — 1 WORD — NOT RELEASED]
```

*SMASH TO BLACK.*

*END COLD OPEN.*
