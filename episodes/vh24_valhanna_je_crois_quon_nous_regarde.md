## COLD OPEN

```
INT. SERVER ROOM — ICELAND — NO YEAR GIVEN
```

*Early. The light is the plain light of the tubes, and no other. The doorway is a doorway, a
rectangle of corridor beyond. The crew is awake, on their feet, in no formation. The tape is still
on the floor, silver, with a hoofprint. The seven chairs are where the inspector left them, the seventh facing the door. The thermoses stand in a
row, with Hana's. A line of crew cups. The sheet from the printer lies in the tray where it fell. Nobody has read it.*

**LOWER THIRD (DOC UI):**
```
TC  01 / 01  ·  06:20:14
```

*The sound guy sees it first.*

**SOUND GUY**
*(French; SUBTITLED: "It has a date.")*
Elle a une date.

*They all look. The first moon, the first day. The date field is populated. The blank is gone.*

**PA**
*(French; SUBTITLED, small: "Where did the day go?")*
Où est passé le jour ?

**HANA**
*(French; SUBTITLED: "We spent it.")*
On l'a dépensé.

---

*The wall. Cam kneels at the base of it with the lens. Rule 10 still blinks at the bottom of the
stencil. The crew gathers behind her. No one speaks. The cursor waits for the sentence.*

**CAM**
*(French; SUBTITLED: "I think I know it.")*
Je crois que je le connais.

**HANA**
*(French; SUBTITLED: "Say it. Don't write it yet.")*
Dis-le. Ne l'écris pas encore.

**CAM**
*(French; SUBTITLED: "Nobody reads the end for him.")*
Personne ne lit la fin à sa place.

*Silence. The PA says: "Yes." The sound guy: "Yes." Hana does not say it; she touches the wall
beside the cursor, and the cursor moves. Characters arrive, stencil-black, in the printer's face:*

**ON SCREEN:**
```
10. NOBODY READS THE END FOR HIM.
```

*The line holds. The cursor goes out. There is no eleventh line.*

**LOWER THIRD (DOC UI):**
```
RULE 10: DECIDED (THE CREW)
BIRD CORRECTION: PENDING — BY CHOICE: THE UNIT'S
```

---

*Behind them, a sound. The corridor door, the one nobody has used since Hana came through it. Hana
has already turned. Cam does not need to.*

*The Duck comes through. Tuxedo rumpled, collar up, a green enamel thermos held in both wings
against its chest as it might hold an egg. It stops just inside the door and looks at the room. At
the horse. At the row of thermoses. At the crew who have never seen it in person.*

**THE DUCK**
*(English)*
Ah. Good. The tape held.

*It walks the length of the room with absolute dignity, and sets its thermos at the end of the row,
beside Hana's. Straightens it. Steps back to look at the row, five thermoses, and nods.*

**THE DUCK (CONT'D)**
*(English)*
Somebody should put a lid on that soup.

*From the thermos on the sill — the tea, now back on the floor of the room — the Producer, small,
light, a half-step too slow:*

**PRODUCER (O.S.)**
*(French; SUBTITLED: "He's coming.")*
Il arrive.

---

*The corridor door again. This time it opens slowly, the way a door opens that the person on the
other side is not sure he is allowed to push.*

*TYLER comes in.*

*Rain on a canvas jacket. Boots that have been walking since a time that has no date. A look about
him of a man who has been awake for centuries and has just realized he may sleep. He stops in the
doorway. His eyes go to the camera.*

*He does not look away from it. He walks toward it.*

**LOWER THIRD (DOC UI):**
```
GAP METER: 0.00 HZ
```

*The sound guy looks down at the Gap Meter in his palm. The needle is at the pin, steady. Nobody
says: somebody has chosen.*

*The horse is the first to move. She crosses the floor in six strides and puts her forehead
against Tyler's chest, and he puts both hands in her mane, and 7.83 Hz fills the room, and no one
is looking at a meter.*

**TYLER**
*(French; SUBTITLED, into the mane: "Hello, you.")*
Bonjour, toi.

*He looks up. At the crew, one at a time. Cam, last, at the lens. He holds it. And then, in the same
quiet voice with which he said it in the Duck's dream, he turns the sentence around.*

**TYLER (CONT'D)**
*(French; SUBTITLED: "I think you're being watched too.")*
Je crois qu'on vous regarde aussi.

*Nobody breathes. Cam does not lower the camera.*

**CAM**
*(French; SUBTITLED, level: "We know. We're the ones watching.")*
On sait. C'est nous qui regardons.

*A beat, then Tyler laughs — a short, surprised, honest laugh, the first of its kind since the
inspector's. The PA is already holding out the green thermos, the tea, and Tyler has seen the
seventh chair: set apart, squared to the tiles, facing the other door, the one with the light in it. He looks at it for
a moment as a man looks at a seat that was laid for him before he knew he was coming. He takes the
thermos. He sits.*

**TYLER**
*(French; SUBTITLED, gently: "I'm the man who —")*
Je suis l'homme qui —

*He stops. His mouth stays shaped around the next word. Whatever it was, it does not come. The room
does not press him. The audio drops to room tone, 7.83 beneath it, the bar below the scale, held.*

THE TEA WAS FOR HIM
THE TENTH INGREDIENT: STILL NOT ACCOUNTED FOR
