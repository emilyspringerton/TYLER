# TYLER — VALHANNA, EPISODE 40 — COLD OPEN
## "Les Mains Vides" ("Empty Hands")
### Build 0178 | Einhorn Media | VALHANNA, continued — MOON 1, DAY 16

---

**PRODUCTION NOTE, READ BEFORE THE SCRIPT:**

Monday, the third time (Amon #7, 1.618 Hz, reconciliation of opposites). Minutes of the third meeting,
read by the PA. The agenda has one item and everyone knows what it is. The older Hana has sat on the
line for eleven days with one boot in the corridor. She asks to come in.

**What she brings** is the thing the unit has wanted to ask and has not. VH01: *"I'm looking at your
face and I already know what mine looks like in this room, later, with blood on the sleeve."* VH12:
*"Don't let him stop."* VH23: *"You'll know when you're me. You decided not to be."* She has not said,
in sixteen days, what happened. She says it now. She does not say why. **She does not know why.**
She was in the corridor.

**What she gives is a date and a small fact.** The date is on her wrist. The fact is two words
long, and it is the whole of the plan.

**Guard-rails.** Rule 21: nothing about the transit stop, no location of the event beyond "the
corridor." Rule 22: not said. Writer's Room Rule 6 (wall): *"Do not read past the present."* A guest
may tell; a document may not (the Duck's formulation). The wall keeps its sixth line. The eleventh
arrives.

**Not resolved:** the cause; whether she knew the order; what the unit does about it. The
sentence she cannot finish is "he left…"

**PA (V.O., reading the minutes)**
*(French; SUBTITLED: "MINUTES. Unit A, regular meeting number three. Moon one, day sixteen. Called to
order at ten. The Chair took the chair. The eighth chair was occupied. The eighth chair asked to
speak.")*
PROCÈS-VERBAL. Unité A, réunion ordinaire numéro trois. Lune un, jour seize. Ouverte à dix heures. La
présidente a pris la présidence. La huitième chaise était occupée. La huitième chaise a demandé la
parole.

*The older Hana is sitting where she has sat since the fifth day, her chair across the PA's tape, her
right boot in the corridor and her left in the room. She looks at the boot in the corridor.*

**OLDER HANA**
*(French; SUBTITLED: "I would like to come in.")*
Je voudrais entrer.

**HANA**
*(French; SUBTITLED: "You're in.")*
Tu es entrée.

**OLDER HANA**
*(French; SUBTITLED: "Not all of me.")*
Pas tout entière.

*Hana looks at the PA. The PA looks at the minutes. There is no line in them for this.*

**PA (V.O.)**
*(French; SUBTITLED: "It was moved that the eighth chair be moved six inches into the room. The motion was
seconded by the Duck.")*
Il a été proposé que la huitième chaise soit avancée de quinze centimètres dans la pièce. La motion a
été appuyée par le canard.

**DUCK**
*(English)*
Seconded. I'm still going to say it.

*It carries. The horse sneezes before the vote is called, which the minutes record as a contribution
to the debate. The older Hana stands. She takes the back of her chair in both hands, lifts it (it is light, it
folds), and sets it down six inches on the room's side of the strip of silver tape, and sits.*

*Both boots in the room. The Doc UI, for the first time, finds the number it had been looking for.*

**LOWER THIRD (DOC UI):**
```
OLDER HANA: INSIDE THE LINE
SUBJECT COUNT: 2 (HANA / HANA) — FRAMING: GRANTED
```

*Nothing happens. The tubes breathe. The tape is the same tape. There is a hoofprint in it and the print of a
chair-leg, and now the print of a woman's boot beside the other; and the room has the particular
feeling of a place in which something has just not gone wrong.*

---

**OLDER HANA**
*(French; SUBTITLED: "I brought something I can't say. I can only show it.")*
J'ai apporté quelque chose que je ne peux pas dire. Je peux seulement le montrer.

*She lays her left arm on the cloth. She turns back the cuff. The cuff is stiff with what dried on
it. She works it up her forearm in small stages, unbuttoning the one wooden button. Underneath, on
the inside of the wrist, against skin, is a watch.*

*A man's steel watch, too big for her, held on the wrist by a strap with an extra hole punched in
it with a knife. The glass is cracked across, from the six to the one. Under the crack the second hand does
not move. The hour hand is on six. The minute hand is a hair past it. The little window in the dial,
the date window, shows two numerals.*

**LOWER THIRD (DOC UI), zooming on the dial, the cold type:**
```
01 / 22   06:03:11
```

*It is a different watch from the ones the Syndicate's memos describe. It is not a Syndicate watch. It
is a hardware-store watch, a thing you'd buy at a counter on the way to a train. It is stopped at a time that
is neither high noon nor midnight but the odd, ordinary, defenseless hour of three minutes past six.*

**SOUND GUY**
*(French; SUBTITLED, barely: "May I?")*
Je peux ?

*She nods. He bends. He does not touch it. He holds the Gap Meter beside it, in the habit of a man who
brings an instrument to everything; the needle swings and settles at 14.91, as it has been all
morning, and does nothing at all.*

**OLDER HANA**
*(French; SUBTITLED: "This is when.")*
Voilà quand.

*The PA has his chart. The line from bottom-left to top-right. He has written, in pencil, at the far
right edge, a number. 22. His pencil, as he looks at the watch, stops.*

**PA**
*(French; SUBTITLED, almost inaudible: "It's the same day.")*
C'est le même jour.

---

**HANA**
*(French; SUBTITLED, very steady: "Tell us.")*
Dis-nous.

*The older Hana is silent for a while. She turns her sleeve down. She takes a breath of the kind
you take before you lift something.*

**OLDER HANA**
*(French; SUBTITLED: "He left on the twenty-second. At dawn. Through the corridor.")*
Il est parti le vingt-deux. À l'aube. Par le couloir.

*Nobody moves.*

**OLDER HANA (CONT'D)**
*(French; SUBTITLED: "The thermoses were cold.")*
Les thermos étaient froids.

*A beat in which the Producer's steam, on the fourth chair, shrinks an inch and comes back.*

**OLDER HANA (CONT'D)**
*(French; SUBTITLED: "We'd done the coffee last. The soup first.")*
On avait fait le café en dernier. La soupe en premier.

**CAM**
*(French; SUBTITLED, from behind the rig: "Was that wrong?")*
C'était une erreur ?

**OLDER HANA**
*(French; SUBTITLED, with a small, helpless shrug: "I don't know. It's what I remember.")*
Je ne sais pas. C'est ce dont je me souviens.

*She presses her thumb against the back of her other hand, once, as though to check a count.*

**OLDER HANA (CONT'D)**
*(French; SUBTITLED: "The corridor peeled.")*
Le couloir s'est décollé.

**SOUND GUY**
*(French; SUBTITLED: "Peeled?")*
Décollé ?

**OLDER HANA**
*(French; SUBTITLED: "Like a label. From the end of it. A line of light along the wall, the width of a
hand, and the edge of the wall came away from the wall. I took hold of the edge.")*
Comme une étiquette. Par le bout. Une ligne de lumière le long du mur, de la largeur d'une main, et le
bord du mur s'est détaché du mur. J'ai pris le bord.

*She does not look at her sleeve. She does not need to.*

**OLDER HANA (CONT'D)**
*(French; SUBTITLED: "It cut.")*
Ça a coupé.

*Not a sound in the room.*

**HANA**
*(French; SUBTITLED, quietly: "And he?")*
Et lui ?

**OLDER HANA**
*(French; SUBTITLED, with a long, slow, level voice, as one states a number one has stated to oneself a
very great many times: "He didn't come back.")*
Il n'est pas revenu.

*Tyler is sitting in the seventh chair. He has not moved. His hands lie on the cloth. His eyes are
on the older Hana, and there is no tell. There is simply attention.*

**OLDER HANA (CONT'D)**
*(French; SUBTITLED: "I'm what's left of the one who waited.")*
Je suis ce qui reste de celle qui a attendu.

---

*The PA, writing, writes slowly. He is a man writing the minutes of a thing that has not yet happened.
He cannot find the passive voice for it.*

**SOUND GUY**
*(French; SUBTITLED, low: "That's reading ahead.")*
Ça, c'est lire en avance.

*It is the wall's sixth line. He means it. He is also, plainly, hoping for an answer.*

**PA**
*(French; SUBTITLED, not looking up: "She isn't a document. She's a guest.")*
Ce n'est pas un document. C'est une invitée.

**DUCK**
*(English, drily)*
A guest may tell. A document may not.

*The sound guy looks at the Duck. He looks at the older Hana. He nods, once, a man accepting a
distinction he finds adequate.*

**HANA**
*(French; SUBTITLED, to her older self: "What did we do wrong?")*
Qu'est-ce qu'on a fait de mal ?

**OLDER HANA**
*(French; SUBTITLED: "I don't know why. I wasn't in the room. I was in the corridor. All I know is what I
saw when I got there.")*
Je ne sais pas pourquoi. Je n'étais pas dans la pièce. J'étais dans le couloir. Je sais seulement ce que j'ai vu
en arrivant.

**HANA**
*(French; SUBTITLED: "What did you see?")*
Qu'as-tu vu ?

*The older Hana opens her left hand on the cloth. It is empty. She looks at it. She turns it over, and
looks at the other side, and turns it back.*

**OLDER HANA**
*(French; SUBTITLED: "His hands. He had nothing in them.")*
Ses mains. Il n'avait rien dedans.

**OLDER HANA (CONT'D)**
*(French; SUBTITLED: "We let him leave with his hands empty.")*
On l'a laissé partir les mains vides.

**OLDER HANA (CONT'D)**
*(French; SUBTITLED, to Tyler, directly, for the first time since the door: "He had nothing to owe us.")*
Il n'avait rien à nous devoir.

---

*Tyler has his coat on the back of the seventh chair. His hands are on the cloth, open, nothing in them.
The unit is looking at them. He sees that they are, and looks down at them himself, and turns them
over, as she did.*

**TYLER**
*(French; SUBTITLED, mildly, to the cloth: "I don't promise.")*
Je ne promets pas.

**OLDER HANA**
*(French; SUBTITLED, gently, the gentlest thing she has said: "I know. That's why I'm not asking.")*
Je sais. C'est pour ça que je ne demande pas.

*She turns to the table.*

**OLDER HANA (CONT'D)**
*(French; SUBTITLED: "I'm not asking him to promise. I'm asking that he be lent something.")*
Je ne lui demande pas de promettre. Je demande qu'on lui prête quelque chose.

---

*The PA's pencil comes up. He writes it. It takes him a long time. When he lifts his head, he
is no longer a man writing minutes; he is the Secretary, who has a motion.*

**PA**
*(French; SUBTITLED: "I move that we lend him something.")*
Je propose qu'on lui prête quelque chose.

**DUCK**
*(English)*
Seconded.

**PA (V.O.)**
*(French; SUBTITLED: "The motion carried by acclamation. The horse sneezed. The corridor was inside the
line. The Secretary noted that he did not remember when he had last been so sure of a thing.")*
La motion est adoptée par acclamation. Le cheval a éternué. Le couloir était dans la ligne. Le Secrétaire
a noté qu'il ne se souvenait pas de la dernière fois où il avait été aussi sûr de quelque chose.

*On the north wall, behind the racks, the black ink comes up through the paint, a stroke at a time, as
it has before. It writes quickly this time. It does not hesitate.*

**ON SCREEN (the wall):**
```
11. LEND HIM SOMETHING.
```

*The old rule ten sits above it, undisturbed. NOBODY READS THE END FOR HIM. Nobody has read it. But
now there is an eleventh line, and the unit, reading it, feels what a unit feels when it has written
something on a wall for itself in its own hand.*

---

**HANA**
*(French; SUBTITLED, to the older Hana, as one speaks to someone who has crossed a long way: "Do you want
soup?")*
Tu veux de la soupe ?

**OLDER HANA**
*(French; SUBTITLED, after a pause that goes on a little too long: "It's still warm?")*
Elle est encore chaude ?

**PA**
*(French; SUBTITLED: "Sixty-two.")*
Soixante-deux.

*She takes the bowl. She eats, at the table, inside the line, with a woman who has her hands and her
reflexes and her cup. The clock goes on. The little watch at her wrist, uncovered now, ticks nothing at
all.*
