nobody can verify against anything real, because none of it happened.

- It does not present "MR LECH," "SARAH CARTER," or any quoted content as real. All of it is
  flagged, in-text, as unverified model output — the render inserts are reproduced verbatim
  because the previous three episodes' whole methodology is quoting raw output honestly, not
  because this content earned any more trust than the watermelon did.
- It does not attribute any invented statement to the real public figure named in the
  fabrication. He is mentioned only as a subject other (fictional) speakers refer to, never given
  words of his own by the render or by this episode.
- It treats the fabrication as the actual subject and the actual problem — a documentary crew
  reckoning with the fact that a small, uncredentialed model can manufacture something that reads
  like real political reporting, on a real and serious subject, with zero basis. The crew's
  response is professional alarm, not amusement. That's a real editorial choice for this episode,
  not a formality.
- **Placement, same as x05**: Series X, unnumbered — does not touch S06's own Detroit-archive
  continuity, makes no claim about real people, real events, or real politics beyond "a model
  produced text shaped like a claim, unprompted, and here is exactly what it said and why nobody
  should trust it."
