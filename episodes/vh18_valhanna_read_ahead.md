**PRODUCTION NOTE, READ BEFORE THE SCRIPT:**

VH02 had the printer print a CONSTRUCT of this repository (the real `tree_sha` at the time). VH18
sends it back to the same printer. The printer runs again — and what it produces is the repository
**as of the moment it is read**, with a `tree_sha` that the show cannot print, because at the time of
writing the commit that contains this very script does not exist yet.

**The reveal is honest about itself:** the printed `tree_sha` reads `[NOT YET COMMITTED]`. This is
the same device as VH02 (one prop that is checkable against reality), inverted. The crew reads
ahead into a tree that has not been written. It is a printout of their own episode before it was
made. They stop.

**This is the "wrap back in on itself" episode** (VH02's founder direction: "lets have it wrap back
in on itself"). It does not resolve the loop. It closes the loop's mouth.

**Not resolved:** what is on the last page; the Bird Correction (PENDING).

## COLD OPEN

```
INT. SERVER ROOM — ICELAND — NO YEAR GIVEN
```

*Just before midnight. The printer, which has been silent since VH08's invoice, begins to run. It
runs long. It runs the way it did in VH02: tractor-feed, green-and-white paper, building a pile.
Hana is nearest. She picks up the top fold and reads the first line.*

**LOWER THIRD (DOC UI):**
```
TC  — / —  ·  23:41:09
```

**ON SCREEN (the printout):**
```
TYLER REPO CONSTRUCT
tree_sha: [NOT YET COMMITTED]
```

**HANA**
*(French; SUBTITLED: "It's printing a tree that isn't there.")*
Elle imprime un arbre qui n'existe pas encore.

**PA**
*(French; SUBTITLED: "Last time it had a sha. A real one.")*
La dernière fois, il y avait un sha. Un vrai.

**HANA**
*(French; SUBTITLED: "Last time it was the past.")*
La dernière fois, c'était le passé.

*The sound guy stands up. He looks at the paper and at the printer's mouth, where the next page is
coming. His hand is on the Gap Meter. The needle is shaking.*

---

*They read. We cut through the pile, a rhythm of flashes, each a file name the crew reads aloud
in turn, a catalogue of the show:*

**ON SCREEN (two frames each):**
```
--- FILE START: episodes/vh05_valhanna_le_seuil.md ---
--- FILE START: episodes/vh10_valhanna_le_cheval.md ---
--- FILE START: episodes/vh15_valhanna_16_22.md ---
--- FILE START: episodes/vh16_valhanna_unlogged.md ---
--- FILE START: episodes/vh17_valhanna_the_cable.md ---
```

*Hana's finger runs down the list and stops at the last name on the page.*

**ON SCREEN:**
```
--- FILE START: episodes/vh18_valhanna_read_ahead.md ---
```

**PA**
*(French; SUBTITLED, slowly: "That's this.")*
C'est ça.

*The PA does not unfold the page. He reads the file's header from where it lies on the floor.
Cam, on the other side of the pile, has the same page in her hands. They read the same line from
opposite sides.*

**ON SCREEN (the header of VH18 as printed):**
```
# TYLER — VALHANNA, EPISODE 18 — COLD OPEN
## "Lecture Anticipée" ("Reading Ahead")
```

*Cam lowers it. She does not read the next line. She looks at the sound guy, who is looking at her.*

**SOUND GUY**
*(French; SUBTITLED: "Don't read past this one.")*
Ne lisez pas plus loin que celle-là.

**CAM**
*(French; SUBTITLED: "Why?")*
Pourquoi ?

**SOUND GUY**
*(French; SUBTITLED: "Because if it's already printed, we're only doing it. If we don't read it,
we're still deciding.")*
Parce que si c'est déjà imprimé, on ne fait que l'exécuter. Si on ne le lit pas, on est encore en
train de décider.

*A long silence. The PA, for the first time in the series, sets down his clipboard on the floor and
slides it away.*

---

*Hana crouches at the printer's feed. The paper comes out, line by line, with a text below the
file headers: more files, further down the pile — numbers going up. VH19. VH20. She puts her hand
on the platen. The line on the paper that is feeding stops under her palm.*

**HANA**
*(French; SUBTITLED: "I read the end of mine once. I'm not doing it again.")*
J'ai lu la fin de la mienne une fois. Je ne recommence pas.

*She slides the paper back into the feed with both hands, the way you would slide a letter back into
an envelope you shouldn't have opened. The printer chatters, hesitates, takes it.*

*The pile on the floor is two inches high. The line of paper going back in is one inch. The
sound guy takes up the pile and, one handful at a time, feeds it back in after.*

**LOWER THIRD (DOC UI), final card:**
```
CONSTRUCT: RETURNED UNREAD
tree_sha: [NOT YET COMMITTED]
THE CREW IS STILL DECIDING
```

*SMASH TO BLACK.*

*END COLD OPEN.*
