# TYLER — VALHANNA, EPISODE 13 — COLD OPEN
## "Le Plan à Deux" ("The Two-Shot")
### Build 0151 | Einhorn Media | VALHANNA, continued

---

**PRODUCTION NOTE, READ BEFORE THE SCRIPT:**

Hana has been absent from the crew's frame since VH02 (present in VH01 only as dreamed). In VH12 a hand, a sleeve and a
verb arrived through the door, and the hand's owner said *"Not yet. Not her."* VH13 is "her": the
present Hana, the crew's director, walks in from the corridor behind the crew.

**The production problem this episode stages as an in-universe device** (the same technique as
VH01's Duck-dream framing): the Doc UI **cannot hold two Hanas in one shot.** A two-shot of the
director and the figure at the door is the thing the camera refuses to produce, and the crew works
around it, shot by shot, with cuts.

**Language:** French with SUBTITLED gloss.

**Not resolved:** whether the two are one person at different times; what the cut-away hides;
the Bird Correction (PENDING).

## COLD OPEN

```
INT. SERVER ROOM — ICELAND — NO YEAR GIVEN
```

*The crew has not moved since VH12. The tape has been re-laid, straighter. The horse is standing on
the near side of it now. Nobody can say when that happened.*

**LOWER THIRD (DOC UI):**
```
TC  — / —  ·  15:20:44
SUBJECTS IN FRAME: 3
```

*Behind the crew, at the opposite end of the room from the open door, the corridor door opens.
It has been shut since VH01. HANA comes through it with a rolled map under one arm and a
thermos in the other hand, dusted with the grey of a long walk. Mid-thirties, rain in her hair.
She stops three steps in and takes in the room: the crew, the horse, the tape, the three
warm thermoses, the light from the door.*

**HANA**
*(French; SUBTITLED: "I'm late.")*
Je suis en retard.

**PA**
*(French; SUBTITLED, a sound halfway to a sob: "You're here.")*
Tu es là.

**HANA**
*(French; SUBTITLED: "I was held up. I'll explain when I understand it.")*
J'ai été retenue. Je t'expliquerai quand j'aurai compris.

*She looks at the horse. The horse looks at her. Something passes between them that is not
recognition but its cousin.*

*Then she sees the fifth folding chair, in the arc, with a bowl on the seat in front of it. She
crosses to it. She puts the back of two fingers against the bowl, the way you test a child's
forehead. The soup is hot. It has been hot, by the PA's count, for the length of the day.*

**HANA**
*(French; SUBTITLED: "Somebody kept it warm.")*
Quelqu'un l'a gardé chaud.

**PA**
*(French; SUBTITLED: "The inspector set it. We didn't know who for.")*
L'inspecteur l'a posé. On ne savait pas pour qui.

*Hana sits. She does not eat. She holds the bowl in both hands and
looks at the open door over the rim of it.*

---

*Cam raises the camera to frame Hana at the corridor door. The Doc UI shows a green box around her.*

```
SUBJECT: HANA — ACQUIRED
```

*She swings the lens toward the far door, where the light is, where, as she does, a hand appears
on the frame — olive sleeve, white knuckles.*

```
SUBJECT: HANA — ACQUIRED
```

*The green box appears around the hand. And the first box, around Hana, goes red. A single line:*

**LOWER THIRD (DOC UI):**
```
SUBJECT COUNT: 1 ERROR — SUBJECT HANA RESOLVES TO 2 — CANNOT BE FRAMED SIMULTANEOUSLY
```

**SOUND GUY**
*(French; SUBTITLED: "The camera's refusing a two-shot.")*
La caméra refuse le plan à deux.

**CAM**
*(French; SUBTITLED: "Then we'll do it the old way.")*
Alors on le fait à l'ancienne.

*She cuts. We cut with her.*

---

*A sequence in single shots, each lasting about a breath, edited together as a conversation:*

**HANA (present), close**
*(French; SUBTITLED: "Who's there?")*
Qui est là ?

*cut to*

**THE HAND on the frame, close**
*(French; SUBTITLED: "The one who said it.")*
Celle qui l'a dit.

*cut to*

**HANA (present), close**
*(French; SUBTITLED: "Said what?")*
Dit quoi ?

*cut to*

**THE FIGURE (shoulder and ear only), in profile, hair the same color as Hana's, a cut above the
temple that has been closed with three stitches**
*(French; SUBTITLED: "The verb. I got it wrong. I've been wrong about it for a long time.")*
Le verbe. Je me suis trompée. Depuis longtemps.

*cut to*

**HANA (present), close. She has gone very still.**
*(French; SUBTITLED, small: "Which verb?")*
Quel verbe ?

*cut to*

**THE HAND, closing on the doorframe.**
*(French; SUBTITLED: "You'll know when you're me.")*
Tu sauras quand tu seras moi.

*Nobody cuts away. We hold on the hand, on the stained cuff, for four seconds with no one speaking.
The horse has moved between the two ends of the room without anyone seeing; she stands at the
exact midpoint, her head toward Hana, her tail toward the door.*

---

*Hana steps toward the tape. She does not cross it. She sets down her thermos on the floor beside
the others. The fourth thermos in the row. She stands up and looks at the open door and, without
turning, speaks to the lens:*

**HANA**
*(French; SUBTITLED: "Cam. Don't put me in a frame with her. Not until I say.")*
Cam. Ne me mets pas dans le même plan qu'elle. Pas avant que je le dise.

**CAM**
*(French; SUBTITLED: "It won't let me anyway.")*
De toute façon, il ne me laisse pas.

**HANA**
*(French; SUBTITLED: "Good. Something's still on my side.")*
Bien. Quelque chose est encore de mon côté.

**LOWER THIRD (DOC UI), final card:**
```
HANA: PRESENT
HANA: ALSO PRESENT
FRAMING: DECLINED
```

*SMASH TO BLACK.*

*END COLD OPEN.*
