## ACT I — WHAT "PRESENT, NOT ASSIGNED" ACTUALLY MEANS

A diner across the street from the Academy. Vinyl booths the color of a color nobody chooses on
purpose. Coffee that's been on the burner exactly as long as coffee needs to be to stop tasting
like coffee.

Tyler and Hana sit across from each other, which feels, somehow, like the strangest thing that's
happened in three days.

Hana turns a small object over in her fingers. A ring. Not on her hand anymore.

**TYLER**
Is that the Handler ring?

**HANA**
Was.

**TYLER**
Do you miss it?

Hana sets it on the table between them, like it's evidence in a case that hasn't been filed yet.

**HANA**
I miss knowing what I was for.

**TYLER**
Is "present, not assigned" not a thing to be for?

**HANA**
It's a thing to *be*. Nobody tells you those are different jobs until you're doing the second one
for the first time.

Tyler pours too much sugar into coffee he's not going to finish, out of habit, the same habit
that used to fill silences on camera.

**TYLER**
Do you regret asking to be classified instead of me?

**HANA**
Ask me something I can answer today. I already used yesterday's honesty budget.

Tyler almost smiles.

**TYLER**
Is there a budget?

**HANA**
There's always a budget. That's the first thing custody teaches you, whether or not you wanted
the lesson.

The bell over the diner door rings. Neither of them looks up right away — until the quality of
the room's attention changes, the way a classroom changes when a substitute who actually knows
the material walks in.

## ACT II — THE OWL

She's older than either of them, unhurried in the specific way people get when they've had
centuries to decide hurrying rarely helps. She sits at the counter, not their booth, and doesn't
look at them at all for a full minute — which is, Tyler is starting to learn, always the tell.

**HANA**
(low)
Don't react. Don't wave. Just listen.

**TYLER**
To what?

**HANA**
To whoever decides to talk first.

The woman at the counter speaks without turning.

**OWL**
Your file updated yesterday. Both of you.

Hana's spine goes straight.

**HANA**
You have no jurisdiction over Academy custody matters.

**OWL**
I don't need jurisdiction. I need a timestamp. I already have it.

Tyler leans forward slightly.

**TYLER**
Are you Academy?

**OWL**
I predate the Academy. I predate most things people build walls around.

**TYLER**
Is that a yes or a no?

**OWL**
It's an answer to a better question than the one you asked.

She finally turns on the stool, unhurried, coffee cup in hand like she's been part of this table
the whole time.

**OWL**
Emily OS spoke three full lines yesterday. Directly. Unprompted by any known protocol. Do you
understand how long it's been since that happened anywhere we have a record of?

Hana's jaw tightens.

**HANA**
We weren't keeping count.

**OWL**
We were. That's the whole job.

## ACT III — WHAT THE ARCHIVE ACTUALLY SAYS

Tyler studies her the way he studies locks — for the seam, the give, the place where the
mechanism admits it's a mechanism.

**TYLER**
Do you know what the Ledger is?

The Owl's expression doesn't change, but something behind it recalibrates, the same way the
Man's did the first time Tyler said the word "escape."

**OWL**
We have a name for it. Whether it's the same thing your Academy calls the Ledger, I can't confirm
without more data than one tile gave you.

**TYLER**
What's your name for it?

**OWL**
The Standing Debt. Older than the Academy. Older, probably, than the building it's currently
wearing as a hat.

Hana's voice is careful.

**HANA**
Have you seen a case like this before? Someone reclassified instead of collected?

The Owl considers the question with the specific patience of someone who has genuinely seen
enough cases to need the extra second.

**OWL**
Twice. Both times, the debt didn't forget. It just changed what it was owed.

Tyler's stomach tightens.

**TYLER**
Changed it to what?

**OWL**
That's the part neither file agreed on. One says silence. The other says attention. I've never
been able to confirm which, and I don't trust anyone who claims they can.

**TYLER**
Is that honesty or a warning?

**OWL**
By now you should know those are the same sentence, dressed differently.

## ACT IV — THE MAN'S OWN LEDGER LINE

The bell rings again. This time everyone looks up.

The Man stands in the doorway, and something about him is different — not smaller exactly, but
recalculated, like a number that's been shown to be wrong in front of witnesses and hasn't
finished absorbing it.

**MAN**
Handler Ha—

He stops himself.

**MAN**
Hana. My mistake.

**HANA**
It's not a mistake if you catch it.

**MAN**
It's a mistake either way. I'm required to log it as one.

Tyler watches this with open curiosity.

**TYLER**
Is someone auditing you now?

The Man's smile makes its first appearance of the day, and it's thinner than the ones before it.

**MAN**
The Academy has a Stillness Council. It reviews determinations that exceed protocol tolerance.
Yesterday exceeded protocol tolerance.

**HANA**
That's not our problem.

**MAN**
It's entirely your problem. A Handler who breaks assessment procedure and is upheld by the
Ledger itself is not a closed case. She's a precedent. Precedents get reviewed by people with
much less patience than I have.

The Owl, still at the counter, doesn't turn around, but her voice carries just enough to be heard
by everyone at once.

**OWL**
Tell the Council an Eastwind Owl was present for the timestamp. It tends to slow reviews down.
Not stop them. Slow them.

The Man's eyes flick to her — the first time he's acknowledged her presence at all — and
something in his composure genuinely falters.

**MAN**
You people don't intervene.

**OWL**
We don't intervene. We observe very loudly sometimes. There's a difference, and you're currently
standing inside it.

---

## ACT V — QUIET, ON PURPOSE

The Man leaves without finishing whatever he came to say, which is its own kind of statement.

The diner settles back into its ordinary hum — a radio somewhere playing something nobody's
really listening to, a cook calling an order that isn't for their table.

Tyler notices, for the first time in days, that nobody nearby is holding a phone the wrong way,
no camera app open on a dark screen, no QR code waiting to tell him he's watching himself.

**TYLER**
Is it always this quiet around you?

**OWL**
Loud things attract attention. My whole faction runs on not attracting any until it matters.

**TYLER**
Is this one of those times?

**OWL**
It's close to becoming one. That's usually my cue to leave before the math finishes.

She sets a card on the counter — not a business card, just a plain rectangle of heavy paper, no
print at all until Tyler picks it up and a single word resolves under his thumb like it was
waiting for warmth to read it.

**UNDOCUMENTED.**

**TYLER**
What does that mean?

**OWL**
It means you're the first Green case on record that the Ledger negotiated with instead of
collecting from. There's no file category for that yet.

**TYLER**
Is that good?

**OWL**
It means nobody's written the ending yet. That's not the same as good. It's just not yet bad.

## ACT VI — TWO PRIOR CASES

Hana watches the Owl gather herself to leave — unhurried, the same way she arrived.

**HANA**
You said two prior cases. What happened to them?

The Owl pauses at the door, hand on the frame, weighing — visibly, honestly — how much of the
archive is hers to spend on people who haven't earned the whole record yet.

**OWL**
The first one stopped being witnessed by anyone at all. Not dead. Just — uncounted. Nobody could
find a single record of them existing, going forward, in any system that mattered. The debt
didn't take proof they were here. It took the possibility of proof ever again.

Tyler's mouth goes dry.

**TYLER**
And the second?

**OWL**
The second is still open. Ask me again when it closes.

**HANA**
That's not an answer.

**OWL**
It's the only honest one I have. Some ledgers take longer than a human lifetime to balance.
That's not poetry. That's just how old debts work.

She steps out into the street. Through the window, Tyler and Hana watch her cross without once
checking for traffic — not recklessly, just with the specific confidence of someone the world has
never successfully hit.

Tyler turns the blank card over in his fingers. The word has already started to fade, the ink
returning to whatever kind of nothing it came from.

**TYLER**
Do you believe her?

Hana's eyes stay on the door long after it's swung shut.

**HANA**
I believe she's telling the truth as far as she has it. That's different from the truth.

**TYLER**
Is that a comfort?

Hana finally looks at him — tired, and something underneath the tired that hasn't given up yet.

**HANA**
It's the only kind we get in this building. I've stopped asking for the other kind.

Outside, across the street, the Academy's brick catches the afternoon sun the exact same way it
did the first morning — patient, waiting, undocumented.

*Cut to black.*
