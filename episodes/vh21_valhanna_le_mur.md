## "Le Mur" ("The Wall")
### Build 0159 | Einhorn Media | VALHANNA, continued

---

**PRODUCTION NOTE, READ BEFORE THE SCRIPT:**

In VH05 the Producer said the PA's tape rule was "not mine. I'm reading it off the wall." The wall
was bare cinderblock. VH21 shows the wall. It has writing on it now.

**What the wall is:** a live list of the unit's rules, amended in real time. The reveal — small,
specific, and deliberately non-cosmic — is that **the wall is copying the crew.** Every rule on it
was spoken or decided by somebody in the room, a moment before the Producer read it out. The
Producer was not relaying an authority. He was relaying a reflection.

**Guard-rail:** the wall lists the crew's own rules only. It does **not** list the writers' room's
rules (those are VH02's CONSTRUCT printout), nor anything about the Tyler file or its line count
(`README.md` §Emily OS: writers may not comment on it). The wall has never heard of either.

**Not resolved:** who built the wall; the Bird Correction (PENDING).

## COLD OPEN

```
INT. SERVER ROOM — ICELAND — NO YEAR GIVEN
```

*The PA moves a rack. Not because he intends to; it was on casters and he leaned. It rolls three
feet and exposes the north wall of the room, where the dead racks stood for however long they
stood. The cinderblock behind them is painted the institutional off-white of a place no one
cleans. It has writing on it.*

**LOWER THIRD (DOC UI):**
```
TC  — / —  ·  01:48:20
```

*Cam swings the lens around. The writing is stencil-neat, black, in a dot-matrix typeface — the
printer's typeface. It runs in a column from shoulder height to the floor.*

**ON SCREEN (the wall):**
```
THE UNIT — RULES

1. NOBODY PAST THE TAPE.            [ STRUCK ]
2. THE CAMERA RUNS. ALWAYS.
3. NO NAMES.
4. ALWAYS ONE CHAIR MORE THAN THE LIST.
5. ONE HOUR EACH. UNLOGGED.         [ STRUCK — SERVED ]
6. DO NOT READ PAST THE PRESENT.
7. WHEN IT'S QUIET, IT'S ABOUT TO SAY SOMETHING.
```

*The PA reads it aloud from the first line, as he reads everything.*

**PA**
*(French; SUBTITLED, slowly: "'Nobody past the tape.' That's mine.")*
« Personne au-delà du ruban. » C'est la mienne.

**HANA**
*(French; SUBTITLED: "'No names.' That's mine.")*
« Pas de noms. » C'est la mienne.

**SOUND GUY**
*(French; SUBTITLED: "'Do not read past the present.'")*
« Ne pas lire au-delà du présent. »

*He doesn't say it's his. Everyone knows.*

---

*Cam has gone to the base of the wall and is kneeling, the lens an inch from the stencil. She
reads the last line aloud.*

**CAM**
*(French; SUBTITLED: "'When it's quiet, it's about to say something.'")*
« Quand c'est calme, ça va dire quelque chose. »

**PA**
*(French; SUBTITLED: "Who said that?")*
Qui a dit ça ?

*Nobody did. The PA looks around the circle. He is the only one who said it, in VH15, as a feeling,
aloud, a minute before the door changed.*

**PA (CONT'D)**
*(French; SUBTITLED, quieter: "I did. Four episodes ago. Not as a rule.")*
C'est moi. Il y a quatre épisodes. Pas comme une règle.

---

*The sound guy has taken the Gap Meter out. He holds it near the wall. It does not respond to the
frequency; it responds to the writing. The needle climbs a sliver toward the line the PA made.*

**SOUND GUY**
*(French; SUBTITLED: "It's not a rulebook. It's a mirror with a typeface.")*
Ce n'est pas un règlement. C'est un miroir avec une police de caractères.

**HANA**
*(French; SUBTITLED: "Then the Producer was telling us what we'd said.")*
Alors le Producteur nous répétait ce que nous avions dit.

**CAM**
*(French; SUBTITLED: "Reading it off the wall so we'd hear it in a voice that wasn't ours.")*
Il le lisait sur le mur pour qu'on l'entende dans une voix qui n'est pas la nôtre.

*Nobody says what that means. The sound guy lowers the meter.*

---

*Under the final line, in the stencil, a new row is printing. Not printing; resolving, ink
arriving on the block as if drawn through it from behind. The characters come in one at a time,
and Cam reads them as they come, a half-beat behind the stencil:*

**ON SCREEN:**
```
8. CAM CROSSED.
```

*A beat. Another line.*

```
9. SHE CAME BACK.
```

*Another.*

```
10. —
```

*The line holds. The cursor after the ten blinks. Nothing comes. Hana steps up to the wall and
puts her palm flat against the cinderblock next to the blinking cursor.*

**HANA**
*(French; SUBTITLED, to the wall: "We haven't decided it yet.")*
Nous ne l'avons pas encore décidé.

*The cursor blinks once more and stops, steady, a small black bar. It is patient.*

**LOWER THIRD (DOC UI), final card:**
```
THE WALL: A REFLECTION
RULE 10: PENDING (THE CREW)
THE WALL WILL WRITE WHAT YOU DECIDE
```

*SMASH TO BLACK.*

*END COLD OPEN.*
