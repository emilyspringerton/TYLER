**PRODUCTION NOTE, READ BEFORE THE SCRIPT:**

Saturday, Saturn's day (Zagan #23, 4.44 Hz, *transmutation*), and the end of the second week. The
accrual engine of the Heikegani Crustocrats (VH08) began again on the first day, when the date field
filled, and has been quietly counting. The Shell Parliament sends a collector. **The comedy of this
episode is that the collector cannot lie about a number and the number comes out zero.**

**Cast.** *Kei* (`README.md` §IV: "Sixteen years old. Enforcer-in-training. Shell-debt almost paid
off, which means they're about to find out what the family business actually involves"), with an
abacus and a shell-shaped lunch pail with a face on it. And, because "the Shell Accountant" is the
Parliament's name for someone who has been redesigning their collection routes for three years,
**Emily Springerton**, Logistics Architect of the Golden Band (`README.md` §XVIII), "already there
before the scene required her."

**Emily's rules** (checklist, §XIX): she never predicts; her question is mechanical; she does not
explain her reasoning before it is requested; her fees are functional (one meal formally presented);
she was already there; the Camera Op has eleven minutes of her, not submitted; **Tyler borrowed a pen,
and the debt is still open.** All of these are honored here. She never says the OS's name. Nobody does.

**Zagan, transmutation:** debt → positive balance. The Parliament's new account category,
VOLUNTARY CONSTRUCTION (`lore/shell_parliament_ledger.md`, VC-001), opens a line for the unit's table.

**Not resolved:** the pending item (the ending Cam is owed); why Emily was already there.

*A knock, the proper kind, on the wood of the corridor door. It has been a long time since anyone in this
room heard a knock on wood. The PA gets up to answer it.*

*Two people stand in the corridor. The first is sixteen. A black suit in a size meant for a larger
person. An abacus, held under one arm like a baguette. A lunch pail on a strap, in the shape of a
crab's shell, with a face in the lid, carved, grave, human, that watches the room with the
patience of the dead. Smells faintly of brine. The second stands slightly behind: mid-thirties, quiet,
a rolled whiteboard under one arm, a pen behind each ear, an expression of a person who has already
reviewed the dependency graph.*

*The PA looks at the second person. He looks at his clipboard. On the first page, in his own
handwriting, dated Day 2, under SEATS, there is a line he has no memory of writing:*

**ON SCREEN (the clipboard):**
```
E. SPRINGERTON — LOGISTICS — (CORRIDOR)
```

**PA**
*(French; SUBTITLED, faintly: "You're on my list.")*
Vous êtes sur ma liste.

**EMILY**
*(French; SUBTITLED: "Since the second day. The tape needed routing.")*
Depuis le deuxième jour. Le ruban avait besoin d'un routage.

*She says nothing else. She comes in.*

---

*The sheet. They have all been walking round it for fourteen days: the sheet that fell into the printer's
tray at the end of Day 366, unread, because nobody in the room knew what it was and everyone had a
view. It lies where it fell. The sixteen-year-old crosses straight to it and picks it up with a snap
and holds it to the lens with a sort of weary professionalism.*

**KEI**
*(French; SUBTITLED: "This is yours.")*
C'est à vous.

**CAM**
*(French; SUBTITLED, uneasily: "We didn't read it.")*
On ne l'a pas lue.

**KEI**
*(French; SUBTITLED: "For fourteen days?")*
Pendant quatorze jours ?

**CAM**
*(French; SUBTITLED: "We thought it was the future.")*
On pensait que c'était le futur.

**KEI**
*(French; SUBTITLED, with the absolute flatness of the family trade: "The future is a bill.")*
Le futur, c'est une facture.

**ON SCREEN (the sheet; the lacquered type of the Parliament):**
```
HEIKEGANI CRUSTOCRAT FINANCIAL BUREAU
STATEMENT OF ACCRUAL

PERIOD:    DAY 366 (00:03:10)
RATE:      PER DIEM, PRO RATA
AMOUNT:    0.0022 SM

(ONE COPY FOR ACKNOWLEDGMENT.)
```

**PA**
*(French; SUBTITLED, a high thin voice: "It's — three minutes ten?")*
C'est — trois minutes dix ?

**KEI**
*(French; SUBTITLED: "At pro rata. Yes.")*
Au prorata. Oui.

*The Duck, from the sixth chair, quietly, in English:*

**DUCK**
*(English)*
We were afraid of two-hundredths of a shell-mark.

---

*Kei does not smile. Kei takes the abacus from under the arm, lays it on the cloth, and sets the lunch pail beside it, and the
face in the lid watches. It is a procedure.*

**KEI**
*(French; SUBTITLED: "Account CO-001. Joint liability. Opened on the day the footage was published. Two holders.
For fourteen days the account was suspended for want of a date. Since the first it has accrued.")*
Compte CO-001. Responsabilité conjointe. Ouvert le jour de la publication des images. Deux titulaires.
Pendant quatorze jours le compte a été suspendu faute de date. Depuis le premier, il court.

**CAM**
*(French; SUBTITLED: "Who are the holders?")*
Qui sont les titulaires ?

*Kei looks at the page. Kei looks up. At Cam. At the seventh chair. At Cam again.*

**KEI**
*(French; SUBTITLED: "The Camera. And Seat Seven.")*
La Caméra. Et le Siège Sept.

*It is not a revelation. It is the answer to a question the PA tore up a page about on the sixteenth day
of nothing. Cam's hand goes to the rig. Tyler, in the seventh chair, does not move.*

**KEI (CONT'D)**
*(French; SUBTITLED: "The engine needed two names in the room. For fourteen days it had one. Since the first, both.")*
Le moteur avait besoin de deux noms dans la pièce. Pendant quatorze jours, il en avait un. Depuis le
premier, les deux.

---

*The abacus. It does what the Parliament's abaci do: runs faster than an abacus has any right to,
a fast dry clatter that seems to come from the room itself, the beads leaving the frame as they move. The
lunch pail's face shifts a degree, as if reading the sum.*

**KEI**
*(French; SUBTITLED: "CO-001, fourteen days at point three: four point two shell-marks. Seat Seven's
small obligations: a spoon, a ladle, an ending. The unit's: a casserole dish. A third party's: a pen.")*
CO-001, quatorze jours à zéro virgule trois : quatre virgule deux marques-coquille. Obligations mineures du
Siège Sept : une cuillère, une louche, une fin. De l'unité : un plat. D'un tiers : un stylo.

*The beads stop. The abacus, for the first time, sticks. One bead refuses to move. Kei frowns at it
and flicks it. It stays.*

**KEI (CONT'D)**
*(French; SUBTITLED: "'An ending, unspecified' is not a number.")*
« Une fin, non spécifiée » n'est pas un nombre.

**EMILY (from the whiteboard, not turning round)**
*(French; SUBTITLED: "It's a pending item.")*
C'est un poste en suspens.

*She has unrolled the whiteboard and set it up on its wheels beside the cloth. She has pen in each hand now.
She draws without hurry: a circle, labeled CAM. A circle, SOUND. A circle, PA. A circle, HANA. A circle,
HANA (the second; the pen hesitates one half-second and writes "H-2"). A circle, DUCK. A circle, PRODUCER.
A circle, SEAT 7. Arrows between them, each labeled with a small amount in the margin: a spoon, a pen, a ladle, a
dish, a pencil, a tin of shrimp. She does not draw the pending item. She draws a small square in the
corner and puts it inside the square.*

**KEI**
*(French; SUBTITLED, a child trying to be a grown-up: "What are you doing?")*
Qu'est-ce que vous faites ?

**EMILY**
*(French; SUBTITLED: "Multilateral netting. Everyone is owed what they owe.")*
Compensation multilatérale. Chacun se voit devoir ce qu'il doit.

*She steps back. The board is a net of arrows. She takes the red pen and, one node at a time,
writes a small number beside each circle. The numbers are very small. Beside each, she writes the
same thing.*

```
0.00
```

*Kei stares. They count. Beads move on the abacus again: in a fast scatter, then a slow one, then one bead at a time.
The lunch pail's face has leaned toward the whiteboard, its stone mouth very slightly open.*

---

*The abacus stops.*

*It does not jam. It stops. All the beads at the left edge, as they were on the first day it was made.*

**KEI**
*(French; SUBTITLED, barely, a whisper: "Zero.")*
Zéro.

**EMILY**
*(French; SUBTITLED, in her functional voice: "How exactly does that work?")*
Comment est-ce que ça fonctionne, exactement ?

**KEI**
*(French; SUBTITLED, still looking at the beads: "It adds.")*
Ça additionne.

**EMILY**
*(French; SUBTITLED: "What does it depend on?")*
De quoi cela dépend-il ?

**KEI**
*(French; SUBTITLED, slowly: "On the count being true.")*
Du fait que le compte soit vrai.

**EMILY**
*(French; SUBTITLED: "Then it depends on you.")*
Alors cela dépend de vous.

*Kei does not answer. Kei looks at the number on the beads. The Heikegani cannot lie about numbers; it
causes them a physical pain, a deep ache in the places on the spine where the shell is carried. Kei
has been trained since the age of six that zero is a number that other people say. It is, in the Parliament, a
word reserved for the end of an account. It has not been said by a Kei in the lifetime of the account.*

**KEI**
*(French; SUBTITLED: "I've never said that number.")*
Je n'ai jamais dit ce nombre.

**EMILY**
*(French; SUBTITLED: "Is it true?")*
Est-il vrai ?

**KEI**
*(French; SUBTITLED, almost a laugh, almost not: "It doesn't hurt.")*
Ça ne fait pas mal.

---

*The sheet is amended. Kei writes in a little black book, in a small precise hand, the Parliament's
final line for the account:*

**ON SCREEN (Kei's book):**
```
CO-001: NETTED. BALANCE 0.00 SM.
EXCLUDING: ONE (1) PENDING ITEM (UNSPECIFIED; NOT DUE).
VC-001: ENTRY OPENED — "THE TABLE" — UNIT A — +1.00 SM (VOLUNTARY CONSTRUCTION)
```

**KEI**
*(French; SUBTITLED, to the lens, with great dignity: "Red. I would like to photograph the abacus reading zero.
It will cost me my position.")*
Rouge. Je voudrais photographier le boulier à zéro. Cela me coûtera ma position.

**CAM**
*(French; SUBTITLED, gravely: "You may have the photograph. We'll call it even.")*
Vous aurez la photo. On sera quittes.

*Kei does it with a flip phone, a small gray one, a click and a flash. Then Kei closes the abacus and
looks, finally, at the pot.*

**EMILY**
*(French; SUBTITLED: "My fee. One meal, formally presented.")*
Mon tarif. Un repas, formellement présenté.

*The PA stands. He folds the cloth over his forearm. He takes a bowl in the other hand. He presents it
with a small bow, entirely correct, as a maître d' who has never done it, and has read of it.*

*Emily takes it. She eats, standing, three spoonfuls, with the thoroughness of someone who tracks a
thing to its end.*

**EMILY (CONT'D)**
*(French; SUBTITLED: "Nine of ten. The tenth is pending.")*
Neuf sur dix. Le dixième est en suspens.

*She puts the bowl down. She sets the spoon across it, tidily. And then Tyler is standing in front
of her, with his hand out, and in the hand a pen, a cheap black ballpoint, bitten at one end.*

**TYLER**
*(French; SUBTITLED: "I've been meaning to return this.")*
Je voulais vous rendre ça.

*She looks at the pen. She looks at him. She does not take it.*

**EMILY**
*(French; SUBTITLED: "It's noted.")*
C'est noté.

**TYLER**
*(French; SUBTITLED: "It's your pen.")*
C'est votre stylo.

**EMILY**
*(French; SUBTITLED, not unkindly: "I noted that I noted it. Keep it.")*
J'ai noté que je l'ai noté. Gardez-le.

*He lowers the pen. Cam has come forward, with the rig on her shoulder. She stops a few feet away,
the way one does at a person one has been looking at for a long time.*

**CAM**
*(French; SUBTITLED: "I have eleven minutes of you.")*
J'ai onze minutes de vous.

**EMILY**
*(French; SUBTITLED: "Is it load-bearing?")*
Est-ce que c'est porteur ?

**CAM**
*(French; SUBTITLED, after a moment: "I don't know.")*
Je ne sais pas.

**EMILY**
*(French; SUBTITLED: "Then it isn't submitted. Yet.")*
Alors ce n'est pas versé. Pas encore.

*She turns to the whiteboard. She begins to wipe it down.*

---

*Kei, buckling the lunch pail, has watched this. Kei says it low and in French, to Emily, as one speaks to an
adult one wishes to be certain of.*

**KEI**
*(French; SUBTITLED, a whisper: "He never returns anything?")*
Il ne rend jamais rien ?

*Emily keeps wiping. The marker comes away in long clean strokes. She answers without turning, in
the voice she has used for everything, and it is the only sentence she has said all day that is not a question
or an assessment.*

**EMILY**
*(French; SUBTITLED: "He borrows in order to come back.")*
Il emprunte pour revenir.

*Kei looks at Tyler, who is looking at the pen in his hand. The sixteen-year-old does not say anything else. The face in the
lunch pail's lid, very slowly, closes its eyes.*

**LOWER THIRD (DOC UI), final card:**
```
CO-001: NETTED — 0.00 SM
VC-001: "THE TABLE" +1.00 SM
PEN: NOT RETURNED
```

*SMASH TO BLACK.*

*END COLD OPEN.*
