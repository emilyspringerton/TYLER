### Build 0141 | Einhorn Media | VALHANNA, continued

**PRODUCTION NOTE, READ BEFORE THE SCRIPT:**

Founder, real-time (2026-10-13): *"next episode"* + a lore artifact receipt (`lore/day_out_of_time_receipt.md`):
12 months sum to 365 days, 13 moons of 28 sum to 364, and **one day is left over**, belonging to
no month and no weekday.

VH02 ended on `ENCOUNTER: THE CREW MEETS` with the sentence unfinished. **This script does not
finish it.** It answers *when*, not *who*. The door opened onto the one day nothing is dated on.

**The tie to canon:** `INTERLUDE.md` Frame 04 already established that Tyler's date field is
unpopulated and that the Shell Parliament's accrual engine "cannot calculate debt against it."
The Day Out of Time is that field's missing cell given a name. The CO-001 joint liability account
(`characters/camera_op.md` §V) accrues against dates; on this day it has none to accrue against.

**What this script will not say:** that a single hand built the calendar. The artifact asserts it;
the archive's own footnotes decline to. The episode inherits the Cataloguer's restraint: it uses the
arithmetic (true, and checkable on a napkin) and leaves the motive alone.

**Language:** French with SUBTITLED gloss, the standing MODE_TYLER convention. The crew speaks
French in this room the way the Duck dreamed it in VH01; nobody remarks on it.

**Not resolved:** who is at the door; Hana's absence (third episode running); the thermoses;
Entry 23; the Bird Correction (still PENDING).

## COLD OPEN

```
INT. BEDROOM — PRESENT DAY — NIGHT
```

*The Duck, tuxedo rumpled, sits up in the dark. On the wall opposite the bed, a wall calendar. The
Duck has never owned a wall calendar. It counts the pages without meaning to: thirteen. It counts
the squares on the top one: twenty-eight, four rows of seven, no stray days hanging off the
bottom.*

**THE DUCK**
*(English, quietly, to nobody)*
That's not how the month goes.

*(it checks the pages again; still thirteen)*

*(it does not remark on the calendar again)*

**SMASH CUT**

```
INT. SERVER ROOM — ICELAND — NO YEAR GIVEN
```

*The room from VH01 and VH02. The door is open. Light comes through it in a clean line across the
floor, from somewhere that is not Iceland. Nobody has gone through it. Nobody has come through it.
Cam holds the frame on the doorway and has not lowered the rig.*

**LOWER THIRD (DOC UI) — the burned-in timecode, which has run without trouble since 1901:**
```
TC  13 / 28  ·  09:41:07
```

*As it reads, the date half stops. The seconds keep counting. The date field goes to dashes.*

```
TC  — / —  ·  09:41:08
```

**SOUND GUY**
*(French; SUBTITLED: "The clock lost the date.")*
L'horloge a perdu la date.

**CAM**
*(French; SUBTITLED: "It didn't lose it. There isn't one.")*
Elle ne l'a pas perdue. Il n'y en a pas.

*The PA has his clipboard against his chest. He writes on it anyway, slowly, a column of numbers,
and reads them aloud the way a person reads a thing they would like to be wrong.*

**PA**
*(French; SUBTITLED: "Twelve months. Thirty-one, twenty-eight, thirty-one... three hundred sixty-five.")*
Douze mois. Trente et un, vingt-huit, trente et un... trois cent soixante-cinq.

**PA (CONT'D)**
*(French; SUBTITLED: "Thirteen of twenty-eight. Three hundred sixty-four.")*
Treize fois vingt-huit. Trois cent soixante-quatre.

*He stops.*

**PA (CONT'D)**
*(French; SUBTITLED: "There's one left over.")*
Il en reste un.

**SOUND GUY**
*(French; SUBTITLED: "And in a leap year?")*
Et les années bissextiles ?

**CAM**
*(French; SUBTITLED: "Then there are two.")*
Alors il y en a deux.

*Nobody asks which of the two this is.*

---

*The radio clicks. The Producer, light as ever, a half-step off tempo.*

**PRODUCER (O.S., radio)**
*(French; SUBTITLED: "There's no rest in a production. Keep rolling.")*
Il n'y a pas de repos dans une production. Continuez à tourner.

*Cam does not answer him. The sound guy turns a dial that does not need turning. The PA checks his
clipboard, finds the Producer's call sheet clipped behind his own pages, and unfolds it.*

**ON SCREEN (the call sheet, as the PA holds it to the lens):**
```
CALL SHEET — DAY 31 THROUGH DAY 364
 [ one row per day, every one with a date, a weather note, a shot count ]
DAY 365  ——
```

*Nothing is printed on the last line. It is not crossed out. The row simply has no content.*

**PA**
*(French; SUBTITLED: "He has no plan for today.")*
Il n'a rien prévu pour aujourd'hui.

---

*Cam lowers the camera.*

*It is the first time in the archive that she has lowered it with the record running. She holds it
at her hip, lens toward the floor, the red light on. The doorway is out of frame. We watch it only
in the PA's face, and the sound guy's, as they watch it.*

**SOUND GUY**
*(French; SUBTITLED, very low: "I'm getting twenty-eight point seven.")*
Je capte vingt-huit virgule sept.

**CAM**
*(French; SUBTITLED: "That's almost twenty-eight.")*
C'est presque vingt-huit.

**SOUND GUY**
*(French; SUBTITLED: "Almost.")*
Presque.

*Nobody does the subtraction. The meter holds. The tubes breathe.*

**ON SCREEN (small, in the dream-quality type VH01 used):**
```
CO-001 JOINT LIABILITY: ACCRUAL SUSPENDED
BIRD CORRECTION: PENDING
```

*A shadow crosses the floor from the doorway. It is long. It is not the shape of anyone the
archive has recorded. The PA turns his clipboard over so nobody sees what he writes on the back of
it.*

*Cam raises the camera again. She does not say why.*

**LOWER THIRD (DOC UI), final card:**
```
TODAY IS NOT A DAY OF THE WEEK
ENCOUNTER: [UNLOGGED]
```

*The cursor after the bracket blinks, as in VH02, and nothing comes.*

*SMASH TO BLACK.*

*END COLD OPEN.*
