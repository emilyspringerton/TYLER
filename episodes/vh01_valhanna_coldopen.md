**PRODUCTION NOTE, READ BEFORE THE SCRIPT:**

Founder, real-time (2026-09-30): *"we are starting a new season of TYLER; VALHANNA; tyler and
hana wake up in a server room in 1986; there is a printer with a long printout; all the servers
are gone; they find a thermos of minestrone soup still warm; also a second thermos of coffee; it
turns out they are in iceland; the printout is very dangerous and tyler is excited but Hana is
terrified of what it is; the source code for the mecha cube; written in LO; they know if they look
at it the bird correction resolves; this is a pilot cold open for a new season so be sure to bring
back some of that; hana begs tyler not to look at it; he feeds it back into the printer and the
duck wakes up in a cold sweat; the whole thing is in french up until the duck wakes up; he doesnt
remember knowing french; jenesequoi."*

Same casual, arbitrary season-naming precedent as Season 6 (Take 2) itself (`EPISODES.md`:
*"lets just arbitrarily say next season is season 6"*) — the founder names a season in one
sentence, in real time, and the writer's room files it. **VALHANNA** is not a number. It sits
next to NEVER_LAND and Series X: a named season, not a numbered one. It inherits Hana directly
from Season 6 (Take 2) (`s06b_e00_the_residue_test.md` through `s06b_e02_yellow_cut_short.md`,
Build 0136–0138 — the most recently active continuity thread in this repo) rather than
introducing a new character of the same name; see Continuity below for exactly how.

The **minestrone** is not invented fresh either. It is `characters/the_auditor.md` §II/§IV's own
established signature dish — "his own, made in a pot too large for one household" — and the
Uncompromised's own guild tradition ("There is always minestrone. The Convener makes it."). A
still-warm thermos of it, unattended, in an abandoned Icelandic server room, is written here as a
found-object mystery in the show's own established idiom (the folded hoodie in
`x00_the_custody_of_a_duck.md`, the pen Emily Springerton hasn't returned in `CITY_OF_LIGHT.md`)
— *whose* thermos this is is not answered in this cold open. It is filed as a flag, not a reveal.

The **Bird Correction** has been the show's longest-running unresolved status line — "PENDING" at
the end of nearly every season from S01 through S11 (`README.md`, `s01e01_pilot.md`,
`s02e06_the_ramp.md`, `s01e01_pilot_coldopen_x2_experimental.md`, Jiangshi Memo, etc.) — despite
two much earlier documents (`_.md`, Build 0016: *"BIRD CORRECTION: NO LONGER PENDING"*;
`CITY_OF_LIGHT.md`: *"The Bird Correction. Complete... noted as a closed item so it does not get
reopened"*) declaring it closed. This cold open is written as the reason for that contradiction,
not a resolution of it: an early opportunity to close it for real, in 1986, that Tyler chooses not
to take. Every later season's "PENDING" is downstream of this scene. **Bird Correction status
after this episode: still pending. By choice, this time — the first time the record shows the
correction had a door and nobody walked through it by accident.**

The **French** and the **Duck** are the same device the show has already used once, restaged
honestly rather than hidden: the coldopen "x2" experimental variant framed a rendering failure as
an in-universe Emily OS memory render, glitching in a costume the show already had a place for.
This cold open borrows that same honesty. It is not narrated by Emily OS this time — the Duck
dreams it, in French, and doesn't remember French awake. Per the standing Consistency Check rule
("at least one impossible detail treated as unremarkable" — `s01e01_pilot_coldopen_x2_experimental.md`
Production Note), nobody in this script — not Tyler, not Hana, not the Duck once it's awake —
remarks on the French. It is simply what the dream was in.

## CONTINUITY — HOW HANA GETS HERE

Season 6 (Take 2) leaves Hana mid-shoot, running a documentary crew (Cam, the sound guy, the PA)
that keeps almost — but not quite — filming something none of them can name, while a second,
older version of her is glimpsed once, mid-catastrophe, blood on her sleeve, shouting a warning
the boom mic only ever half-catches: *"DON'T LET HIM START—"* This cold open does not resolve
that. It is written as a candidate for what "starting" might mean, seen once, from the one angle
Season 6 (Take 2) has never used: a dream, not a shoot. Nobody holds a camera in this scene. That
absence is the point — the older Hana with blood on her sleeve has always appeared to a camera.
This Hana appears to nobody but the Duck.

## COLD OPEN

```
INT. SERVER ROOM — ICELAND — 1986 (AS DREAMED)
```

*Fluorescent tubes, half of them dead, the other half doing that thing dying fluorescent tubes do
— not flickering, exactly, more like breathing badly. Rows of empty racks. Not powered-down.
Empty. Cable runs still zip-tied to the floor trays, terminating in nothing, like a river system
somebody drained overnight and forgot to tell the riverbed.*

*One machine is still on. A dot-matrix printer, the loud kind, chewing through green-and-white
tractor-feed paper in long, unhurried breaths. It has clearly been running for a while. The
printout has folded itself into a pile on the floor the height of a dictionary and is still
growing.*

*TYLER and HANA are on the floor, backs against a dead rack, the way people wake up who fell
asleep somewhere they didn't mean to fall asleep. Tyler wakes first. He is, unmistakably, having a
good time about it.*

**TYLER**
*(French; SUBTITLED: "Okay. This is new.")*
Bon. Ça, c'est nouveau.

**HANA**
*(French; SUBTITLED: waking, sharp, already assessing)*
Ne bouge pas. Compte les sorties avant de compter quoi que ce soit d'autre.

*(SUBTITLED: "Don't move. Count the exits before you count anything else.")*

*Tyler does not do this. He is already standing, already looking at the printer the way a man
looks at a dessert menu he's been told not to order from.*

**LOWER THIRD (DOC UI, dream-glitched — the font is wrong for the decade, and nobody in the dream
notices):**
```
1986 // ICELAND // "IT TURNS OUT" — the printer knew before they did
```

**TYLER**
*(French; SUBTITLED)*
Le serveur est mort. Les serveurs sont partis. Mais l'imprimante — l'imprimante travaille encore.

*(SUBTITLED: "The server's dead. The servers are gone. But the printer — the printer's still
working.")*

**HANA**
*(French; SUBTITLED, already up, already at the door, checking it, finding it locked from a side
that isn't theirs)*
Tyler.

**TYLER**
*(French; SUBTITLED, not looking away from the paper)*
Attends.

*(SUBTITLED: "Wait.")*

*On the floor by the dead rack: two thermoses, upright, side by side, like someone set them down
on purpose and meant to come back. Hana finds them before Tyler does.*

**HANA**
*(French; SUBTITLED)*
Il y a quelqu'un ici. Ou il y avait quelqu'un ici il y a très peu de temps.

*(SUBTITLED: "Someone's here. Or someone was here very recently.")*

*She opens the first thermos. Steam. Minestrone — thick, orange-red, unmistakably somebody's own
recipe and not a can. Warm enough that it has not had time to stop being warm. The second thermos:
coffee, black, the same temperature.*

**TYLER**
*(French; SUBTITLED, finally looking up from the printout, delighted in the specific way he gets
delighted about things that should worry him more)*
Encore chaud.

*(SUBTITLED: "Still warm.")*

**HANA**
*(French; SUBTITLED, very quietly, not delighted at all)*
C'est ce qui m'inquiète.

*(SUBTITLED: "That's what worries me.")*

*Outside the one window with a working blind: not a parking lot. Snow, a flat black sky doing the
particular non-committal thing an Icelandic sky does at an hour that could be 4 AM or 4 PM, and, at
the edge of frame, a road sign in a language neither of them has any business being able to not
read and somehow reads anyway.*

**HANA**
*(French; SUBTITLED)*
On n'est pas au Michigan.

*(SUBTITLED: "We're not in Michigan.")*

**TYLER**
*(French; SUBTITLED, already crouching by the printout, not really answering her)*
On est en Islande.

*(SUBTITLED: "We're in Iceland.")*

**ON SCREEN (small, dream-quality, the way credits look in a dream — present but unreadable until
you try to read them, then suddenly perfectly legible):**
```
BIRD CORRECTION: PENDING
```

*Tyler lifts the fed-out length of printout with both hands, the way you'd lift something out of
water to see how deep it goes. It is not code he recognizes from any year he's actually lived
through. It is not English. It is barely typography — dense clusters of symbol and small glyph
where the pins on a 1986 dot-matrix head have no business rendering anything this precise, colons
doing work colons don't usually do, one long literal string repeating itself like a keyword.*

```
🌑🟥❓ 🟦❔:🌒 ❗vec PARENA CONSTRUCT 312❗ ➡️ 🌓🌔
🧊❓🟥:🟦❔🌒:🌓❕vec PARENA CONSTRUCT 312 🟩➡️🌔
    ⟶ MECHA_CUBE.CORE // build target: [unresolved]
```

**TYLER**
*(French; SUBTITLED, reading it like a man reading a menu in a restaurant he's certain he's eaten
at before)*
Je ne connais pas ce langage. Et pourtant je le lis.

*(SUBTITLED: "I don't know this language. And yet I'm reading it.")*

**HANA**
*(French; SUBTITLED, closer now, one hand actually on his arm — the first time either of them has
touched anything in this room)*
Tyler. Qu'est-ce que c'est.

*(SUBTITLED: "Tyler. What is it.")*

**TYLER**
*(French; SUBTITLED, quiet, for once actually careful with a sentence)*
Le code source. Du Cube Mécanique.

*(SUBTITLED: "The source code. For the Mecha Cube.")*

*A long pause. The printer keeps feeding paper. It does not care what was just said.*

**HANA**
*(French; SUBTITLED)*
Le Cube n'existe pas encore. Il n'existera pas avant — 

*(SUBTITLED: "The Cube doesn't exist yet. It won't exist for — ")*

**TYLER**
*(French; SUBTITLED, cutting her off, gently)*
Je sais.

*(SUBTITLED: "I know.")*

*(a beat)*

**TYLER (CONT'D)**
*(French; SUBTITLED)*
Et je sais aussi que si je lis ça jusqu'au bout — pas la première ligne, pas la moitié, jusqu'au
bout — la Correction de l'Oiseau se résout. Maintenant. Ce soir. Ici.

*(SUBTITLED: "And I also know that if I read this all the way through — not the first line, not
half of it, all the way through — the Bird Correction resolves. Now. Tonight. Here.")*

*Hana does not let go of his arm.*

**HANA**
*(French; SUBTITLED, and for the first time in the scene her voice does the thing her voice does
in the garage — flat, controlled, the sound of someone who has already decided how this ends and
is trying to get there before the room does)*
Non.

**TYLER**
*(French; SUBTITLED, almost laughing, almost eleven years old about it)*
Hana. Neuf cents ans que ce mot est en attente. "En attente." Et là, ce soir, dans une pièce vide
en Islande, il y a une imprimante qui — 

*(SUBTITLED: "Hana. Nine hundred years that word has been pending. 'Pending.' And here, tonight,
in an empty room in Iceland, there's a printer that — ")*

**HANA**
*(French; SUBTITLED, sharp, final, the exact register she uses on set when she calls a stop)*
Je m'en fous de depuis quand. Je regarde ton visage et je sais déjà à quoi ressemble le mien dans
cette pièce, plus tard, avec du sang sur la manche. Je ne veux pas savoir comment on y arrive. Je
veux juste qu'on n'y arrive pas.

*(SUBTITLED: "I don't care how long it's been pending. I'm looking at your face and I already know
what mine looks like in this room, later, with blood on the sleeve. I don't want to know how we
get there. I just want us not to.")*

*(a very long pause)*

**HANA (CONT'D)**
*(French; SUBTITLED, quiet now)*
S'il te plaît. Ne le lis pas.

*(SUBTITLED: "Please. Don't read it.")*

*Tyler looks at her. Looks at the printout — the last unread length of it still curling out of the
platen, patient, unhurried, in no rush at all, the way something written for him nine hundred years
in advance can afford to be. Looks at her again.*

*He does not finish reading it.*

*He feeds it back in.*

*Not dramatically. Not like a man making a sacrifice. Like a man refolding a map he already read,
the exact gesture — though nothing in this dream knows this — he uses on a hoodie in a factory in
a different, unrelated transcript. He walks the printout back to the feed tray, hand over hand,
and lets the platen take it in reverse, the pins retracting each line the way they laid it down,
the dot-matrix head running backward with a sound like a breath being pulled back in instead of
let out.*

**ON SCREEN, dream-quality, updating in real time:**
```
BIRD CORRECTION: PENDING
BIRD CORRECTION: PENDING (unchanged)
BIRD CORRECTION: PENDING — BY CHOICE
```

**HANA**
*(French; SUBTITLED, very quietly, the first thing she's said that isn't an instruction)*
Merci.

*(SUBTITLED: "Thank you.")*

**TYLER**
*(French; SUBTITLED)*
Ne me remercie pas encore. Je ne sais pas ce que je viens de reporter. Je sais seulement à qui.

*(SUBTITLED: "Don't thank me yet. I don't know what I just deferred. I only know who to.")*

*The printer swallows the last line. Goes quiet. The fluorescent tubes keep breathing badly. Snow,
outside, doing nothing in particular. The two thermoses, still upright, still someone else's.*

*Tyler looks straight into where a lens would be, if there were a lens, which there isn't, which he
seems to notice anyway.*

**TYLER**
*(French; SUBTITLED, the smile — the one everyone always mentions — arriving late, the way it
always does)*
Je crois qu'on nous regarde.

*(SUBTITLED: "I think we're being watched.")*

*He smiles.*

## SMASH CUT

```
INT. BEDROOM — PRESENT DAY — CONTINUOUS
```

**THE DUCK** sits bolt upright, tuxedo rumpled from sleep in a way no duck's tuxedo has any
business getting rumpled, in a cold sweat that should not physically be possible for a duck to
produce and is producing anyway. It breathes hard for a second. Looks at its own wings like it's
checking they're still wings.

**THE DUCK**
*(English, badly shaken, the old-noir affectation completely gone for once)*
Okay. Okay, that was — 

*(it stops)*

*(it tries again, reaching for the line, and the line isn't there)*

**THE DUCK (CONT'D)**
I don't — I was saying something. I was saying something and it wasn't in English and I *knew*
what it meant and now I —

*(a long beat)*

I don't speak French.

*(pause)*

I have never spoken French. I have never had a reason to speak French. I have opinions about a
hoodie and a strong take on my own leaderboard standing and exactly zero French, and forty seconds
ago I was fluent.

*(it sits with this)*

**THE DUCK (CONT'D)**
*(quieter, almost to itself, reaching, not quite landing on it)*
Je ne sais... 

*(it can't finish it)*

*(it doesn't try again)*

**LOWER THIRD, final card:**
```
BIRD CORRECTION: STILL PENDING
THE DUCK REMEMBERS EVERYTHING ABOUT THE DREAM EXCEPT THE ONE LANGUAGE IT WAS FLUENT IN
```
