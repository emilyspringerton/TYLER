# TYLER — VALHANNA, EPISODE 16 — COLD OPEN
## "Non Consigné" ("Unlogged")
### Build 0154 | Einhorn Media | VALHANNA, continued

---

**PRODUCTION NOTE, READ BEFORE THE SCRIPT:**

`lore/day_out_of_time_receipt.md` §III item 4 names the counter-move: *perform the schedule
outwardly, keep your own unlogged day inside it.* Four episodes of waiting have not been that. VH16
is. Each of the four crew members — Cam, the sound guy, the PA, Hana — takes one hour of the
evening for something **unfilmed.**

**Format:** four vignettes, separated by black. The camera is never in the room during any of
them. We get only what the crew says afterward, in one sentence each, to nobody. **The Doc UI
logs the absence and nothing else.** This is the "warmth" episode of the second half (Rule 7);
the realization (that the warmth is not a performance) is in the sentences.

**Not resolved:** what each did; the Bird Correction (PENDING); Entry 23 and PA-001 (words kept).

## COLD OPEN

```
INT. SERVER ROOM — ICELAND — NO YEAR GIVEN
```

*Evening. The light through the door is the same clean line, but the room feels different: the
crew is moving the way people move who have decided something. Hana is by the corridor door with
her map. She unrolls it, looks at it, and rolls it up again without reading it.*

**LOWER THIRD (DOC UI):**
```
TC  — / —  ·  17:30:00
```

**HANA**
*(French; SUBTITLED: "One hour each. Off the record. Nobody follows anybody.")*
Une heure chacun. Hors champ. Personne ne suit personne.

**CAM**
*(French; SUBTITLED: "And the camera?")*
Et la caméra ?

**HANA**
*(French; SUBTITLED: "Leave it running. Pointed at the door.")*
Laissez-la tourner. Pointée sur la porte.

*Cam looks at the rig for a long moment. Then she sets it on its tripod, locks the head, frames the
doorway in a wide, and takes her hand off it. The record light stays on.*

**LOWER THIRD (DOC UI):**
```
OPERATOR: NONE
LOCKED OFF: DOOR
```

*Nobody explains it to the horse. She understands it anyway.*

---

*CUT TO BLACK. Held for four seconds. Room tone, 7.83 under it.*

**LOWER THIRD (DOC UI):**
```
18:00:00 — CAM — UNLOGGED
```

*BLACK. Sound of the room. A door, the corridor one, opening and closing. Silence. After a while, a
second door, the same door, opening and closing.*

**LOWER THIRD (DOC UI):**
```
19:00:00 — CAM — RETURNED
```

*The picture returns. Cam sits in her folding chair, boots off, eating a bar of dark chocolate she
did not have before. She does not look at the camera. Her eyes are red at the rims.*

**CAM**
*(French; SUBTITLED, to nobody: "I called somebody. They picked up.")*
J'ai appelé quelqu'un. On a décroché.

*She breaks off a square and holds it out, without looking, in the direction of the horse. The
horse takes it.*

---

*BLACK.*

**LOWER THIRD (DOC UI):**
```
19:00:01 — SOUND GUY — UNLOGGED
```

*Room tone. Under it, from far off, almost nothing: a long note on a single instrument. We cannot
tell what. It is not in the room. It is somewhere the sound guy has gone.*

**LOWER THIRD (DOC UI):**
```
20:00:00 — SOUND GUY — RETURNED
```

*Picture. The sound guy is sitting against the dead rack with his headphones off. The boom is on the
floor beside him. His hands are open on his knees. He is smiling in the way of a person who has been
alone with a thing he did not want to share and has now shared it with himself.*

**SOUND GUY**
*(French; SUBTITLED, to nobody: "It was in tune.")*
C'était juste.

---

*BLACK.*

**LOWER THIRD (DOC UI):**
```
20:00:01 — THE PA — UNLOGGED
```

*Room tone. A pen scratching, very close. A sound that goes on for a long time. At the end, the
distinct rip of a sheet of paper being torn in half. Then in half again.*

**LOWER THIRD (DOC UI):**
```
21:00:00 — THE PA — RETURNED
```

*Picture. The PA is in his folding chair, clipboard on his lap, a pen in his hand. His eyes are dry. The
folded invoice from VH08 is not in his back pocket. He looks lighter by an inch.*

**PA**
*(French; SUBTITLED, to nobody: "It doesn't fit in my pocket anymore because it isn't there.")*
Ça ne rentre plus dans ma poche parce que ça n'y est plus.

---

*BLACK.*

**LOWER THIRD (DOC UI):**
```
21:00:01 — HANA — UNLOGGED
```

*Room tone. And then — we do not see it — a sound we have not heard in this series: two voices in
the corridor, both hers, one hoarse. We cannot make out the words. We hear them laugh once, together,
in the same breath. We hear a door.*

**LOWER THIRD (DOC UI):**
```
22:00:00 — HANA — RETURNED
```

*Picture. Hana stands in the middle of the room with her hands at her sides. Nothing in her face
has changed except that it is her face. She picks up the thermos she put down in VH13, unscrews it,
and pours a little onto the floor — as one pours out for the absent. The steam goes up in the line
of light.*

**HANA**
*(French; SUBTITLED, to nobody: "She's not angry.")*
Elle n'est pas en colère.

---

*The room, 22:00. Four people, one horse, a camera still locked on the door. They sit down in a
loose circle without being told to, thermoses between them, the three warm ones and their own. Not
one of them has said what they did. Not one of them will.*

**LOWER THIRD (DOC UI), final card:**
```
4 HOURS UNLOGGED
0 HOURS LOST
THE DAY IS STILL NOBODY'S
```

*SMASH TO BLACK.*

*END COLD OPEN.*
