**The calendar.** Thirteen moons of twenty-eight days, each moon a clean four weeks, each week
beginning on a Sunday (VH03's arithmetic, run forward). The weekday names are the French ones, and
the French ones are planetary: *dimanche* the Sun, *lundi* the Moon, *mardi* Mars, *mercredi*
Mercury, *jeudi* Jupiter, *vendredi* Venus, *samedi* Saturn. That matters because the condensed
frequency table in `README.md` §XII is also keyed to planets, and the two lists line up:

Mars has no row in the condensed table. On Tuesdays the Doc UI prints NO ENTRY and the instruments
read nothing (see VH27). The series bible's epigraph is "Tyler, Detroit, Tuesday, year unknown";
the Auditor read The Field on a Tuesday and did not finish his sandwich; the Duck says it is always
Tuesday in its town. This is why.

**New Doc UI lines, kept for the whole block:** the DAY BANNER (weekday, spirit, Hz); the GAP

**This episode's job is a welcome.** Nobody asks Tyler what he is; Law 1 is kept by hospitality
here, not by discipline: nobody asks. He asks for one thing, that his name not be written, and
gets it. The Doc UI blanks the field the way the Auditor's badge is blank; the field goes to

**Not resolved:** the tenth ingredient; the sheet in the tray; Entry 25 (VH); the white on Cam's

## COLD OPEN

```
INT. SERVER ROOM — ICELAND — NO YEAR GIVEN
```

*The same tubes, breathing the same way. Seven folding chairs in the places the inspector left
them: six in an arc, one apart, facing the doorway that is only a doorway now. In the seventh,
Tyler. He has not slept. He holds the green thermos in his lap the way one holds a sleeping animal,
with both hands and no pressure, and the horse's head rests on his knee beside it, eyes half
shut. In the sixth chair, the inspector's own, the Duck sits upright and asleep, tuxedo collar up,
a white thermos on its lap. Four more chairs, four more people, in the postures of a unit that has
slept where it sat.*

**LOWER THIRD (DOC UI):**
```
DAY 01 · DIMANCHE · BAEL 0.33 HZ
TC  01 / 01  ·  07:02:11
GAP  0.00 HZ                  UI SPACING: STANDARD
```

*In the corner of the overlay, where a subject's line has always been, one line sits in the UI's
most formal type:*

```
SUBJECT: TYLER — PRESENT
```

*Hana is awake. She has been awake for some time with a cup between her hands that she is not
drinking from. She reads the line. Then she looks at him.*

**HANA**
*(French; SUBTITLED: "No names.")*
Pas de noms.

**TYLER**
*(French; SUBTITLED: "Say it if you like. Just don't write it.")*
Dis-le si tu veux. Mais ne l'écris pas.

*He is not looking at the overlay. He is looking at the thermos in his lap, as if the thermos had
asked.*

**TYLER (CONT'D)**
*(French; SUBTITLED: "A name said is a gift. A name written is a deed.")*
Un nom dit est un cadeau. Un nom écrit est un acte de propriété.

*Nobody answers. The sound guy, who has been awake and pretending otherwise, lifts his head. Cam
has the rig on her knee, and she does not raise it; she looks at the monitor on the side of it,
where the line is also written. Hana says it to the room, flat, the way she says "keep it wide":*

**HANA**
*(French; SUBTITLED: "Blank it.")*
Effacez-le.

**LOWER THIRD (DOC UI):**
```
FIELD: BLANKED AT THE SUBJECT'S REQUEST
SUBJECT: SEAT 7 — PRESENT
```

*The PA, who has been drawing a seating plan on the back of the call sheet, adds a small box and a
numeral, and writes nothing in it.*

**PRODUCER (O.S., from the thermos, very light, a half-step off)**
*(French; SUBTITLED: "Is it all right that I'm on his knee?")*
Est-ce que ça va, que je sois sur son genou ?

**TYLER**
*(French; SUBTITLED: "I've had worse guests on worse knees.")*
J'ai eu de pires invités sur de pires genoux.

*The Gap Meter sits on the equipment case where the sound guy left it, its brass cooled, its
needle on the pin. He glances at it. He taps the case once with a knuckle. Zero.*

---

*Three raps on the corridor door. Not on the wood; on the air beside it.*

*The Auditor comes in sideways with the pot. The pot is wider than the doorway. It comes through
the doorway. Behind it the man himself, white hair uncombed, short sleeves in a room that is not
warm, the circuit-flower on his pocket, a ladle through his belt like a sword. It is Sunday. He
makes it every Sunday, and has for thirty years or for as long as nobody remembers.*

**THE AUDITOR**
*(French, wrong in the gender of everything but the soup; SUBTITLED: "Good morning! Don't get up.")*
Bonjour ! Ne vous levez pas.

*Tyler gets up. He sets the thermos on the seat of the chair, upright, tends the lid, and wipes his
right hand on the napkin on the armrest, carefully, front and back and between each finger and
back again, a full three seconds longer than a hand needs. Cam's eyes go to it. She says nothing.*

**TYLER**
*(French; SUBTITLED: "I'd rather get up.")*
Je préfère me lever.

*They shake hands. The Auditor does it with his whole arm. His delight is not a mask over anything.*

**THE AUDITOR**
*(French; SUBTITLED: "I've been told a great deal about you.")*
On m'a beaucoup parlé de vous.

**TYLER**
*(French; SUBTITLED: "Not by me.")*
Pas par moi.

**THE AUDITOR**
*(French; SUBTITLED: "That's what I liked.")*
C'est ce qui m'a plu.

---

*He sets the pot on the floor in the middle of the arc and counts the chairs aloud with the
ladle, the way a conductor counts a band in. Cam. The sound guy. The PA. The fourth, with nothing
on it. Hana. The Duck in the sixth. The seventh, empty now, the thermos upright on its seat.*

**THE AUDITOR**
*(French; SUBTITLED: "Seven. The list says seven. I'm one short.")*
Sept. La liste dit sept. Il m'en manque une.

**PA**
*(French; SUBTITLED: "Who's on the list?")*
Qui est sur la liste ?

*The Auditor counts on his fingers, grave, enjoying it.*

**THE AUDITOR**
*(French; SUBTITLED: "The camera. The ears. The clipboard. The lady. The radio. The gentleman.
The duck. Seven.")*
La caméra. Les oreilles. Le bloc-notes. La dame. La radio. Le monsieur. Le canard. Sept.

*The horse lifts her head and gives him a long look.*

**THE AUDITOR (CONT'D)**
*(French; SUBTITLED: "The lady on four legs doesn't take a chair. Forgive me.")*
La dame à quatre pattes ne prend pas de chaise. Pardonnez-moi.

*The horse puts her head back down. She forgives him.*

**THE AUDITOR (CONT'D)**
*(French; SUBTITLED: "Thursday. On Thursdays there are eight of us. I'll bring it Thursday.")*
Jeudi. Le jeudi, on est huit. Je l'apporterai jeudi.

---

*He lifts the lid. The steam goes up as steam does, and then, as it did on the first day, it
leans. It crosses the arc in one long ribbon toward the seventh chair, past Hana, past the Duck,
and arrives. It does not hover over the empty seat. It settles over the thermos on the seat, and
over the man standing beside it, and stays there the way a cat stays.*

*The Auditor looks at the steam. He looks at Tyler. He ladles a bowl and hands it over without a
word, and a spoon — and there is no spoon.*

**TYLER**
*(French; SUBTITLED: "May I borrow a spoon?")*
Je peux emprunter une cuillère ?

*The PA has one on his belt: tin, bent, his own, the one he stirs his coffee with. He unclips it.
He holds it out and does not let go of it at once.*

**PA**
*(French; SUBTITLED: "It's mine. I want it back.")*
Elle est à moi. Je la veux en retour.

**TYLER**
*(French; SUBTITLED: "Naturally.")*
Naturellement.

**LOWER THIRD (DOC UI):**
```
DEBT: ONE (1) SPOON (SMALL) — PA → SEAT 7
```

*The PA writes it on the clipboard with the satisfaction of a man opening an account at a bank he
trusts.*

---

*Tyler sits. He tastes. The whole unit pretends to look elsewhere and does not manage it. He holds
the spoon in his mouth a moment longer than a spoon needs.*

**TYLER**
*(French; SUBTITLED: "It's missing the —")*
Il manque le —

*He stops. The sentence sits in the air with its last word on the other side of it.*

*The Auditor has gone still, ladle in hand. Of all the things he has done since coming through that
door, this is the first that is not a performance of ease. He looks at Tyler for a long moment,
kindly, the way a man looks at someone who has just walked up to a locked room and put a hand on
the door.*

**THE AUDITOR**
*(French; SUBTITLED, very gently: "Not in front of the lens.")*
Pas devant l'objectif.

*Tyler nods. He swallows. He does not finish.*

**THE AUDITOR (CONT'D)**
*(French, to Cam; SUBTITLED: "It isn't against you.")*
Ce n'est pas contre vous.

**CAM**
*(French; SUBTITLED, level: "I know.")*
Je sais.

*She lowers the rig a few degrees. She does not turn it off. The red light stays on.*

---

*Afternoon, if the tubes were a clock. The pot is a third empty. The Duck has woken, said "Ah.",
and gone back to its chair. Tyler has taken out the coin.*

*Nobody has seen it yet. It is old and small and has no particular color. He sets it on the
thumbnail the way a man sets a sentence on the tongue, and flips it, and the room does a thing it
does not do for anything else: it holds.*

**LOWER THIRD (DOC UI), a half-second late, the way it is always late:**
```
COIN: 01 / 01 — CAUGHT LATE (0.4 S)
```

*He has caught it without looking, and pockets it, and the date it showed is gone.*

**SOUND GUY**
*(French; SUBTITLED: "What does it say?")*
Qu'est-ce qu'elle dit ?

**TYLER**
*(French; SUBTITLED: "Today. That's new.")*
Aujourd'hui. C'est nouveau.

**CAM**
*(French; SUBTITLED: "It gives days now?")*
Elle donne des jours, maintenant ?

**TYLER**
*(French; SUBTITLED: "It gives what you ask it for.")*
Elle donne ce qu'on lui demande.

*For the length of that line his eyes have come up to the rig. They rest on the lens. Then they
move.*

**LOWER THIRD (DOC UI):**
```
LENS CONTACT (SEAT 7): 1.9 S
```

**SOUND GUY**
*(French; SUBTITLED: "One point nine.")*
Un virgule neuf.

**CAM**
*(French; SUBTITLED, flat: "Two seconds is a taunt.")*
Deux secondes, c'est une provocation.

**TYLER**
*(French; SUBTITLED: "One point nine is manners.")*
Un virgule neuf, c'est de la politesse.

*Hana laughs. It is short and it surprises her. She puts the cup down for the first time that day.*

---

*Evening, nominally. The bowls are stacked. The horse is asleep on her feet. The sound guy has
the headphones half on, listening to the thing under the floor.*

**SOUND GUY**
*(French; SUBTITLED, low: "Seven eighty-three. Steady.")*
Sept quatre-vingt-trois. Stable.

*He frowns.*

**SOUND GUY (CONT'D)**
*(French; SUBTITLED: "It's humming in seven-eight and in eleven-eight at once. It doesn't come out
even. It doesn't land.")*
Il fredonne en sept-huit et en onze-huit en même temps. Ça ne tombe pas juste. Ça ne se résout pas.

**TYLER**
*(French; SUBTITLED, mild: "I know.")*
Je sais.

*He is wiping his hands again. The napkin. Front and back, between the fingers, and he has just
begun the second pass when he looks up and finds Cam looking.*

*He stops.*

*He puts the napkin down on his knee, as it falls: crumpled, unfolded, ordinary. He leaves it
there. For the first time since he walked in, nothing about his hands is being done for anyone.*

**LOWER THIRD (DOC UI):**
```
TELL (HANDS): ABSENT
```

*Cam sees it. She does not say it aloud. She takes the notebook from her jacket and writes
something, one line, and we see the pen and not the page.*

---

*Night. The tubes are down to a third. The Auditor has gone, and left the pot, empty and rinsed, to be
collected on Thursday, and the ladle on the arm of the seventh chair — a courtesy, or the first thing Tyler owes him; the PA's clipboard
says so in his handwriting under SPOON.*

*Everyone has slept where they sat except Cam, and Tyler. He is humming, very low, and he is not
performing that either. He is looking at nothing. His hand is on the horse's neck.*

*The timecode runs toward morning.*

**LOWER THIRD (DOC UI):**
```
TC  01 / 01  ·  05:59:58
```

*05:59:59. 06:00:00.*

**LOWER THIRD (DOC UI), flipping, the banner changing in place the way a clock does:**
```
DAY 02 · LUNDI · AMON 1.618 HZ
TC  01 / 02  ·  06:00:01
GAP  0.99 HZ
```

*On the equipment case the needle has moved. One hair. It is a very small thing, and the room
makes no sound about it.*

*Tyler sees it. His humming stops for exactly one beat. He looks at the Gap Meter, and at the case,
and then up, to the rig, where Cam is awake behind the lens with her eye open. She has seen him
see it.*

**TYLER**
*(French; SUBTITLED, easy: "Good morning.")*
Bonjour.

*He does not say anything about the needle. She does not ask. It is the second agreement of the
day, and nobody has written it down.*

*SMASH TO BLACK.*

*END COLD OPEN.*
