# TYLER — VALHANNA, EPISODE 22 — COLD OPEN
## "Le Canard Sait" ("The Duck Knows")
### Build 0160 | Einhorn Media | VALHANNA, continued

---

**PRODUCTION NOTE, READ BEFORE THE SCRIPT:**

VH01 ended with the Duck waking in a cold sweat, in English, with no memory of French. VH11 had the
Duck narrating in noir English and asking the audience to "make sure the tape holds." VH22 is the
Duck's turn. **It remembers French.** Not all of it. One sentence.

**Why this matters to the structure:** the whole opening has been the Duck's dream (VH01 framing).
If the Duck can now dream in a language it cannot speak awake, then the language is leaking out of
the dream, and the dream is leaking out of the server room. The episode is the Duck finding out
that its dream has a door.

**Language:** English for the Duck's waking scenes. French for the one sentence, SUBTITLED.

**Not resolved:** whose dream the room is; the Bird Correction (PENDING).

## COLD OPEN

```
INT. BEDROOM — PRESENT DAY — NIGHT
```

*The Duck is sitting up in bed. Where the wall calendar hung there is still a rectangle of cleaner plaster; the thirteen pages lie
squared on the nightstand, and a folding chair leans closed against the wall. The Duck is not awake in the way it was in VH03. It is awake the way a person is
awake who has been woken by a sound in the next room and is waiting for it to repeat.*

**LOWER THIRD (DOC UI):**
```
DOCUMENTARY UNIT B // THE DUCK // TC — / —
```

**THE DUCK**
*(English, quietly, to nobody)*
Tape's holding.

*It rubs its bill. It swings its feet to the floor. It looks at the clock on the nightstand. The
clock has a face, black hands, white. 4:21. The Duck stares at it for several seconds.*

**THE DUCK (CONT'D)**
*(English)*
That's a long time to be four twenty-one.

*It gets up, walks to the window, and parts the blind with two feathers. Outside: the street light,
the parked cars. A man across the road is walking a dog, slowly, in the way of someone who has no
other place to be at this hour. The Duck watches him. And out of its own mouth, unbidden:*

**THE DUCK (CONT'D)**
*(French; SUBTITLED: "Somebody is about to arrive, and we are very glad.")*
Quelqu'un va arriver, et nous en sommes très heureux.

*The Duck stops. It puts a feather to its bill. It repeats it, in English, to hear it.*

**THE DUCK (CONT'D)**
"Somebody is about to arrive, and we are very glad."

*It turns slowly from the window.*

**THE DUCK (CONT'D)**
That's not my sentence.

---

**SMASH CUT**

```
INT. SERVER ROOM — ICELAND — NO YEAR GIVEN
```

*Cam, the PA, Hana in the room. The horse standing. Cam, with the rig on her shoulder, stops
mid-step and turns her head toward nothing. The sound guy — out of frame — says "Did anyone —" and
trails off.*

**LOWER THIRD (DOC UI):**
```
AUDIO: FRENCH (1 SENTENCE) — SOURCE: UNLOGGED (DREAM → ROOM)
```

**CAM**
*(French; SUBTITLED, quiet: "Somebody is about to arrive, and we are very glad.")*
Quelqu'un va arriver, et nous en sommes très heureux.

*She says it in the Duck's voice. Not an imitation; the sentence has just travelled. The PA lets his
pen go. Hana does not look surprised. She looks like a person who has heard the thing she was
waiting to hear.*

---

**CUT BACK**

```
INT. BEDROOM — PRESENT DAY — NIGHT
```

*The Duck is sitting on the edge of the bed with its feet not touching the floor. It has a thermos
on its knee. It does not remember how it got there. Steam rises from it.*

**THE DUCK**
*(English)*
Oh, you're joking.

*It unscrews the cap and sniffs. Its face changes: relief, grief, and something older than either.
It does not drink it. It holds the thermos in both wings, and holds it, and holds it, the way you
hold a thing you were handed to keep warm.*

**THE DUCK (CONT'D)**
*(English, to the thermos)*
Okay. I'll bring it.

**LOWER THIRD (DOC UI), final card:**
```
THE DUCK REMEMBERS ONE SENTENCE
THE DUCK IS HOLDING A THERMOS
THE DREAM HAS A DOOR
```
