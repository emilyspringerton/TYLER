---

**PRODUCTION NOTE, READ BEFORE THE SCRIPT:**

Tuesday again: NO ENTRY, the day the Field's table has no row and no instrument reads Tyler. On
the first Tuesday he told a story (VH27). On this one he asks for the camera.

**The series' epigraph** is this episode's last third: *"If you keep filming, you become part of
it."* (`README.md`, attributed to Tyler, Detroit, Tuesday, year unknown.) The line has been
sitting at the head of the bible since Build 0002. It has never been said on screen. It is said here,
to the person it was always going to be said to, in the way it was always going to be meant: **as
a compliment.**

**Form.** The camera is Tyler's. The frame is his eye. He is never in shot (except at the very end,
when he turns the lens on himself, once, for 1.8 seconds). The Doc UI changes its labels to match:
`OPERATOR: SEAT 7`. For the first time in the series the unit looks *into* the lens, and the lens
looks back.

**The cost.** The camera is lent on **red** (VH26): "yes, and it costs," and Cam names the cost out
loud. Law 5 (small debts as wards): the debt is not small. It is the kind that can never quite be
paid, which is why it is a good ward.

**What he knows.** He knows what each of them takes: tea, coffee, how. This is not a trick. It is the
oldest thing in the repo: `episodes/s07e06_what_stays.md`, Vienna, "*presence is what still
knows you.*" A man at Café Bernstein who brought everyone's coffee without being asked.

**Not resolved:** the cost; the sentence the PA is waiting for; the tenth ingredient.

*Cam, alone by the dead rack, with the rig across her knees, is taking the lens cloth to the front
element in slow circles. Tyler has come up beside her. He has stopped a respectful distance away,
as a man stops at a counter he has not yet been served at.*

**TYLER**
*(French; SUBTITLED: "May I borrow the camera?")*
Je peux emprunter la caméra ?

*Her hand stops on the glass. She does not look at him. She looks at the five strips of tape across the
lens hood: black, green, red, yellow, white.*

**CAM**
*(French; SUBTITLED, after a while: "Red.")*
Rouge.

**TYLER**
*(French; SUBTITLED: "It costs.")*
Ça coûte.

**CAM**
*(French; SUBTITLED: "Someday, tell me how it ends.")*
Un jour, dis-moi comment ça finit.

*It is not "finish a sentence." It is not a demand. She has chosen with great care what she would
ask for if she could ask for anything.*

*He considers it. The sound that the tubes make seems to consider it with him.*

**TYLER**
*(French; SUBTITLED: "I owe you that.")*
Je te dois ça.

*She holds the rig out. He takes it with both hands, a man given a sleeping animal. He rests it on his

*The picture swings, dips, rights itself. We see the way Tyler sees. It is not steadier than Cam. It is

---

*The first subject. The PA, on the stool by the table, with the clipboard on his knee and a cup in
his hand. The lens finds him. He stiffens.*

**TYLER (O.S., behind the lens)**
*(French; SUBTITLED: "What do you take?")*
Qu'est-ce que tu prends ?

**PA**
*(French; SUBTITLED, wary: "In what?")*
Dans quoi ?

**TYLER (O.S.)**
*(French; SUBTITLED: "Coffee. One sugar. Oat milk. Three sugars on the days you're lying to yourself.")*
Du café. Un sucre. Du lait d'avoine. Trois sucres les jours où tu te mens.

**PA**
*(French; SUBTITLED: "I'm not lying to myself.")*
Je ne me mens pas.

**TYLER (O.S.)**
*(French; SUBTITLED: "Today, no. Today it's tea.")*
Aujourd'hui, non. Aujourd'hui, c'est du thé.

*The PA looks down at his cup. It is tea. He does not remember pouring it. He looks back at the lens
with a face that has been seen.*

*The camera moves on. The sound guy, the headphones around his neck.*

**TYLER (O.S.)**
*(French; SUBTITLED: "A bay-leaf infusion. You tell nobody because it smells like soup.")*
Une tisane à la feuille de laurier. Tu ne le dis à personne parce que ça sent la soupe.

**SOUND GUY**
*(French; SUBTITLED, quietly delighted: "It does smell like soup.")*
Ça sent la soupe.

*Hana.*

**TYLER (O.S.)**
*(French; SUBTITLED: "Black. Only after three p.m. Before that you pretend you don't want it.")*
Noir. Seulement après quinze heures. Avant, tu fais comme si tu n'en voulais pas.

**HANA**
*(French; SUBTITLED, flat: "It's nine-fifteen.")*
Il est neuf heures quinze.

**TYLER (O.S.)**
*(French; SUBTITLED: "And you've been looking at the pot since eight.")*
Et tu regardes la cafetière depuis huit heures.

*She looks away. She does not deny it. The lens holds her face a moment longer, and she lets it.*

---

*The Duck, on the high stool by the thermos.*

**TYLER (O.S.)**
*(French; SUBTITLED: "A finger of whatever's in the glass. Never the same twice.")*
Un doigt de ce qu'il y a dans le verre. Jamais la même chose deux fois.

**DUCK**
*(English)*
I've been told I'm hard to buy for.

*The horse, standing a little apart, who has been watching the lens with the particular lack of interest of a
mare who is not at all disinterested.*

**TYLER (O.S.)**
*(French; SUBTITLED: "Apples. But she prefers to be asked.")*
Des pommes. Mais elle préfère qu'on lui demande.

*She puts her nose to the glass. The lens fogs. The picture is, for a moment, only the breath of
a horse.*

*The tea thermos on the fourth chair.*

**TYLER (O.S.)**
*(French; SUBTITLED: "Green tea. Nothing in it. For three hundred years.")*
Du thé vert. Rien dedans. Depuis trois cents ans.

**PRODUCER (O.S.)**
*(French; SUBTITLED, a thread of a voice: "Is it that obvious?")*
Est-ce si évident ?

*And the eighth chair, on the line. The older Hana with her sleeve on the table. She is looking directly
into the lens before it reaches her, as if she had known for a long time that it would.*

**TYLER (O.S.)**
*(French; SUBTITLED, gently: "Coffee. Stronger than hers. In the chipped cup.")*
Du café. Plus fort que le sien. Dans la tasse ébréchée.

**OLDER HANA**
*(French; SUBTITLED, a half-second late: "There was a chipped cup.")*
Il y avait une tasse ébréchée.

**TYLER (O.S.)**
*(French; SUBTITLED: "There'll be one.")*
Il y en aura une.

*The lens does not linger. She watches it go. Something in her sleeve — the only part of her that moves —
relaxes by a fraction.*

---

*Cam is the last. She is in her chair, a little apart, her hands empty in her lap, looking at nothing
the way she looks at nothing when the camera is in her hands. They are empty now. The lens finds
her, and for once there is nothing in front of it for her to hide behind.*

Rien. Tu tiens une tasse pour avoir quelque chose à tenir.

**CAM**
*(French; SUBTITLED: "Nobody ever told me that.")*
Personne ne me l'avait dit.

**TYLER (O.S.)**
*(French; SUBTITLED: "I know.")*

*She looks into the lens. It is not a decision. It is the thing the eye does when it is looked at by a

**TYLER (O.S.)**
*(French; SUBTITLED, gentle, the line that has been at the head of the bible since Build 0002:
"If you keep filming, you become part of it.")*
Si tu continues de filmer, tu en fais partie.

*Her eyes do not leave the lens.*

**CAM**
*(French; SUBTITLED: "I know.")*
Je sais.

**TYLER (O.S.)**
*(French; SUBTITLED: "No. I mean it as a compliment.")*
Non. Je le dis comme un compliment.

**CAM**
*(French; SUBTITLED, barely: "Why?")*
Pourquoi ?

**TYLER (O.S.)**
*(French; SUBTITLED: "Because the ones who aren't part of it don't look like they know what they're looking at.")*
Parce que ceux qui n'en font pas partie n'ont pas l'air de savoir ce qu'ils regardent.

*A silence. The lens does not move. We hear the tubes. We hear someone breathing who is not Tyler.*

**TYLER (O.S.) (CONT'D)**
*(French; SUBTITLED: "A man in Vienna used to know what everyone took. He never asked.")*
À Vienne, il y avait un homme qui savait ce que chacun prenait. Il ne demandait jamais.

**CAM**
*(French; SUBTITLED: "What happened to him?")*
Qu'est-ce qui lui est arrivé ?

**TYLER (O.S.)**
*(French; SUBTITLED: "He's still there. That's what staying is.")*
Il est toujours là. C'est ça, rester.

*She lowers her eyes. She nods. She is crying a little, in the discreet way of someone who has been
trained to keep her face still for the sake of a picture, and she lets the picture take it.*

and steadies on a face that has rarely been in the shot it is making.*

*He looks straight into the lens.*
