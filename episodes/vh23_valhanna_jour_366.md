**PRODUCTION NOTE, READ BEFORE THE SCRIPT:**

VH03 left one question deliberately open: *whether this is the 365th day or the 366th.* The
arithmetic of the receipt (`lore/day_out_of_time_receipt.md`) says a leap year has **two** unowned
days, and the Cataloguer declined to say which one the transcript meant. VH23 does not answer it by
decree. It answers it by letting the first day end.

**The day does not end at midnight (VH19).** It ends at some moment later, which the unit does not
choose. When it ends, it does not become a date. It becomes the second one.

**The thing the episode is for:** Hana's "until I say" (VH13). The present Hana says it. The Doc UI
finally gets its two-shot.

**Not resolved:** who is at the door; the Bird Correction (PENDING).

## COLD OPEN

```
INT. SERVER ROOM — ICELAND — NO YEAR GIVEN
```

*The long grey hour that is called dawn in rooms without windows. The crew has slept in shifts.
The horse is awake, tail swishing, watching the door. The Gap Meter on the equipment case reads a
number the sound guy has stopped saying aloud, because it keeps going down.*

**LOWER THIRD (DOC UI):**
```
TC  — / —  ·  05:59:58
GAP METER: 3.14 HZ
```

*At 06:00:00 the doorway goes out. Not dim. Out. The line of light on the floor is gone. The room
is the room, tubes breathing, one color.*

**LOWER THIRD (DOC UI):**
```
TC  — / —  ·  06:00:00
THE DAY ENDED
```

**PA**
*(French; SUBTITLED: "It's over.")*
C'est fini.

**HANA**
*(French; SUBTITLED: "It's the first one. It's over.")*
C'est le premier. C'est fini.

*Nobody moves. And then — a quarter-second after the first light goes — a second line begins on the
floor. Thinner, cooler, pale blue. The doorway again, a few degrees off true. A second doorway, in the same frame.*

**LOWER THIRD (DOC UI):**
```
TC  — / —  ·  06:00:01
DAY 366
```

**SOUND GUY**
*(French; SUBTITLED: "There were two.")*
Il y en avait deux.

**CAM**
*(French; SUBTITLED: "In a leap year.")*
Dans une année bissextile.

**PA**
*(French; SUBTITLED, a breath of laughter: "He left the leap-year line in on purpose.")*
Il a laissé la ligne de l'année bissextile exprès.

*Nobody asks who "he" is. The PA looks quickly at his clipboard, where a new row has appeared at the
bottom of the call sheet from VH03. It is blank in the same way.*

```
DAY 365  ——
DAY 366  ——
```

---

*A hand on the doorframe. The olive sleeve, the stained cuff. The wood creaks as the weight shifts.
And this time the face comes after it.*

*She steps into the pale blue light. The older Hana. Hair grey at the temple; a cut above the ear
stitched with three black stitches, healed to a ridge; eyes the same as Hana's, with something
laid under them that years laid there. She stops at the threshold. She does not cross the tape.*

**OLDER HANA**
*(French; SUBTITLED: "I'm not coming in. I'm only saying hello.")*
Je n'entre pas. Je dis seulement bonjour.

*The present Hana is already walking. Past the PA, past the horse. She stops at the line of the
tape, face to face with her across it. The distance between them is six feet.*

**HANA**
*(French; SUBTITLED, steady: "Now.")*
Maintenant.

**CAM**
*(French; SUBTITLED: "Are you sure?")*
Tu es sûre ?

**HANA**
*(French; SUBTITLED: "It's the only day left that isn't anyone's. I'm saying it today.")*
C'est le seul jour qui reste qui n'est à personne. Je le dis aujourd'hui.

*Cam, behind her, lifts the camera. She has to turn her whole body to get both of them. The
viewfinder holds. The Doc UI, for the first time, does not go red.*

**LOWER THIRD (DOC UI):**
```
SUBJECT COUNT: 2
HANA / HANA
FRAMING: GRANTED
```

---

*A two-shot, the first of the series: the two women, one in the room and one in the doorway, six
feet apart, the tape between them, the light on one face blue and on the other, from the tubes,
white.*

**OLDER HANA**
*(French; SUBTITLED: "You know what the verb is.")*
Tu sais ce qu'est le verbe.

**HANA**
*(French; SUBTITLED: "Stop.")*
Arrêter.

**OLDER HANA**
*(French; SUBTITLED: "I've told you, you'll know when you're me. You're not. You decided not to be.")*
Je te l'ai dit : tu sauras quand tu seras moi. Tu ne l'es pas. Tu as décidé de ne pas l'être.

**HANA**
*(French; SUBTITLED: "Somebody has to stay the one who's here.")*
Il faut bien que quelqu'un reste celle qui est là.

*The older Hana's face does a thing. It is not grief. It is gratitude and fatigue. She nods once.
She puts the stained hand to her own sleeve, to the cuff, and for a moment it looks as though she
will say what it is.*

*She doesn't. She turns, and goes back out through the pale door, and the blue line closes.*

**LOWER THIRD (DOC UI), final card:**
```
DAY 366: ENDED AT 06:03:11
TC  — / —  ·  06:03:12
```

*A dot-matrix chatter from the printer. A single sheet falls in the tray.*

*SMASH TO BLACK.*

*END COLD OPEN.*
