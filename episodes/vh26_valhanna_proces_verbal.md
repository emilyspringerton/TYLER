### Build 0164 | Einhorn Media | VALHANNA, continued — MOON 1, DAY 2

---

**PRODUCTION NOTE, READ BEFORE THE SCRIPT:**

Monday is the Moon's day (Amon #7, 1.618 Hz, *reconciliation of opposites*; `README.md` §III:
"faction treaty scenes"). The unit's first treaty is with itself. VH21's wall established that the
crew's decisions arrive on the north wall as stencil; this episode is the first time they meet in
order to make one.

**Form.** The episode is the PA's minutes, read aloud at the end of the day in a passive voice he was
never trained in and has mastered anyway. He has never worked for the Syndicate, and he is better at
it than they were. The scene plays under the reading. The minutes are in French; the SUBTITLES gloss them.

**The Producer** is a guest, and a guest may speak only when recognized. That the Producer asks
only questions here is Robert's Rules, not a regression: `README.md` §IX says the Producer "speaks
only in questions," and VH02–VH24 let him make statements because the project he was running
ended at Memo #081. A guest is allowed to ask. A guest is not allowed to move.

**The consent tape.** Five colors of gaffer tape come out of the PA's belt, in the wrong order,
and are put into the right one by reasoning rather than instruction. The right order is not a
secret. It is the logic of consent: **a refusal has to come before a yes, or the yes means nothing.**
Nobody in the room knows this is also the order of something else. The reader of `manuscripts/
tyler_book2.md` and S06b E0–E1 will.

**Not resolved:** who the older Hana votes with when she votes; what the tape order is for.

*The Auditor's pot, emptied and turned upside down on the floor, has become a table. Six chairs are
drawn up to it. Hana sits at the head with a spoon in her hand — not the PA's; her own. She raps
the pot. Rap. Rap. Rap. It rings.*

**PA (V.O., reading the minutes, flat, perfect)**
*(French; SUBTITLED: "MINUTES. Unit A, regular meeting number one. Moon one, day two. The meeting was
called to order at ten o'clock. The Chair took the chair.")*
PROCÈS-VERBAL. Unité A, réunion ordinaire numéro un. Lune un, jour deux. La séance a été ouverte à
dix heures. La présidente a pris la présidence.

*Hana looks around the table.*

**PA (V.O.)**
*(French; SUBTITLED: "Present: the Camera, the Ears, the Secretary, the Chair, Seat Seven, one duck.
One horse attended as an observer. The Producer attended as a guest.")*
Présents : la Caméra, les Oreilles, le Secrétaire, la Présidente, le Siège Sept, un canard. Un cheval
a assisté en qualité d'observateur. Le Producteur a assisté en qualité d'invité.

*On the fourth chair there is nothing. The green thermos is on Tyler's knee again. Nobody has
mentioned it.*

**PA (V.O.)**
*(French; SUBTITLED: "A quorum was questioned. The Chair asked whether the corridor was present.")*
Le quorum a été mis en question. La présidente a demandé si le couloir était présent.

**OLDER HANA (O.S., corridor, close to the door, hoarse)**
*(French; SUBTITLED: "Present.")*
Présente.

**PA (V.O.)**
*(French; SUBTITLED: "The Chair recognized the corridor. The Chair was observed to look at the
floor for some time afterward.")*
La présidente a reconnu le couloir. On a observé que la présidente a regardé le sol assez longtemps
ensuite.

---

*ITEM ONE.*

**PA (V.O.)**
*(French; SUBTITLED: "Item one. The Camera moved to amend rule two of the wall, 'The camera runs.
Always.'")*
Point un. La Caméra a proposé de modifier la règle deux du mur : « La caméra tourne. Toujours. »

*Cam, on her chair, with the rig across her knees and the red light on.*

**CAM**
*(French; SUBTITLED: "I move that it say what we actually do.")*
Je propose qu'il dise ce qu'on fait vraiment.

**HANA**
*(French; SUBTITLED: "Which is?")*
C'est-à-dire ?

**CAM**
*(French; SUBTITLED: "It runs. And on the first day Seat Seven asked me not to put his name in the
field, and I didn't. The wall says always. We don't do always.")*
Elle tourne. Et le premier jour le Siège Sept m'a demandé de ne pas mettre son nom dans le champ, et
je ne l'ai pas fait. Le mur dit toujours. On ne fait pas toujours.

*The PA's voice reads under it without a hitch:*

**PA (V.O.)**
*(French; SUBTITLED: "The motion was seconded by Seat Seven, who stated that he would like to be
able to say no.")*
La motion a été appuyée par le Siège Sept, qui a déclaré qu'il aimerait pouvoir dire non.

**HANA**
*(French; SUBTITLED: "To the camera?")*
À la caméra ?

**TYLER**
*(French; SUBTITLED: "To the person holding it.")*
À la personne qui la tient.

*The table is quiet. Cam's face doesn't change.*

**CAM**
*(French; SUBTITLED, a beat: "Then say it.")*
Alors dis-le.

**TYLER**
*(French; SUBTITLED, easy, entirely serious: "No.")*
Non.

*She takes her hand off the rig and lets it fall to her knee, lens down, the red light still lit.
It is the whole of the gesture. It is understood.*

**CAM**
*(French; SUBTITLED: "Thank you.")*
Merci.

**TYLER**
*(French; SUBTITLED: "Thank you.")*
Merci.

**PA (V.O.)**
*(French; SUBTITLED: "It was noted that this was the first time either party had said no and meant it
as a courtesy.")*
On a noté que c'était la première fois qu'une des deux parties disait non en le pensant comme une
politesse.

---

*ITEM TWO. The PA reaches into his belt and the table gets five rolls of gaffer tape, thrown down
in the order they came out. He lays them in a row.*

**PA (V.O.)**
*(French; SUBTITLED: "Item two. The Secretary offered five colors of tape and asked that they stand
for something.")*
Point deux. Le Secrétaire a proposé cinq couleurs de ruban adhésif et demandé qu'elles signifient
quelque chose.

*Green. Black. Red. White. Yellow.*

**CAM**
*(French; SUBTITLED: "Black is no. A hand on the lens.")*
Le noir, c'est non. Une main sur l'objectif.

**TYLER**
*(French; SUBTITLED: "Red is yes, and it costs. You say what it costs. It's a small debt.")*
Le rouge, c'est oui, et ça coûte. On dit combien. C'est une petite dette.

**SOUND GUY**
*(French; SUBTITLED: "Green is yes.")*
Le vert, c'est oui.

**HANA**
*(French; SUBTITLED: "Yellow is: ask someone who isn't here. Nobody decides for the absent.")*
Le jaune, c'est : demandez à quelqu'un qui n'est pas là. Personne ne décide pour les absents.

*Her voice does something small on "absents". The corridor does not comment.*

**CAM**
*(French; SUBTITLED: "White is what we keep and don't show.")*
Le blanc, c'est ce qu'on garde et qu'on ne montre pas.

*The five rolls lie on the pot in front of her in the order the PA threw them. Green, black, red,
white, yellow. The PA reaches to stick a strip of each across the rig's lens hood, in that
sequence —*

**OLDER HANA (O.S., corridor)**
*(French; SUBTITLED: "Wrong order.")*
Mauvais ordre.

*The PA's hand stops an inch above the hood.*

**PA**
*(French; SUBTITLED: "Which is the right one?")*
Lequel est le bon ?

**OLDER HANA (O.S.)**
*(French; SUBTITLED, after a pause: "I don't know. I only know that's not it.")*
Je ne sais pas. Je sais seulement que ce n'est pas celui-là.

*The sound guy, with the patience of a man who has been listening to things at the wrong
frequency for a living, picks up the black roll and sets it at the head of the row.*

**SOUND GUY**
*(French; SUBTITLED: "Black first. If you can't refuse, accepting means nothing.")*
Le noir d'abord. Si on ne peut pas refuser, accepter ne veut rien dire.

**TYLER**
*(French; SUBTITLED: "A yes that can't be a no is weather.")*
Un oui qui ne peut pas être un non, c'est de la météo.

*Green next, then red — "oui, et ça coûte" cannot come before a plain oui — then yellow, because
the absent can only be asked about a thing that has been said. White last. What's left.*

**PA (V.O.)**
*(French; SUBTITLED: "The tape was fixed in the following order: black, green, red, yellow, white.
The Secretary noted that no one had been told this order, and that it had been arrived at by
reasoning.")*
Le ruban a été fixé dans l'ordre suivant : noir, vert, rouge, jaune, blanc. Le Secrétaire a noté que
personne ne leur avait dit cet ordre et qu'ils y étaient arrivés par le raisonnement.

*The strips go across the lens hood: black, green, red, yellow, white. A small flag. It looks like
nothing at all.*

**LOWER THIRD (DOC UI):**
```
CONSENT PROTOCOL: ADOPTED (5)
```

*On the north wall, behind the dead racks, black ink arrives through the paint one stroke at a

**ON SCREEN (the wall):**
```
2. THE CAMERA RUNS UNLESS ASKED.
```

*The old line is gone. The PA does not need to be told that it is the same wall.*

**PA (V.O.)**
*(French; SUBTITLED: "Item three. The guest asked to be recognized.")*
Point trois. L'invité a demandé la parole.

**PRODUCER (O.S., from the thermos on Tyler's knee, very small)**
*(French; SUBTITLED: "May I speak? Is it in order for a guest to speak?")*
Puis-je parler ? Est-il d'usage qu'un invité parle ?

**HANA**
*(French; SUBTITLED: "The chair recognizes the thermos.")*
La présidence reconnaît la thermos.

**PRODUCER (O.S.)**
*(French; SUBTITLED: "Is there a chair for me? Would it be out of order to ask? Is it not so that
someone said I had one?")*
Y a-t-il une chaise pour moi ? Serait-il déplacé de le demander ? N'est-il pas vrai que quelqu'un a

*Nobody laughs. The fourth chair sits where it sat, its seat bare, a thin ghost of steam on it from
nothing.*

**TYLER**
*(French; SUBTITLED: "I move that the Producer be seated.")*
Je propose que le Producteur soit assis.

**DUCK**
*(English)*
Seconded. Whatever it is.

*Hana puts it to the table. Cam raises a hand. The sound guy. The PA.*

**OLDER HANA (O.S., corridor)**
*(French; SUBTITLED: "Aye.")*
Pour.

**PA (V.O.)**
*(French; SUBTITLED: "The horse was observed to sneeze. The sneeze was recorded as an aye. The motion
carried.")*
Le cheval a éternué. L'éternuement a été consigné comme un oui. La motion est adoptée.

*Tyler stands. He carries the thermos across the room with both hands and sets it on the fourth
chair, upright, squared to the seat, the way the Auditor squared the seventh with the side of his
shoe. He steps back. Nothing happens. Then, from the green mouth of the thermos, a long slow breath
of steam, as if someone had sat down.*

**PRODUCER (O.S., very quietly)**
*(French; SUBTITLED: "Is it all right if it's warm?")*
Est-ce que ça ne dérange pas que ce soit chaud ?

**PA**
*(French; SUBTITLED: "It's been warm since the first day.")*
C'est chaud depuis le premier jour.

*Hana looks at the PA.*

**HANA**
*(French; SUBTITLED: "Any other business?")*
Questions diverses ?

**PRODUCER (O.S.)**
*(French; SUBTITLED: "Is there any other business?")*
Y a-t-il d'autres questions diverses ?

**PA**
*(French; SUBTITLED, writing: "The Producer asked whether it was in order to ask whether there was any

Le Producteur a demandé s'il était d'usage de demander s'il y avait d'autres questions diverses. La

**TYLER**
*(French; SUBTITLED: "I move to adjourn.")*
Je propose de lever la séance.

**DUCK**
*(English)*
Seconded.

**PA (V.O.)**
*(French; SUBTITLED: "The meeting was adjourned at ten forty-seven. The Chair thanked the corridor.

La séance a été levée à dix heures quarante-sept. La présidente a remercié le couloir. Le couloir a
dit qu'il n'y avait pas de quoi.

*The PA closes the book. He holds the clipboard flat against his chest.*

**PA (V.O., last line, and the first thing in the whole reading that is not in the passive)**
*(French; SUBTITLED: "It was noted that the Secretary read these minutes in a voice that did not

On a noté que le Secrétaire a lu ce procès-verbal d'une voix qui n'a pas tremblé. Le Secrétaire
voudrait qu'on note qu'il ne sait pas comment.
