**PRODUCTION NOTE, READ BEFORE THE SCRIPT:**

Founder, real-time (2026-10-13): *"go explore the TYLER repo; emulate curiosity and poke around in
there; then write the next episode of VALHANNA"* — plus a standing request for more
spontaneously added or updated lore receipts. The exploration was real: every episode and
manuscript in this repository was read before this script was written. This is what the reading
turned up.

VH02 ended on `ENCOUNTER: THE CREW MEETS` with the sentence unfinished. VH03 answered *when*
(the day with no date). **VH04 answers *who*, as far as the archive will let anyone answer it:**
a person, by job title, with a badge that has no name on it. The founder's sentence was unfinished;
the repository, read closely, already had somebody standing in the doorway.

**Why him.** `characters/the_auditor.md` ends its placement paragraph with *"He has not been placed
in a specific episode yet,"* and records three facts that VH01 and VH02 had already left lying on
the floor of that server room unclaimed: he makes **minestrone** in a pot too large for one
household and gives out **nine of the ten ingredients** as "a security feature"; he is the
guild member who **"remembers whose turn it is to bring folding chairs"**; and he lends equipment
with a **"spare" battery that is not spare, due Thursday.** The thermoses in VH01 and VH02 were
flagged against him and never resolved. This script resolves *part* of that — and leaves the rest
exactly where the rest was.

**Three older threads this episode picks up on purpose:**

- `lore/camera_op_sealed_log.md`, Entry 09: *"I measured the chairs again today. Still 63
  centimeters. I don't know what I'm looking for when I measure them. I think I'm looking for the
  thing that isn't strange. There isn't one."* Cam measures a chair. It is 63.
- **The Thursday people** (S07E06, Café Bernstein: "Eight chairs. Empty.") and Tides Book 2's table
  at which **"there is always enough"** and one chair at the end is set "with the same precision as
  all the others. Laid for one person. Nobody there." The Auditor's guild rule — always bring one
  chair more than the list — is the same rule, run by a person with a soldering iron instead of a
  four-hundred-year ledger.
- **Safiya's seal** (S11E03): the archive "left the seal." The Auditor's guild mirrors suppressed
  research before anyone can retract it. He will not mirror Entry 23. *Suppressed* and *sealed*
  are different verbs, and the difference is the whole of his ethics.

**What this script will not say:** his name (title only, on principle); who made the *tenth*
ingredient; who the seventh chair is for; what Entry 23 says; where Hana is. It does not say the
word the Producer will not say. It does not say the sentence Rule 22 forbids.

**Language:** French with SUBTITLED gloss, the standing MODE_TYLER convention. The Auditor's French
is bad in a way that is a pleasure to listen to — wrong gender on the chairs, correct on
everything that matters — and nobody corrects it.

**Not resolved:** Hana's absence (fourth episode running); whose thermoses (the coffee is
answered; the soup is made *more* open); Entry 23; the Bird Correction (still PENDING).

## CONTINUITY

- **The doorway** is the one from VH02/VH03. The shadow that crossed the floor in VH03 was long
  because it was a person carrying something wider than himself.
- **Hana's absence.** Fourth episode running. The PA looks at the empty chair once. Nobody says
  where she is.
- **The Producer.** He never says the forbidden word. He says "production," "project," "unit,"
  and, once, "please don't." The Auditor addresses the radio directly; this is not in any
  protocol the Producer has, and the line goes quiet. It does not cut. It *goes quiet*, which is
  different, and is how a hospitality clause sounds from the outside.
- **Cam and the Camera Op.** The notebook is still the Camera Op's. Cam measures a chair with her
  thumb and a folding rule; the number is 63. She does not say why she knew it would be.
- **28.7.** The Auditor's rig reads it too, on hardware that was decommissioned before any of the
  three of them were hired. It is the first reading of this frequency by an instrument that
  belongs to nobody in this archive. He calls it "a remainder." He does not say a remainder *of
  what.*

## COLD OPEN

```
INT. BEDROOM — PRESENT DAY — NIGHT
```

*The Duck, tuxedo rumpled, asleep on top of the covers. The wall calendar from VH03 is not on the
wall. Where it hung there is a rectangle of cleaner paint, exactly the size of thirteen pages.*

*At the foot of the bed, a folding chair. Wooden slats, steel hinges, a stencil on the back rail in
faded paint: the circuit-flower, a trace in place of petals. It is open. It is facing the Duck.*

*The Duck wakes. Looks at the chair. Sits on the edge of the bed, looks at it longer.*

**THE DUCK**
*(French; SUBTITLED: "It isn't my turn.")*
Ce n'est pas mon tour.

*It folds the chair, neatly, along its hinges, and leans it against the wall. It lies back down.
It does not close its eyes.*

**SMASH CUT**

```
INT. SERVER ROOM — ICELAND — NO YEAR GIVEN
```

*The room from VH01, VH02, VH03. The door is open. The line of light along the floor. Cam holds
the frame on the doorway with the rig on her shoulder; the sound guy has the boom low; the PA has
the clipboard against his chest. The thermoses, upright by the dead rack. The printer, silent
since its last half-line.*

**LOWER THIRD (DOC UI) — the burned-in timecode:**
```
TC  — / —  ·  09:58:41
```

*A sound in the corridor, from the light's side. Not footsteps. A clank, patient, repeated — metal
on metal, a thing being carried by someone who is used to carrying it and knows exactly how much
noise it makes.*

*A pot comes through the door first. It is a stockpot, aluminum, the size of a barrel; both arms
around it; the lid held on by a bungee cord and, apparently, by faith. It is wider than the
doorway. It comes through the doorway.*

*Nobody remarks on this.*

*Behind it, the man carrying it, sideways, one hip leading. Sixty-plus. White hair that has never
once cooperated with a comb. Wire glasses. A short-sleeve government-issue button shirt, suspenders,
and, on the pocket, a small enamel pin shaped like a flower with a circuit trace for petals. Over
one shoulder, by a nylon strap, seven folding chairs, clacking against each other at every step,
the way a man's keys do.*

*A lanyard. A laminated badge turned a half-inch the wrong way. Cam's lens finds it the way it
found the printout.*

**ON SCREEN (the badge, as it fills the frame):**
```
REGIONAL ENERGY AUTHORITY
GRID COMPLIANCE — INSPECTOR
NAME: ——
```

*The name field is not redacted. It is blank, the way DAY 365 was blank. He sets the pot down on
the floor between the thermoses and the printer, rubs both palms down the front of his shirt, and
beams at three strangers and a camera.*

**THE AUDITOR**
*(French; SUBTITLED: "Good morning! Don't get up.")*
Bonjour ! Ne vous levez pas.

*Nobody was sitting. He knows this. It is a pleasantry.*

---

*He unslings the chairs. He opens the first, and the second, with the practiced double-snap of a
man who has done it in church basements and community halls for thirty-one years, and sets them in
a loose arc, facing the open doorway. He is unhurried. He is very happy. He talks while he works.*

**THE AUDITOR**
*(French; SUBTITLED: "I always bring one more than the list.")*
J'apporte toujours une chaise de plus que la liste.

*The PA, who has not been told he may speak, speaks.*

**PA**
*(French; SUBTITLED: "What does the list say?")*
Et que dit la liste ?

**THE AUDITOR**
*(French; SUBTITLED: "Six. There are seven. Somebody always turns up.")*
Six. Il y en a sept. Il y a toujours quelqu'un qui arrive.

*He sets the sixth. He sets the seventh a little apart from the others — squarely facing the
door, a hand's-width farther back than the arc — and squares it to the floor tiles with the
side of his shoe.*

---

*Cam has lowered the rig to her hip — the record still running — and crouched by the nearest
chair. From a jacket pocket, a folding rule, yellow, scarred. She does not decide to do it. She
opens it along the seat height, thumb at the zero.*

**CAM**
*(French; SUBTITLED, to herself: "Sixty-three.")*
Soixante-trois.

**SOUND GUY**
*(French; SUBTITLED: "How did you know?")*
Comment tu le savais ?

*She doesn't answer. She closes the rule.*

---

*The sound guy has been looking at the meter on his belt. He turns it so the Auditor can see.*

**ON SCREEN (the sound guy's meter, one bar, below the labeled scale):**
```
28.7 Hz
```

*The Auditor leans in, glasses down his nose. From the other shoulder, under his arm, he unhooks
something the size of a lunchbox: gray steel, a dial nobody manufactures, a bundle of wires,
a handwritten label that has been written over so many times it is mostly ink. He holds it up
beside the sound guy's meter. The two needles settle on the same place.*

**THE AUDITOR**
*(French; SUBTITLED: "Twenty-eight point seven. Hello, old friend.")*
Vingt-huit virgule sept. Bonjour, vieille amie.

**SOUND GUY**
*(French; SUBTITLED: "What is it?")*
C'est quoi ?

**THE AUDITOR**
*(French; SUBTITLED: "Not a noise. A remainder. It takes two bodies in the room.")*
Pas un bruit. Un reste. Il faut deux corps dans la pièce.

*Nobody does the subtraction. He does not offer it. He watches the needles hold. Then he lifts a
hand, with real courtesy, as if he were interrupting no one, and points past them all at the
corner of the room.*

**THE AUDITOR (CONT'D)**
*(French; SUBTITLED: "That's mine. I left it this morning.")*
Ça, c'est le mien. Je l'ai laissé ce matin.

*He means the coffee. He walks to the thermoses, unscrews the second cap, and sniffs it like a
man checking on a child. Then he does the same to the first.*

*He stops.*

*He pours a thimbleful of the soup into the cap. Sips. Holds it in his mouth.*

*He is, for the first time since he came through the door, entirely quiet.*

**THE AUDITOR (CONT'D)**
*(French; SUBTITLED, very low: "That's my recipe. Nine of ten.")*
C'est ma recette. Neuf sur dix.

**PA**
*(French; SUBTITLED: "And the tenth?")*
Et le dixième ?

*The Auditor looks at the cap. At the thermos. At the doorway. He is delighted, and he is also
doing something behind the delight that the camera catches and does not hold.*

**THE AUDITOR**
*(French; SUBTITLED: "Someone added it.")*
Quelqu'un l'a ajouté.

---

*The radio clicks. The Producer, light, a half-step ahead of his own tempo.*

**PRODUCER (O.S., radio)**
*(French; SUBTITLED: "Keep rolling. Don't prompt him. Let him talk.")*
Continuez à tourner. Ne le provoquez pas. Laissez-le parler.

*The Auditor turns his head. He finds the radio, on the PA's belt, the way you find a cat in
a room — with his whole body, pleasantly. He crosses to it. He does not touch it. He speaks to
it at the height he would speak to a person.*

**THE AUDITOR**
*(French; SUBTITLED: "You have a chair too.")*
Vous avez une chaise aussi.

*The radio does not click. It does not cut. A held quiet comes up from it, the room tone of VH02,
as if someone had put a hand over the mouthpiece to listen. Nobody moves. The tubes breathe.*

*The Auditor, satisfied, nods once at the radio the way you nod to a neighbor, and turns back to
the pot.*

---

*He lifts the lid. A great plume of steam, white, rolling, the density of a small weather event.
It goes up — and then, instead of rising to the dead tubes, it bends. It leans, all of it, across
the room, as if toward an open window. The thermoses' steam does the same. The two plumes braid
together and go, slow and level, toward the seventh chair.*

*The seventh chair is empty. It is facing the door. Nobody has sat in it.*

*He ladles four bowls from a bag that has also, apparently, always been on his shoulder. He puts
one in front of Cam's chair, and one in front of the sound guy's, and one in front of the PA's —
and a fourth, steaming, in front of the fifth chair, the one nobody is sitting in. The PA looks at
that chair and then at his clipboard and writes nothing down.*

**PA**
*(French; SUBTITLED, barely: "Where is she?")*
Où est-elle ?

*Nobody answers. The Auditor, ladle in hand, looks at the fifth chair, then at the PA, with great
gentleness — the look of a man who has been asked the same question at many potlucks and has
learned that the only decent answer is a bowl.*

---

*Cam is still crouched. She has not sat. She has her notebook open to a clean page; the pen is in
her hand; the Auditor has seen the cover.*

**THE AUDITOR**
*(French; SUBTITLED: "Ah. The sealed one.")*
Ah. Le scellé.

*She does not look up. Her hand does not move on the page.*

**THE AUDITOR (CONT'D)**
*(French; SUBTITLED: "I mirror what they suppress. I don't copy what you've sealed.")*
Je copie ce qu'on supprime. Je ne copie pas ce que vous avez scellé.

**CAM**
*(French; SUBTITLED, almost inaudible: "What's the difference?")*
C'est quoi, la différence ?

**THE AUDITOR**
*(French; SUBTITLED: "To suppress is to steal. To seal is to choose.")*
Supprimer, c'est voler. Sceller, c'est choisir.

*He hands her something from his pocket without ceremony: a battery, a squat black cell with
masking tape around it and the word JEUDI written in ballpoint. She takes it. Her grip tells us
it is warmer than it should be.*

**THE AUDITOR (CONT'D)**
*(French; SUBTITLED: "It's not a spare. It's from my hearing aid. I need it Thursday.")*
Ce n'est pas une pile de rechange. C'est celle de mon appareil auditif. Il me la faut jeudi.

**PA**
*(French; SUBTITLED, reading from the call sheet he has not let go of: "Today is not a day of the week.")*
Aujourd'hui n'est pas un jour de la semaine.

*The Auditor considers this honestly, with his whole face.*

**THE AUDITOR**
*(French; SUBTITLED: "Then it isn't due.")*
Alors ce n'est pas dû.

*He laughs. It fills the room. It is the first sound in four episodes that has not been held.*

---

*The room settles. Cam sits at last, in the chair he gave her, the rule across her knees and the
battery in her hand. The sound guy sits. The PA, after a long look at his clipboard, sits.*

*The steam from the pot is still leaning, patiently, toward the seventh chair.*

**LOWER THIRD (DOC UI), the line from VH02 — the cursor after "MEETS" that blinked for two
episodes — finishes:**
```
ENCOUNTER: THE CREW MEETS AN INSPECTOR (NO NAME ON BADGE)
```

*A beat. Then, beneath it, in the same type:*

```
SEATS: 7  ·  SEATED: 3  ·  BOWLS: 4  ·  FACING THE DOOR: 1
BIRD CORRECTION: PENDING
```

*The Auditor looks at the seventh chair, and at the door beyond it, and at the long clean line of
light on the floor. He does not say who he thinks is coming. He picks up the ladle.*

**THE AUDITOR**
*(French; SUBTITLED: "Eat. It gets cold.")*
Mangez. Ça refroidit.

*SMASH TO BLACK.*

*END COLD OPEN.*
