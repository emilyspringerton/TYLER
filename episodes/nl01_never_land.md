## ACT I — THE ACADEMY IN DAYLIGHT (WHERE THE WORLD PRETENDS TO BE FLAT)

The building is the kind of place you could point to on a map without thinking.

A public institution. Old brick. Flags. A plaque with a donor's name worn smooth by time and weather and bored fingers.

People walk in and out carrying coffees and backpacks and ordinary complaints.

Tyler stands at the foot of the steps like he's deciding whether gravity is still reliable.

Hana is half a step ahead of him, as always—just enough to imply leadership, not enough to admit ownership.

**TYLER**
Is it… this one?

**HANA**
Do you see another door?

Tyler watches a couple pass them, laughing, sunlight catching on their teeth.

**TYLER**
Do they know?

Hana's gaze follows the couple until they disappear through the front doors.

**HANA**
If they knew, it wouldn't work.

Tyler tilts his head, listening to the building the way you listen to a room you're about to perform in—counting exits, counting eyes.

**TYLER**
So it's hiding… because nobody names it?

Hana's face tightens. A flicker of pride. A flicker of fear.

**HANA**
Don't say "hiding."

**TYLER**
What should I say?

Hana exhales through her nose like she's annoyed with the entire concept of vocabulary.

**HANA**
Say "waiting."

Tyler smiles, thin.

**TYLER**
Waiting for what?

Hana looks at him like the answer is obvious and forbidden at the same time.

**HANA**
For you to stop asking.

They go inside.

The lobby is normal in the way a staged room is normal.

A receptionist. Brochures. A bulletin board for events that may or may not have ever happened.

Hana doesn't approach the desk. She doesn't make eye contact. She walks past with the confidence of someone who has already been granted access… or someone who has been denied so many times she no longer believes permission is real.

Tyler follows.

He can feel it before he sees it: the geometry loosening. The walls becoming negotiable.

There's a hallway behind the hallway—an angle your eyes refuse to understand until you stop trying to understand it.

Hana slows.

She doesn't turn back, but her voice finds him anyway.

**HANA**
Do you trust me?

Tyler laughs, soft.

**TYLER**
Is that a test?

**HANA**
It's a door.

Tyler looks down the impossible corridor.

The air there is colder, but not in a physical way—more like the cold of a memory you didn't earn.

**TYLER**
What happens if I don't?

Hana pauses, just long enough for the question to bite.

**HANA**
Then you stay in the version where you keep dying.

Tyler's smile fades.

**TYLER**
Do we… die a lot?

Hana doesn't answer. She steps forward into the hallway that shouldn't exist.

Tyler follows her because his feet already know the path.

## ACT II — THE ANNEX (WHERE EVERYTHING IS A RULE IN A HUMAN COSTUME)

The subterranean campus is not a dungeon. It is not a bunker.

It is… organized.

Clean floors. Soft light that doesn't cast honest shadows. Doors with simple labels like an office building—except the labels mean something else if you let them.

Tyler's eyes flick to one: REGISTRY. Another: SEALS. Another: NAMES.

A student passes them—young, focused, wearing an ID badge and a ring that looks antique in a way that feels deliberate.

The student nods to Hana, like she's known here.

Then the student looks at Tyler for half a second too long, and Tyler feels the gaze stick to him like glue.

**STUDENT**
Is he…?

Hana answers without looking back.

**HANA**
He's walking.

The student's expression shifts—respect, pity, curiosity, jealousy. A whole society compressed into a micro-reaction.

**STUDENT**
So it's him.

Tyler's jaw tenses.

**TYLER**
Do I get a name here?

Hana finally looks at him.

**HANA**
Do you want one?

Tyler opens his mouth—then closes it.

He doesn't know whether wanting a name is the same thing as accepting a cage.

A doorway opens ahead of them without anyone touching it.

Inside: a room arranged like a classroom, but wrong. The desks are in a circle. The front is the center. The walls are covered in diagrams that look like corporate training posters until you get close enough to read them.

A man sits at the far end—older, calm, face unremarkable in a way that seems intentional.

He stands when Hana enters.

Not out of politeness.

Out of recognition.

**MAN**
Handler Hana.

Hana stiffens. Tyler's eyes narrow at the title.

**TYLER**
Handler?

Hana's mouth tightens like she regrets the vocabulary of her own life.

**HANA**
It's… not what it sounds like.

**TYLER**
Is it worse?

Hana doesn't answer. She doesn't have to.

The man turns to Tyler and smiles like a professor welcoming a student and like a banker welcoming a debtor.

**MAN**
Tyler.

Tyler's blood runs cold.

**TYLER**
I didn't tell you—

**MAN**
You don't have to.

Tyler looks at Hana—accusation and confusion tangled.

**TYLER**
Did you?

Hana's eyes flash.

**HANA**
I don't lie about custody.

The man gestures to a chair.

**MAN**
Sit. This will be brief.

Tyler doesn't sit.

**TYLER**
Is "brief" another word for "I won't like it"?

The man's smile doesn't move.

**MAN**
It's another word for "you won't escape it."

Hana inhales sharply as if the word "escape" is sacrilege.

Tyler hears it too: a faint hum in the room. Not electricity. Not machinery.

Something like attention.

The man lifts a clipboard. Yes—an actual clipboard, which feels almost funny until it doesn't.

**MAN**
We categorize anomalies for safety.

Tyler laughs.

**TYLER**
Safety?

**MAN**
For the world.

Tyler's grin is razor-thin.

**TYLER**
And for me?

The man looks at Hana instead of answering.

**MAN**
Classify.

Hana's fingers twitch like she's resisting the urge to reach for Tyler's wrist the way she always does when something is about to be checked.

**HANA**
Green.

Tyler flinches as if the word is a hand closing around his throat.

**TYLER**
Green?

Hana's voice is steady, but her eyes aren't.

**HANA**
Anomaly that spreads when witnessed.

Tyler swallows.

**TYLER**
So the camera crew—

Hana cuts in, fast.

**HANA**
Stop.

Tyler's gaze locks on hers.

**TYLER**
Is that why you hate being seen?

Hana's face goes blank in the way people do when they've learned to survive.

**HANA**
It's not personal.

Tyler smiles like it hurts.

**TYLER**
Everything is personal.

The man taps the clipboard once.

**MAN**
We will test containment compliance.

Tyler laughs again, softer.

**TYLER**
Test. Like school.

Hana's gaze drops.

**HANA**
Like debt.

## ACT III — THE SEALED ROOM (WHERE ESCAPE IS A LANGUAGE EXAM)

They lead him down a corridor that curves slightly too far for the building's footprint.

Tyler knows because Tyler is an escape artist and escape artists always count.

A door opens onto a white room with a single chair and a single light and a single lock that looks ordinary enough to be insulting.

The man speaks calmly, professionally.

**MAN**
Your task is simple: exit the room without touching the lock.

Tyler stares at the lock.

**TYLER**
Is that a joke?

Hana watches him like she's holding her breath.

**HANA**
It's… a baseline.

Tyler steps inside anyway.

The door closes behind him with a soft click.

The light hums.

The silence is thick enough to chew.

Tyler paces the room.

He checks the corners. He checks the seams. He checks the air.

Then he stops and stares at the lock again.

He does not touch it.

He crouches, and for a moment his face becomes the face of the boy he used to be—before he learned the smile, before he learned the calm, before he learned the performance was sometimes the only way to make terror look like choice.

He pulls a coin from his pocket.

Not silver. Not a quarter.

Not even a Spanish doubloon, though it has that weight, that history.

It is blank.

A disc of metal with no name.

Tyler turns it in his fingers.

**TYLER** *(to himself)*
If I don't name it… does it stay mine?

He flips the coin.

It spins and spins and lands on the floor without a sound.

That's the first wrong thing.

The second wrong thing is that the room seems to inhale.

Tyler closes his eyes.

He does something small. Not a grand trick.

A tiny escape.

The kind nobody claps for.

The kind you do to prove to yourself you're still real.

He opens his eyes and—

He is standing outside the room.

In the corridor.

The door is still closed.

His hands are still in his pockets.

Hana is staring at him like she's seen a ghost and like she's seen a prophecy.

The man's smile falters by a millimeter.

**MAN**
…How?

Tyler looks at Hana.

**TYLER**
Did I pass?

Hana's voice is a whisper.

**HANA**
Did you feel it?

Tyler's throat tightens.

**TYLER**
Feel what?

Hana takes a slow breath.

**HANA**
The debt.

Tyler's eyes flick past her—down the corridor—where a wall panel is slightly ajar, revealing a painted surface beneath.

Black feathers. A crow's wing.

Tyler's stomach drops.

**TYLER**
Why is that here?

Hana's eyes follow his.

Her face drains.

**HANA**
It shouldn't be.

The man's calm voice sharpens.

**MAN**
Seal the corridor.

Hana looks at him, anger flaring.

**HANA**
You can't just—

**MAN**
We can. We do.

Tyler steps between them like he's mediating a fight and like he's walking into one.

**TYLER**
Is this what the Academy teaches?

The man looks at Tyler like he's being evaluated by a system, not a person.

**MAN**
We teach you to survive the timeline.

Tyler's smile is small and bitter.

**TYLER**
So nobody learns to live.

## ACT IV — HANA AND TYLER (ROMANCE AS A FORBIDDEN PROBLEM SET)

They're sent away. "Rest." "Observe." "Do not provoke."

Hana walks fast. Tyler keeps up without trying.

They end up in a quiet stairwell with no cameras and no students and a window that shows nothing but concrete.

Hana leans against the wall as if she's been holding herself upright for days.

Tyler watches her like he's memorizing a version of her he might not get to keep.

**TYLER**
Do you hate me?

Hana's laugh is brittle.

**HANA**
Do you want the truth?

**TYLER**
Is there any other option?

Hana closes her eyes.

**HANA**
I hate what you do to the world.

Tyler nods slowly.

**TYLER**
And what I do to you?

Hana opens her eyes and they are bright in a way that makes Tyler flinch.

**HANA**
I hate that I… want to keep you.

Tyler's breath catches.

**TYLER**
Is that custody?

Hana's jaw tightens.

**HANA**
Don't.

**TYLER**
Is it?

Hana steps closer.

Her voice is quieter.

**HANA**
I was sent to decide.

Tyler's heart thuds.

**TYLER**
Decide what?

Hana's gaze doesn't move.

**HANA**
Whether you're a person… or a resource.

Tyler smiles like it's a punchline only he hears.

**TYLER**
And what are you?

Hana's eyes flicker.

**HANA**
A vow.

Tyler takes a step closer too.

They are close enough to kiss.

Close enough to betray every rule that keeps the world from tearing open.

**TYLER**
Do you ever want to run?

Hana's lips part.

**HANA**
Do you think we can?

Tyler touches her wrist—light, careful—like he's testing whether touching her changes the timeline.

Hana does not pull away.

**TYLER**
If we did… would the debt find us?

Hana's voice is barely a breath.

**HANA**
It always finds what you love.

Tyler swallows.

**TYLER**
So the trick is to love nothing?

Hana's eyes harden.

**HANA**
That's not a trick. That's a sentence.

Tyler's thumb brushes her pulse.

**TYLER**
Then what's the escape?

Hana looks at him like she's about to break and like she's about to weaponize the break.

**HANA**
You don't escape.

Tyler's smile is sad.

**TYLER**
Then what do I do?

Hana answers like a prayer.

**HANA**
You pay.

## ACT V — THE DOC CREW (WHO AREN'T HERE BUT ARE HERE)

They aren't in this season the same way they were before.

No overt confessionals. No clean lens of "we're filming."

But the world still behaves like it's being recorded.

Tyler notices it first.

A student in the corridor holding a phone at waist height, screen dark, camera app open anyway.

An "incident response" poster with a QR code that leads to nothing—except when Tyler looks, and then it leads to a page that says:

**YOU ARE WATCHING YOURSELF.**

Tyler turns away. The page updates behind his eyes.

Hana sees him wince.

**HANA**
What did you see?

Tyler shakes his head.

**TYLER**
Do you want me to say it out loud?

Hana's silence is the answer again.

They pass a door labeled ARCHIVIST.

Tyler stops.

His gaze locks.

The handle is warm.

Hana grabs his wrist instantly, like muscle memory.

**HANA**
Don't check.

Tyler's voice is almost gentle.

**TYLER**
Why does everyone say that like it's love?

Hana's jaw clenches.

**HANA**
Because it's the only love we're allowed.

Tyler looks at the door like it's a mirror.

**TYLER**
Who's inside?

Hana swallows.

**HANA**
Someone who remembers what we forget.

Tyler's chest tightens.

**TYLER**
Do you forget on purpose?

Hana's voice is quiet.

**HANA**
Do you think I chose to survive?

Tyler looks at her then, really looks.

**TYLER**
Do you remember me before tonight?

Hana doesn't answer for a long time.

When she does, it's a question.

**HANA**
Do you remember me?

Tyler's breath shakes.

**TYLER**
I… dream you.

Hana closes her eyes like she's fighting tears and laughter at the same time.

**HANA**
Then it's already happening.

Tyler's mouth goes dry.

**TYLER**
What's happening?

Hana opens her eyes.

**HANA**
Never_Land.

Tyler frowns.

**TYLER**
Is that a place?

Hana's expression turns unreadable.

**HANA**
It's a refusal.

## ACT VI — THE DEBT COLLECTS (SOFTLY, LIKE A KISS YOU DON'T CONSENT TO)

That night, Tyler is given a dorm room.

Spartan. Clean. A bed. A desk. A window that faces a wall.

Tyler lies down anyway because exhaustion is a kind of surrender.

He closes his eyes.

He dreams the pilot house.

He dreams the basement water tank.

He dreams the crow painting watching.

He dreams the locket in the gutter.

He dreams Hana saying *I was sent to decide* in a voice that sounds like she's already decided.

In the dream, Tyler wakes up beside Hana.

In the dream, Osaka ignites in the correct order.

In the dream, the engines sound like a hymn.

Tyler jolts awake.

He is alone.

The dorm room is too still.

He sits up and looks at the desk.

On the desk is a piece of paper.

No letterhead. No signature.

Just a line, charcoal-dark:

**PAY WHAT YOU SAW.**

Tyler's throat tightens.

He looks at the door.

The doorknob turns—slowly—without anyone on the other side.

Tyler does not move.

The door opens a crack.

Hana's voice comes through, quiet as a confession.

**HANA**
Are you awake?

Tyler exhales—relief and dread tangled.

**TYLER**
Was I asleep?

Hana steps inside.

She looks tired in a way that feels ancient.

**HANA**
Did you dream?

Tyler nods, small.

**TYLER**
Do you?

Hana's gaze drops to the charcoal message on the desk.

Her face goes pale.

**HANA**
…It's collecting.

Tyler's voice breaks.

**TYLER**
Collecting what?

Hana looks at him like she's sorry and like she's angry at the universe for making her sorry.

**HANA**
Proof.

Tyler swallows.

**TYLER**
Proof of what?

Hana's answer is a question, and it hurts more than an answer would.

**HANA**
Proof that we were here.

Tyler stares at her.

**TYLER**
And if we can't prove it?

Hana steps closer.

Her voice is almost tender.

**HANA**
Then it doesn't let you leave.

Tyler's smile returns, weak.

**TYLER**
So this is Never_Land.

Hana's eyes flicker.

**HANA**
Do you understand now?

Tyler looks past her—through the crack in the door—down the corridor.

For a second, just a second, he thinks he sees black feathers moving like smoke.

He blinks and it's gone.

Tyler whispers.

**TYLER**
Is it cursed?

Hana's mouth tightens.

**HANA**
Aren't they all?

They stand there, close enough to touch, not touching.

The episode ends the way a dream ends: not with a conclusion, but with a feeling you can't shake.

Tyler looks at Hana like she's the only real thing in an unreal world.

**TYLER**
What do we do?

Hana answers in a whisper that sounds like a vow and like a threat.

**HANA**
We don't name it.

A beat.

**TYLER**
And if we have to?

Hana's eyes harden.

**HANA**
Then we run.

*Cut to black.*
