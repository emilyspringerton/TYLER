### Build 0155 | Einhorn Media | VALHANNA, continued

**PRODUCTION NOTE, READ BEFORE THE SCRIPT:**

The Producer went off air in VH07. His voice was never transmitted (VH07): it was carried on the
room's 7.83 Hz tone, which VH10 attributed to the horse. VH17 answers the question that fell out of
that: where is the *radio's* signal actually coming from? The sound guy, after the unlogged hour of
VH16, has the patience to follow the cable.

**Writer's Room Rule 2 holds:** the Producer is a Jiangshi-adjacent voice. He says "the project,"
"the unit," "production." He does not say the word. In this episode he says very little at all.

**The reveal is small and weird:** the field radio's antenna lead runs out through the doorway.
At its end, on the sill, is the third thermos (tea). The Producer's voice is *in the tea.*

**Not resolved:** what that means; whose tea; the Bird Correction (PENDING).

## COLD OPEN

```
INT. SERVER ROOM — ICELAND — NO YEAR GIVEN
```

*Late. The crew is asleep or half-asleep around the camera, locked off on its tripod. Hana sits
upright against the wall, awake, eyes open, watching the door. The horse is a long dark shape on the
floor beside Cam.*

**LOWER THIRD (DOC UI):**
```
TC  — / —  ·  23:02:11
```

*The sound guy is awake, too. He is on his hands and knees by the field radio on its case. The
antenna lead — a thin black cable, one of those spooled-up coax lines — runs from the back of the
radio across the floor, up to the tape, and under it. He has been following it with the flat of
his palm for ten minutes. He reaches the end of the visible part and stops.*

**SOUND GUY**
*(French; SUBTITLED, to Hana, very low: "It doesn't go to an antenna.")*
Ça ne va pas à une antenne.

**HANA**
*(French; SUBTITLED: "Where does it go?")*
Où ça va ?

**SOUND GUY**
*(French; SUBTITLED: "I'd like to tell you before I look.")*
J'aimerais te le dire avant de regarder.

*He follows the cable on his knees, slowly, up to the tape, across the tape, to the open door. The
cable reaches the threshold. It does not turn. It goes through the line of light and lies along the
sill, a thin dark thread.*

*It ends in the green enamel thermos.*

---

*Not plugged in. Not taped. The coax terminates inside the cap, where the cap has been unscrewed
a quarter-turn and the cable has been fed through the gap, a single neat loop. The thermos is
upright, steaming faintly. It is the only thing on the sill.*

**LOWER THIRD (DOC UI):**
```
THERMOS (3): SIGNAL SOURCE
```

*The sound guy sits back on his heels. He does not touch the thermos. Hana has come up behind him
without a sound and put her hand on his shoulder.*

**SOUND GUY**
*(French; SUBTITLED: "He's in the tea.")*
Il est dans le thé.

**HANA**
*(French; SUBTITLED: "That doesn't sound like a thing.")*
Ça ne ressemble pas à quelque chose.

**SOUND GUY**
*(French; SUBTITLED: "It's not a thing. It's where the carrier's coming from.")*
Ce n'est pas quelque chose. C'est d'où vient la porteuse.

*He hands the radio's handset back along the cable, to Hana, as if passing a baton.*

---

*Hana looks at the handset. She looks at the thermos. She presses the key.*

**HANA**
*(French; SUBTITLED: "Producer. We found you.")*
Producteur. On t'a trouvé.

*For eleven seconds: the carrier. Then, from the thermos — not from the radio, from the thermos,
so close to the open door that the light seems to bend around the voice — the Producer. Light.
Half a step off tempo. Very small, as if heard down a mile of tube.*

**PRODUCER (O.S., from the thermos)**
*(French; SUBTITLED: "That's — kind of you. I don't need anything.")*
C'est — gentil. Je n'ai besoin de rien.

**HANA**
*(French; SUBTITLED: "The inspector said you had a chair too.")*
L'inspecteur a dit que vous aviez une chaise aussi.

**PRODUCER (O.S., from the thermos)**
*(French; SUBTITLED: "I do. The fourth. I'm not in it.")*
Oui. La quatrième. Je n'y suis pas.

*Hana's eyes go, without her permission, to the fourth folding chair in the arc. Empty. A thin line of
steam stands on its seat, as if something had just been set down on it and taken back.*

**HANA**
*(French; SUBTITLED: "Whose tea is it?")*
À qui est ce thé ?

*A pause. In the thermos, the steam rises, waits, rises.*

**PRODUCER (O.S.)**
*(French; SUBTITLED: "It's not mine. I'm only the part of it that talks.")*
Il n'est pas à moi. Je ne suis que la partie qui parle.

*The sound guy has taken his hand away from his mouth.*

**PRODUCER (O.S., CONT'D)**
*(French; SUBTITLED: "It was made for somebody. It's been kept warm for somebody. I carry the
project's voice so that the tea doesn't have to say anything.")*
On l'a fait pour quelqu'un. On l'a gardé chaud pour quelqu'un. Je porte la voix du projet pour
que le thé n'ait rien à dire.

*Hana says nothing. Her hand on the handset has gone quite still.*

**HANA**
*(French; SUBTITLED, finally: "Who is it for?")*
C'est pour qui ?

*The carrier. The steam. The Producer's voice, from very far, from very near, half a step too
slow:*

**PRODUCER (O.S.)**
*(French; SUBTITLED: "Keep rolling.")*
Continuez à tourner.

*And then the carrier closes, like a lid.*

**LOWER THIRD (DOC UI), final card:**
```
THE PRODUCER IS A VOICE ON A WIRE
THE WIRE IS IN THE TEA
THE TEA IS FOR SOMEONE
```

*SMASH TO BLACK.*

*END COLD OPEN.*
