## COLD OPEN

```
INT. SERVER ROOM — ICELAND — NO YEAR GIVEN
```

*Camera 2, the PA's handheld, still on the doorway. It has been on the doorway for eleven minutes.
The PA's arm is shaking. He has not lowered it. At the edge of frame, Hana's hand is on his
shoulder. She has not said a word to him in a quarter of an hour.*

**LOWER THIRD (DOC UI):**
```
TC  — / —  ·  00:11:40
CAMERA 2: DOORWAY — CAM STATIONARY — 11:40 ELAPSED
```

*On the sill, in the light, Cam, still, a statue of herself. Then she breathes in as though she has
been underwater, and takes a step back across the line.*

*The horse, beside her, snorts and tosses her head. They come across the tape together. Cam stops
two steps into the room and looks at the PA, at Hana, at the sound guy, at each of them as one
checks a headcount.*

**CAM**
*(French; SUBTITLED: "How long?")*
Combien de temps ?

**PA**
*(French; SUBTITLED, his voice cracking: "Eleven minutes.")*
Onze minutes.

*Cam looks at the camera on her shoulder. She lowers it. She presses the eject on the card slot,
and the card pops up into her palm, a small black rectangle.*

---

*The sound guy has a laptop. He takes the card from her and slides it in. The file opens. The
player shows a progress bar, long, a long line of white.*

**LOWER THIRD (DOC UI):**
```
CARD: CAM1_0001.MOV
DURATION: 01:12:00
```

**SOUND GUY**
*(French; SUBTITLED, soft: "That's an hour twelve.")*
Ça fait une heure douze.

**HANA**
*(French; SUBTITLED: "Seventy-two minutes.")*
Soixante-douze minutes.

*Nobody says the number again. The sound guy scrubs the file. White. White. White. In the
middle, at about 36:00, a sound. He turns up the volume. It is a woman's voice, very close to the
mic, saying a line in English, softly. We do not hear the line; the audio drops to room tone, 7.83
beneath it.*

**LOWER THIRD (DOC UI):**
```
AUDIO: 1 LINE WITHHELD (BY WHOM: CAM)
```

**CAM**
*(French; SUBTITLED, flat: "I withheld it.")*
C'est moi qui l'ai retenue.

**SOUND GUY**
*(French; SUBTITLED: "You haven't heard it.")*
Tu ne l'as pas entendue.

**CAM**
*(French; SUBTITLED: "I will.")*
Je l'entendrai.

*Hana sits down on the floor next to her, not touching, but near. The seventy-two minutes of white
pass on the laptop's screen at normal speed, in the quiet of the room.*

---

*Cam takes out the notebook, the small cloth one with the soft corner. Opens it to the last
written page. Entry 23 (VH) is there, one word crossed out and the line rewritten shorter, and below it Entry 24 (VH):
the chair, sixty-three, which she released herself.*

*Below it, in the same ink, in a hand that is hers and is not hers — the letters a hair rounder, a
slant slightly off — there is another entry. She has not written it. The notebook has been closed
since the unlogged hour.*

**ON SCREEN (the page, held to the lens, the writing out of focus):**
```
SEALED LOG // ENTRY 25 (VH) // [AUTHOR: NOT CAM]
```

*She reads it. She does not read it aloud. Her lips don't move. She reads it a second time, and
then she turns the page to face the PA, and he reads it, and then he turns the page to Hana.*

*Hana reads it. She shuts her eyes. She does not speak. The page goes back to Cam.*

**PA**
*(French; SUBTITLED: "Is it true?")*
C'est vrai ?

**CAM**
*(French; SUBTITLED: "I don't know yet. I wasn't there for it.")*
Je ne sais pas encore. Je n'étais pas là pour ça.

**PA**
*(French; SUBTITLED: "You were gone seventy-two minutes.")*
Tu étais partie soixante-douze minutes.

**CAM**
*(French; SUBTITLED: "I was gone eleven.")*
J'ai été partie onze.

*Beat.*

**CAM (CONT'D)**
*(French; SUBTITLED, quietly: "Both.")*
Les deux.

**LOWER THIRD (DOC UI), final card:**
```
11 MINUTES ELAPSED
72 MINUTES RECORDED
ENTRY 25 (VH): WRITTEN. NOT BY HER. NOT RELEASED.
```

*SMASH TO BLACK.*

*END COLD OPEN.*
