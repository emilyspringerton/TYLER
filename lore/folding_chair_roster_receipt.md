### Build 0142 | Einhorn Media | TYLER × TIDES OF PARADOX | VALHANNA

## I. WHAT IT IS

One laminated index card, 10 × 15 cm, found taped to the underside of the seventh folding chair
after VH04. Laminated on both sides (the lamination is home-done and slightly wavy). The front is
a roster in ballpoint. The back is a recipe card. A strip of masking tape on the edge carries one
word, `JEUDI`, in the same hand.

It was **not** founder-supplied. The Cataloguer found it. It is recorded as found, and the usual
rule applies: not endorsed, not tampered with, and nothing has been removed from the tape.

## II. THE FRONT — THE ROSTER

Header, block capitals: `REGIONAL CHAPTER — CHAIRS`. Below it, in a smaller hand: *"List + 1.
Always. Somebody turns up."*

| Gathering | On the list | Chairs brought | Seated | The extra chair |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Substation 14 basement, after the retraction | 9 | 10 | 10 | used |
| Library annex, public workshop on reading your own meter | 14 | 15 | 17 | used (two on the floor) |
| A kitchen | 6 | 7 | 7 | used |
| Community hall, the soldering evening | 22 | 23 | 22 | not used; sat in by the cat |
| Church basement, forty new mirrors | 11 | 12 | 12 | used |
| *(no date)* | 6 | 7 | 3 | — |

The last row has no place and no date in the first two columns. The "seated" cell reads `3` in
ballpoint and has been gone over once more in pencil, lightly, as if somebody wanted to be sure of
it. In the margin, in pencil, in the same hand: *"The seventh was set facing the door. Not my
habit. Noted."*

## III. THE BACK — THE RECIPE

Header: `MINESTRONE (POT: THE LARGE ONE)`. Ten numbered lines. Nine are filled in.

```
1. Onion
2. Celery
3. Carrot
4. White beans (soak; do not shortcut)
5. Tinned tomato
6. Cabbage, the dark kind
7. Small pasta, added late
8. Olive oil, more than you think
9. Rind of the hard cheese
10.
```

Line 10 is **blank**. It has not been crossed out, erased, or covered. The numeral is there and
the line after it is not. The lamination over it is flat and clean; whatever the tenth ingredient is,
it was never written on this card.

In the bottom corner, small: *"Nine of ten. A security feature."*

## V. THE CATALOGUER'S FOOTNOTES

[^1]: The arithmetic of the final row is not wrong, and the archive is a little uncomfortable
that it is not. *List six, chairs seven, seated three.* Three of the seven were occupied; one of
the empty ones carries a bowl; one has a radio's worth of silence resting on it; one faces the door.
That is the whole count. It is not more mysterious than that and it is not less.

[^2]: The nine-of-ten rule is on the card in the Auditor's own hand. The Cataloguer notes, without
comment, that the **thermos soup in the server room is a ten-line recipe with all ten lines
filled.** The tenth was not on the card because the person who wrote the card never had it. The
archive has no account of who did.

[^3]: *Seated: 3.* The Cataloguer has checked the box twice. The Auditor did not sit. A man who
brings the chairs does not, by long habit, count himself among the ones that need them.
