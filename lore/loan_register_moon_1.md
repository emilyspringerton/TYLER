### Build 0183 | Einhorn Media | TYLER × TIDES OF PARADOX | VALHANNA — MOON 1

---

## I. WHAT IT IS

One sheet of paper, headed in a production assistant's hand, read aloud on the evening of Moon 1,
Day 20 (VH44) and added to by hand on the morning of Day 22. It is the unit's answer to a woman's
sentence: *"We let him leave with his hands empty. He had nothing to owe us."* Wall rule 11 — `LEND HIM
SOMETHING` — is its authority. The tape color on every line is **red:** *yes, and it costs.*

Netted by the Heikegani collector on Day 14: **0.00 SM,** excluding the pending item. After the
register, the Parliament's working notes (in pencil) read: *eight reservations.*

## IV. THE ONE-LINE THEORY, AS STATED

> People think the debt is the leash. It's the porch light.
— Seat 7, Day 20 (VH44)

> He borrows in order to come back.
— E. Springerton, Day 14 (VH38)

## V. THE CATALOGUER'S FOOTNOTE

[^1]: Fourteen days earlier a Heikegani minor asked whether he *never* returns anything. The answer
was a sentence. The sentence was *the whole register,* in advance, in six words. The Cataloguer has
read a great many documents in which people did not know what they were writing and had not, until
this morning, read one in which they did.
