# Ally Cat Security — New Client Prospectus

*Found in: a Shell Parliament procurement folder, forwarded three times, each forward stripping
one more header until nobody could say who received it first. Real, in-universe artifact —
kanban `ALLYCAT-000`: "maybe it starts as an in universe firm?" This is that "maybe," written
down as the actual document a faction would have been handed.*

---

**ALLY CAT SECURITY**
*Threat Hunting & Perimeter Integrity*

At Ally Cat Security, we redefine what it means to protect your holdings.

The "Ally" in our name is the promise: a dedicated, fiercely loyal partner, retained under your
own terms, answerable to no other client while your contract is live. The "Cat" is the method.
We do not wait at the fence line for an alarm. We go looking. Our hunting teams work the same
patience, stillness, and total night vision a real predator already has — tracking a threat
through however many layers it's hiding in, and closing on it before it ever reaches your own
perimeter, whatever kind of perimeter that happens to be this decade.

With Ally Cat Security, you retain a partner who answers only to you, and a hunter who is
already moving before you've finished asking for help.

*(Standard engagement terms overleaf. First incursion assessment complimentary. References
available on request — several are still under NDA. Several are dead. We remain equally
discreet about both categories.)*

## Real, honest working notes (not part of the pitch itself)

The pitch above is deliberately real-world-cybersecurity-shaped, dropped unmodified into a
setting that has never had cybersecurity as such — Ally Cat's whole hook is that the client has
to translate it themselves. A Shell Parliament actuary reads "perimeter" as a ledger boundary. A
Jiangshi Syndicate handler reads it as a containment ward. Nobody at Ally Cat corrects either of
them, because both are functionally correct, and that ambiguity is the actual product: a
threat-hunting firm that never specifies what kind of threat, because it has genuinely hunted
all of them at some point and would rather you not ask which contract that was.

**Real, grounded connection points into the existing compendium** (`multiverse_heroes.md`), not
invented in isolation:

- **Shell Parliament** (Heikegani Crustocrat) is the real, established bureaucratic-ledger
  faction this document's own framing device (a procurement folder, forwarded and stripped) fits
  most naturally — a security retainer is exactly the kind of line item that faction's own real
  ledgers already track.
- **Jiangshi Syndicate** is the real, established containment-and-binding faction Ally Cat's own
  "perimeter integrity" language reads as a private-sector mirror of — the same real job, done
  for a fee instead of under whatever doctrine actually binds the Syndicate's own agents.
- Deliberately **not** committed to a single owner faction, or to being human, feline, or
  anything else specific — the prospectus never says, and that's a real, deliberate choice: Ally
  Cat's own identity stays open until a real story needs it closed.

**Real, honest, not resolved here**: whether Ally Cat is a recurring background presence (a name
that shows up on other factions' own paperwork, the way `art1.jpeg`'s "Office Star" flavor text
did in `DUNGEON_NORTHSTAR.md`) or an actual on-screen faction with its own real roster is a real,
founder-level creative decision — this document is the "maybe it starts as" itself, not a
commitment past that. No episode, character kit, or compendium entry created yet.
