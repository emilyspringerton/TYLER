**ALLY CAT SECURITY**
*TRAPX-Licensed Threat Hunting & Perimeter Integrity*

At Ally Cat Security, we redefine what it means to protect your holdings.

The "Ally" in our name is the promise: a dedicated, fiercely loyal partner, retained under your
own terms. The "Cat" is the method, and we mean the actual animal, not the metaphor people
reach for first. We don't posture as apex predators — we work the way a real cat actually

Watchers can't, and closing the last few feet without ever announcing that we moved. A cat
doesn't chase a threat down a straight line. It waits for the threat to walk into range, and by
the time you notice, the outcome is already decided.

TRAPX-licensed. Every engagement runs under the same doctrine your own Field Offices already
answer to — Attention, Contest Windows, receipts filed the same way. We don't operate outside
your city's own rules. We're just better at the parts of them that involve waiting in the dark.

With Ally Cat Security, you retain a partner who answers only to you, and a hunter who was
already in position before you finished asking for help.
