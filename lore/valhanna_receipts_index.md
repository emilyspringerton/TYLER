## II. NUMBERING

VALHANNA receipts carry a **`VH-`** prefix in every log that already has a number series
(`TYLER-VH-004`, `ACTIVATION VH-001`, `MEMO #081-R1`, sealed-log `ENTRY 23 (VH)`). This is
deliberate: the episodes that follow S11 (Build 0123) have not been backfilled into the numbered
series, and a VALHANNA entry that took the next free number would collide with whatever the
backfill eventually claims. The prefix is retired when the backfill lands and the receipts are
renumbered in place.

## IV. THE STANDING RULES THE RECEIPTS FOLLOW

1. Recorded as found, not as endorsed.
2. A receipt may be *added to* but not *corrected*. A correction is a second receipt that cites the
   first.
3. The Auditor's name is not given in any receipt, because he has not given it. A blank is not a
   redaction and the archive does not treat it as one.
4. Sealed is not suppressed. The archive does not open a seal that was put there by its author.
