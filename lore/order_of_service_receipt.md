## I. WHAT IT IS

A record of five thermoses, the order in which they stand, and the order in which they were served.
Not founder-supplied. Assembled from the PA's clipboard (VH42), a line scraped in dust on a concrete
floor by a delivery deacon's shoe (VH42), the Doc UI's service log for the morning of Moon 1, Day 22
(VH45), and a text message that reads, in full, `SUIT: right order`. Recorded as found. The Archive
does not endorse the order. It has watched it be wrong.

## III. THE WRONG ORDER (VH01 → VH42)

For forty-one episodes the thermoses stood in the order in which they had *appeared*: soup, coffee, tea,
flask, milk (red, black, green, yellow, white). The Brother's correction: *red first, black second;
that's the whole error.* The same error, carried in memory by the older Hana, was "the coffee last and
the soup first" (VH40). The Suit's text on the older Hana's flip phone: `wrong order again` (VH42).
The Brothers' name for it is a *precedence violation* and its jurisdiction is Aunt Sally's
(`HQ-CANON-TYLER-105`, §3).

## IV. THE RIGHT ORDER

```
(negro (verde (rojo (amarillo (blanco)))))
```

Parentheses first. The logic is the logic of consent: a refusal has to come before a yes or the yes
means nothing (VH26: "a yes that can't be a no is weather"); a yes comes before a cost, because a cost
is a condition on a yes; the cost comes before the thing everyone forgets, because the forgotten thing
is what the cost was for; what is left comes last. **It was arrived at by the unit by reasoning on Day
2, and corrected only in the placement of the vessels on Day 18.** The Brother: *"You have been doing it
in order since Monday."*
